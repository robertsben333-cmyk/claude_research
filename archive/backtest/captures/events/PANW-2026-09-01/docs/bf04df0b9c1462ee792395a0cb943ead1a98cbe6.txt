Guidance for Q4 and full-year 2026 calls for continued double-digit revenue growth, strong Next-Generation Security ARR expansion, non-GAAP operating margin of about 29%, and adjusted free cash flow margin of 37.5%.

         How did Palo Alto Networks (PANW) perform in fiscal Q3 2026? 
         
          Palo Alto Networks reported fiscal Q3 2026 revenue of $3.0 billion, up 31% year over year. According to Palo Alto Networks, Next-Generation Security ARR reached $8.1 billion, growing 60%, and remaining performance obligation rose 36% to $18.4 billion.
         
     
  
     
         What were Palo Alto Networks’ Q3 2026 earnings per share (PANW)? 
         
          In Q3 2026, Palo Alto Networks reported GAAP net loss of $177 million, or ($0.22) per diluted share. According to Palo Alto Networks, non-GAAP net income was $684 million, or $0.85 per diluted share, compared with $0.80 in fiscal Q3 2025.
         
     
  
     
         What guidance did Palo Alto Networks (PANW) give for fiscal Q4 2026 revenue and EPS? 
         
          For fiscal Q4 2026, Palo Alto Networks expects revenue of $3.345–$3.355 billion, representing 32% year-over-year growth. According to Palo Alto Networks, diluted non-GAAP EPS is projected between $0.96 and $0.98, based on 830–840 million shares outstanding.
         
     
  
     
         What is Palo Alto Networks’ full-year 2026 outlook for revenue and profitability (PANW)? 
         
          For fiscal 2026, Palo Alto Networks expects revenue of $11.415–$11.425 billion, up 24% year over year. According to Palo Alto Networks, non-GAAP operating margin is guided to 28.9%–29.2%, with adjusted free cash flow margin targeted at 37.5%.
         
     
  
     
         How fast is Palo Alto Networks’ Next-Generation Security ARR growing in 2026? 
         
          Next-Generation Security ARR reached $8.1 billion in Q3 2026, up 60% year over year. According to Palo Alto Networks, Q4 2026 and full-year 2026 NGS ARR are expected at $8.90–$8.95 billion, implying 59%–60% year-over-year growth.

         How strong was Palo Alto Networks’ cash flow in fiscal Q3 2026? 
         
          Net cash from operating activities in Q3 2026 was $871 million, up from $628 million a year earlier.
