          Palo Alto Networks (PANW) Expands Stock Repurchase Program by $1 Billion
           
          Strategic Move to Enhance Shareholder Value with Additional Stock Buyback Authorization
                          GuruFocus News         03/11/2026 19:00            + GuruFocus.com   on                                                
 Palo Alto Networks, Inc.  PANW  has announced a significant expansion of its stock repurchase program, with the Board of Directors approving an additional $1 billion authorization. This decision, made on March 10, 2026, increases the company's existing repurchase authorization, which originally stood at $4.1 billion. The previous authorization, initiated in February 2019 and extended multiple times, had been fully utilized as of March 6, 2026.
  
 In a strategic move to enhance shareholder value, Palo Alto Networks repurchased $1 billion worth of its common stock between February 20 and February 24, 2026. This transaction involved approximately 6.8 million shares at an average price of $147.69 per share. The new authorization allows the company to continue repurchasing shares opportunistically, utilizing available working capital.
  
 The repurchase program is flexible, permitting purchases through various methods including open market transactions, privately negotiated deals, block purchases, and 10b5-1 trading plans. The authorization is set to expire on December 31, 2026, but the company retains the right to suspend or discontinue the program at any time without prior notice.
  
 As of March 6, 2026, Palo Alto Networks had approximately 811 million shares of common stock outstanding. This strategic initiative underscores the company's commitment to returning value to its shareholders while maintaining financial flexibility.
