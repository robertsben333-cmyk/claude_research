Campbell’s reported earnings this morning alongside a comprehensive operational and balance-sheet reset. Between trimming the dividend to prioritize debt reduction, taking targeted brand impairments, and guiding FY27 conservatively below prior-year run rates, management is laying all the cards on the table to reposition the business for sustainable footing. 
 
The Q4 Numbers 
• Net Sales: $2.14B (-8% YoY, Organic -1%) 
• Adj. EBIT: $242M (-25% YoY) vs $321M prior year 
• Adj. Gross Margin: 28.6% (contracted 190 bps on persistent cost inflation and tariffs) 
• Adj. EPS: $0.39 (-37% YoY) vs $0.62 prior year 
• GAAP EBIT collapsed to $4M after absorbing a $117M combined trademark impairment on Cape Cod and Kettle Brand, plus $52M in restructuring charges. 
 
Segment Pressure 
• Snacks was the primary anchor: Sales dropped 12% (Organic -6% on volume/mix weakness in salty snacks), driving operating earnings down 34% to $101M. 
• Meals &amp; Beverages held up relatively better on top line (Organic +3% on volume/mix), but operating earnings still slipped 12% to $181M due to input inflation. 
 
Capital Allocation &amp; Structural Overhaul 
• Dividend CUT 36%: Quarterly payout lowered from $0.39 to $0.25 ($1.00/yr annualized) to accelerate balance sheet debt reduction. 
• New Cost-Out Target: Launching an enterprise initiative targeting $500M in savings by FY30 via plant closures, spend optimization, and completed workforce cuts. 
 
FY27 Guidance Reset 
• Organic Net Sales: (4)% to (2)% 
• Adj. EBIT: (12)% to (7)% 
• Adj. EPS: $1.65 to $1.80 (down 17% to 24% YoY vs FY26 $2.17)
