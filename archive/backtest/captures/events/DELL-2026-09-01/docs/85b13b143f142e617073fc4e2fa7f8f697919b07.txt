     Lenovo,&nbsp;Dell,&nbsp;Apple&nbsp;and Asus&nbsp;saw&nbsp;YoY growth&nbsp;in shipments,&nbsp;and an increase&nbsp;in&nbsp;market share.&nbsp;      2026 will serve as a critical test of market resilience, where survival depends on an OEM’s ability to secure a reliable supply chain while successfully pivoting from low-margin models to more sustainable mid-to-high-end portfolios.   &nbsp;      Seoul,&nbsp;Beijing, Berlin, Buenos Aires, Fort Collins, Hong Kong, London, New Delhi, Taipei, Tokyo –&nbsp;April&nbsp;17, 2026&nbsp;       Global PC Shipments by OEM, Q1 2026 (In Million Units)        Source: Counterpoint Research Preliminary PC Shipment Data      ​​Global PC shipments&nbsp;grew&nbsp;3.2%&nbsp;YoY&nbsp;in Q1&nbsp;2026&nbsp;to&nbsp;reach&nbsp;63.3&nbsp;million units,&nbsp;according to&nbsp;Counterpoint Research’s&nbsp;   preliminary&nbsp;PC&nbsp;shipment&nbsp;data   .&nbsp;The&nbsp;growth&nbsp;was&nbsp;fueled by two key drivers&nbsp;–&nbsp;pre-emptive buying before memory-led price increases hit the retail level, and the necessary hardware refreshes caused by the termination of Windows 10 support.&nbsp;&nbsp;    The competitive landscape saw major players capitalizing on the refresh cycle, with several companies posting double-digit&nbsp;YoY percentage&nbsp;growth:&nbsp;      Lenovo   &nbsp;maintained&nbsp;its market leadership,&nbsp;with shipments rising&nbsp;9% YoY in Q1&nbsp;2026&nbsp;to&nbsp;16.5&nbsp;million units,&nbsp;achieving a 26% market&nbsp;share&nbsp;and&nbsp;marking the highest first-quarter performance on record.&nbsp;     HP   &nbsp;saw a&nbsp;5%&nbsp;YoY&nbsp;decline in shipments, yet it&nbsp;maintained&nbsp;a substantial lead over the third-placed&nbsp;vendor.&nbsp;     Dell   &nbsp;saw&nbsp;a&nbsp;8% YoY&nbsp;increase&nbsp;driven by replacement demand within the commercial sector.&nbsp;     Apple’s   &nbsp;shipments&nbsp;jumped&nbsp;11%&nbsp;YoY in Q1&nbsp;2026, delivering 6.7&nbsp;million units&nbsp;thanks to&nbsp;the&nbsp;initial&nbsp;shipments of the new MacBooks in March. Looking ahead, the ramp-up in production and delivery of these models is set to drive even more significant growth in the following quarter.&nbsp;     Asus   &nbsp;recorded the most explosive&nbsp;YoY&nbsp;growth,&nbsp;surging by&nbsp;an impressive 20% to reach&nbsp;4.8&nbsp;million units,&nbsp;driven by solid consumer notebook demand.&nbsp;      Together, these top five vendors captured&nbsp;nearly&nbsp;80%&nbsp;of the global PC market, underscoring continued consolidation at the high end. Smaller OEMs, by contrast, saw flat or declining volumes.&nbsp;&nbsp;    QoQ Price Trends for PC Memory, Q2 2025 - Q3 2026        &nbsp;Source: Counterpoint's Memory Price Tracker and Forecast*     *Prices in this report are based on the contract price      Senior Analyst&nbsp;Minsoo Kang   &nbsp;noted,&nbsp;“According to Counterpoint’s&nbsp;   Memory Price Tracker and Forecast   ,&nbsp;PC&nbsp;memory prices surged&nbsp;nearly twofold&nbsp;in Q1 2026 compared to the previous quarter, and this upward trend is expected to persist through Q2&nbsp;2026&nbsp;at a moderated pace. The aggressive expansion in AI infrastructure investment is driving up overall&nbsp;component&nbsp;costs, which will&nbsp;likely&nbsp;impact&nbsp;the pricing of CPUs and other key components in PC.&nbsp;Ultimately, the&nbsp;sustained upward pressure on costs and the resulting hike in retail prices are expected to&nbsp;have&nbsp;a significant negative impact&nbsp;on&nbsp;the&nbsp;PC market’s&nbsp;growth in 2026.”&nbsp;    While higher prices may provide some ASP (average selling price) uplift, the projected YoY drop in shipment volumes&nbsp;will result in&nbsp;revenue&nbsp;declines for most of the OEMs. However, around 40% of the current installed base still consists of Windows 10 or older systems, suggesting that&nbsp;a significant portion&nbsp;of devices still require upgrades. Consequently, the PC market’s decline is likely to be less pronounced compared to other consumer electronics segments.&nbsp;    Furthermore, with the launch of Qualcomm’s&nbsp;second-generation chipsets and the steady rollout of AI-capable CPUs from Intel and AMD, the&nbsp;demand&nbsp;for AI PCs&nbsp;is set for sustained growth.&nbsp;     Associate Director David Naranjo   &nbsp;said,&nbsp;“PC demand in Q1 2026&nbsp;benefited&nbsp;from short-term tailwinds, particularly pre-emptive buying ahead of memory-driven price increases and the ongoing Windows 10 refresh cycle. However, these factors are frontloading demand rather than signaling sustained growth. As we move through 2026, supply-side pressures from DRAM and NAND pricing will continue to&nbsp;weigh on&nbsp;volumes, and the refresh cycle alone will not be enough to offset the expected decline. Looking ahead to 2027, easing memory constraints combined with the continued ramp of AI PCs should help stabilize the market and support a return to modest growth.”&nbsp;&nbsp;    This year&nbsp;will be a critical period for testing how PC OEMs respond to market volatility.
