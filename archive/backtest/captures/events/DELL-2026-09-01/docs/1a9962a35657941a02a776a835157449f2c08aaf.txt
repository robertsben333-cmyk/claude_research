Key Elements      Dell Technologies    Evercore ISI    Amit Daryanani    CoreWeave    SpaceX    TipRanks                                                                                           Dell Technologies (DELL) heads into its September 1 earnings report with AI server demand still outpacing supply, and at least one prominent analyst sees room for another standout quarter. 
 Evercore ISI's Amit Daryanani reiterated an Outperform rating on the stock and a $550 price target, implying roughly 25% upside from Dell's recent price near $442. He also added the shares to Evercore's "TAP Outperform List," a tactical designation reserved for names the firm expects to outperform over a short horizon. 
 Wall Street consensus calls for about $44.9 billion in revenue and $4.89 in earnings per share for Dell's fiscal second quarter, which would represent roughly 51% year-over-year top-line growth. Daryanani expects the company to clear both marks and raise its fiscal 2027 outlook, arguing that the debate around Dell's AI infrastructure business has shifted from whether demand is real to how much equipment the company can physically ship. 
 The AI server franchise remains the central story. Last quarter Dell booked $24.4 billion in AI orders, recognized $16.1 billion in AI server revenue, and exited the period with a record $51.3 billion AI backlog. Daryanani said the pipeline is still several times larger than that backlog, underscoring the scale of underlying demand. 
 Supply, not customer appetite, is now the binding constraint. His channel checks suggest component shortages are broadening beyond memory to include CPUs, optical parts, and hard drives, and he expects conditions to remain tight into 2027. That dynamic could cap near-term revenue recognition even as demand accelerates.     
 Outside AI servers, Daryanani expects storage revenue to climb about 10% year over year, helped by easier comparisons and rising data-management requirements tied to AI workloads. He also sees the Client Solutions Group benefiting from a strong commercial PC refresh cycle and higher average selling prices, though operating margins are expected to slow sequentially. 
 Cloud spending from two of Dell's largest customers, CoreWeave (CRWV) and SpaceX (SPCX), should continue to support AI server demand, according to the analyst. The constraint remains fulfillment capacity rather than order flow. 
 Daryanani ranks #21 out of 12,496 analysts tracked by TipRanks, with a 71% success rate and an average return per rating of 37.70%. 
 Dell shares have been on a torrid run, gaining 254% year to date. The broader analyst community holds a Moderate Buy consensus rating based on 14 Buy and six Hold recommendations from 20 analysts, with an average price target of $496.29, implying about 12% upside from current levels. 
 For fiscal 2027, the Street is currently modeling more than 50% revenue growth, and Evercore expects those estimates to move higher following the earnings report.
