             What move do ORCL options imply before the next expiration?

             What is the difference between ORCL&#39;s IV rank and IV percentile? 
             IV rank (currently 64) measures where today&#39;s implied volatility sits between its 52-week low and high. IV percentile (currently 71%) counts the share of trading days in the past year with lower implied volatility than today. Percentile is less distorted by one-off volatility spikes.
