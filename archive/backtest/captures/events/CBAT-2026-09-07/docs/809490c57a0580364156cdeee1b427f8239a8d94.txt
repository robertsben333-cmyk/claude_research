               CBAK Energy Reports Progress in Customer Testing of Full-Tab 26650 LFP Cells for AI Data Center Backup Power 
             
           

         


        
         
           Rhea-AI Summary 
          
              CBAK Energy (NASDAQ: CBAT)  reported progress in customer testing and commercialization planning for its new full-tab cylindrical LFP cells, the  26650 HP V2.0  and  26650 PFS2 V2.0 , developed over a 15‑month R&amp;D program for AI data center BBU and UPS backup power.  According to CBAK Energy, dedicated cathode, anode, and electrolyte formulations and a full-tab architecture target voltage sag, heat buildup, and degradation during high‑rate discharge.
