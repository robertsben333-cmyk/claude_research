                 About lululemon athletica      lululemon athletica (NASDAQ:LULU)  is a design-focused athletic apparel company known for performance-oriented apparel, footwear and accessories. The company’s product portfolio centers on technical apparel for yoga, running, training and everyday active lifestyle use and includes tops, bottoms, outerwear, underwear, bags and a growing footwear assortment. lululemon emphasizes fabric science and product innovation, marketing garments that blend performance features with lifestyle styling. 

 Products are developed in-house and produced through a network of third-party manufacturers. The company sells primarily through its own retail channels — company-operated stores and a global e-commerce platform — complemented by a limited set of wholesale relationships and strategic partnerships. lululemon’s retail footprint and online operations target a broad consumer base while emphasizing direct-to-consumer control over presentation, customer experience and data. 

 Founded in Vancouver, Canada, in 1998 by Chip Wilson, lululemon built its brand around yoga and community-driven marketing before expanding into a wider range of athletic categories and international markets. The company went public in the late 2000s and has since grown its presence across North America, Europe and the Asia-Pacific region, operating hundreds of stores and serving customers worldwide through its digital channels. 

 lululemon’s strategy centers on brand strength, product innovation, community engagement and vertical distribution. Its long-term initiatives have included expanding product categories, investing in digital and omnichannel capabilities, and growing international store and e-commerce penetration. Corporate governance and day-to-day operations are managed by an executive team and board of directors, with the company continuing to prioritize innovation and customer experience as it scales globally.         function SendMagicLinkOnModal(token) {
            var notice = document.getElementById('loginOnModalWarning');
            var email = document.getElementById('txtLoginOnModalEmail').value;
            if (email == "") {
                notice.innerHTML = " Please enter your email address. ";
                return;
            }
            document.getElementById('btnMagicLinkOnModal').classList.add("active-loading");
            $.ajax({
                type: 'GET',
                url: '/scripts/modal/EmailChecker.ashx?command=magiclink&email=' + encodeURIComponent(email) + '&returnUrl=' + encodeURIComponent(window.location.pathname + window.location.search) + '&recaptchaToken=' + encodeURIComponent(token),
                success: function (data) {
                    var btn = document.getElementById('btnMagicLinkOnModal');
                    btn.classList.remove("active-loading");
                    if (data.indexOf('c-red') > -1) {
                        notice.innerHTML = data;
                    } else {
                        notice.innerHTML = " " + data + " ";
                        btn.disabled = true;
                        btn.classList.remove('btn-secondary');
                        btn.classList.add('btn-disabled');
                    }
                }
            });
        }

        // Toggle modal magic link button style based on email input
        document.addEventListener('input', function (e) {
            if (e.target.id === 'txtLoginOnModalEmail') {
                var btn = document.getElementById('btnMagicLinkOnModal');
                if (btn) {
                    var hasEmail = e.target.value.trim() !== '';
                    btn.classList.toggle('btn-disabled', !hasEmail);
                    btn.classList.toggle('btn-secondary', hasEmail);
                }
            }
        });
