Copart Investment Narrative Recap              To own Copart, you generally need to believe its global salvage vehicle marketplace and land network can keep attracting insurance and noninsurance volume, even as accident frequency and insurance behavior evolve. The more immediate portfolio impact for some shareholders may come from Copart's late June reclassification across Russell indices, as it leaves several large cap growth and value benchmarks and joins the Russell Midcap and Midcap Value indices.
