Summary   Snowflake   ( SNOW   -0.34% )  delivered acceleration across core and AI-driven business lines, raising full-year guidance and highlighting significant product and operational advances. Management emphasized AI&#x27;s catalyzing role, with Cortex Code (CoCo) and Snowflake Intelligence named as crucial growth engines now contributing meaningful revenue and customer expansion. The company reported a five-year, $6 billion AWS contract more than doubling prior commitments, supporting both reduced bandwidth costs and AI workload scaling. Share repurchases and sizable cash balances reinforce balance sheet strength, while enhanced partner agreements with AWS, OpenAI, and SAP expand the AI data cloud ecosystem without dependence on a single platform.   Non-GAAP operating margin guidance increased to 13.5% for the full year, with capital efficiency bolstered by measured, acquisition-focused hiring.  Management described a &quot;flywheel effect&quot; as rapid adoption of agentic products compounds both platform consumption and AI monetization opportunities.  Concrete examples cited include a major bank&#x27;s completed large-scale data migration and Nestlé&#x27;s deployment of Snowflake for digital transformation spanning 50,000 global users.  The acquisition of Natoma extends Snowflake’s agentic control plane into practical enterprise application workflows, enabling business users to act seamlessly without leaving the platform.  Leadership transition at Chief Revenue Officer and forthcoming departure of Co-Founder Benoit Dageville signal both stability and ongoing innovation within the executive team.                           Industry glossary    CoCo (Cortex Code) : Snowflake’s AI-driven code-generation and agent platform, enabling developers and partners to automate application and workflow creation within the Snowflake environment.   Snowflake Intelligence : The business user interface overlaying Snowflake’s platform, providing a natural language interface for data insight, actions, and AI-powered analytics.   Agentic control plane : Unified layer within Snowflake where business intent translates into governed actions across enterprise data, models, applications, and security policies.   Observe acquisition : Recent Snowflake acquisition contributing primarily personnel and limited product revenue, with a cited margin headwind.   Natoma : Targeted acquisition aimed at integrating application workflow automation and extending Snowflake’s AI capabilities directly into everyday business tools.
