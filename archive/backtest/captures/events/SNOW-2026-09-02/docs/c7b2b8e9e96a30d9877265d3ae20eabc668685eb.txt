       What the data shows

Within its peer set (ai infrastructure companies at public stage in the $500k+ acv band), Snowflake's NRR is roughly in line with the cell median of  126% .          Competitive dynamics 
         
            Oracle / Teradata  - Free SnowConvert tool released to accelerate customer migrations away from legacy platforms.   Microsoft Fabric / OneLake  - Competition and co-opetition in data fabric and open lakehouse architecture; Snowflake collaborates by allowing reads from OneLake and integrating Cortex with Copilot.   Databricks  - Noted in the context of ecosystem moves such as Databricks acquiring Neon while Snowflake expands Unistore and Iceberg capabilities.   Databricks / Microsoft Fabric / Hyperscalers  - Customers evaluate various data platforms, but Snowflake positions around simplicity, unified governance, and connected data sharing.   AWS, Azure, and GCP  - Large public cloud providers serve as underlying infrastructure and primary competitors across multiple platform capabilities.   Teradata  - Completed complex data warehouse migration of a large US bank from legacy Teradata to Snowflake.
