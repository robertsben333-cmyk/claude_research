			Casey’s targets 120-store expansion after record earnings year	 
 
 
	 
 
				 

 
			 
	  
	 
					 
					 
	 
 
 
			 
	  
	  Kathy A. Bolten   |   Jun 10, 2026 | 4:09 pm  |    2 min read time   |  505 words  |    All Latest News ,  Banking and Finance ,  Registered ,  Retail and Business    |     
 
 
 
	 
 
	  
 
 
	
 Casey’s General Stores plans to step up its expansion in fiscal 2027, adding at least 120 new stores through an even mix of acquisitions and new builds, following a year of record earnings and strong inside-store sales growth, company leaders said during Wednesday’s earnings call with investors. 


 
  
 
 
 
 Darren Rebelez  
 


 The planned addition of new stores puts Casey’s at a 4% expansion rate, which is in line with a typical year, said Darren Rebelez, chairman, CEO and president of the convenience store chain headquartered in Ankeny. Casey’s added fewer stores in its most recent fiscal year because it was concentrating on integrating CEFCO Convenience Stores, a 200-store chain acquired in November 2024, into the company’s operations, he said. 



 The company’s expansion plan comes on the heels of a strong fiscal 2026 in which Casey’s delivered its highest-ever diluted earnings per share of $19.16 (up 30.9% over the prior year), net income of $714.4 million (up 30.7%) and nearly $1.5 billion in earnings before income, taxes, depreciation and amortization (up 23.6%).   



 Casey’s fiscal year ended on April 30. 



 Casey’s is “very bullish” on the current merger and acquisition environment, said Steve Bramlage, the company’s chief financial officer. “There is a very long tail of small players in the industry and a lot of them are under significant operational pressure.” 



 Company officials are having more “conversations than we ever have had” with owners of small convenience stores about possible mergers or acquisitions, Bramlage said. “I think there is a lot of receptivity to what Casey’s can bring to an owner of a business as a steward of that business going forward. … [Casey’s] will continue to pursue those opportunities in a way that makes sense for us and our shareholders.” 



 In recent years, Casey’s has expanded its footprint to include Texas and Florida and to include a larger presence in Ohio, Michigan, Kentucky and Tennessee, Rebelez said. While strong competition exists in those areas, Casey’s is performing well, he said. 



 “I would probably envision us, from a store development standpoint, moving more north and west towards the core footprint of our geography as opposed to south and east towards the heart of Florida,” Rebelez said. “We feel very confident in our ability to compete in whatever geography we&#8217;re operating in.” 



 Casey’s, the third largest convenience store chain in the U.S., operates over 2,900 stores in 19 states. 



 In 2023, Casey’s unveiled a three-year strategic plan that included growing the number of units it had, improving operational efficiencies and accelerating and expanding its food business. During that time, thin-crust pizza was added to Casey’s whole pie business; the hot sandwich lineup was revamped; and chicken wings were added. During the three-year time period, 500 new units were added and stores are operating more efficiently, Rebelez said. “I’m extremely proud of the growth of the organization.” 



 Casey’s officials will release a new three-year strategic plan on June 24 during a presentation in New York.

  function compareVersions(versions) {
    // 1) get common precision for both versions, for example for "10.0" and "9" it should be 2
    var precision = Math.max(getVersionPrecision(versions[0]), getVersionPrecision(versions[1]));
    var chunks = map(versions, function (version) {
      var delta = precision - getVersionPrecision(version);

      // 2) "9" -> "9.0" (for precision = 2)
      version = version + new Array(delta + 1).join(".0");

      // 3) "9.0" -> ["000000000"", "000000009"]
      return map(version.split("."), function (chunk) {
        return new Array(20 - chunk.length).join("0") + chunk;
      }).reverse();
    });

    // iterate in reverse order by reversed chunks array
    while (--precision >= 0) {
      // 4) compare: "000000009" > "000000010" = false (but "9" > "10" = true)
      if (chunks[0][precision] > chunks[1][precision]) {
        return 1;
      }
      else if (chunks[0][precision] === chunks[1][precision]) {
        if (precision === 0) {
          // all version chunks are same
          return 0;
        }
      }
      else {
        return -1;
      }
