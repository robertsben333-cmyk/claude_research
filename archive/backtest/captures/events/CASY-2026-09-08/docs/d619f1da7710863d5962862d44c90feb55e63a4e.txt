       Casey&#8217;s Announces Addition of Finance Leader Stanley J. Sutula III to its Board of Directors 
        
       
        ANKENY, Iowa, June 8, 2026  &#8211; (Business Wire) &#8211; Casey&#8217;s  General Sto res, Inc. (Nasdaq: CASY), the third largest convenience retailer and fifth largest pizza chain in the United States, today announced the appointment of Stanley J. Sutula III to
        its Board of Directors (the &#8220;Board&#8221;). 
        
       
       Mr. Sutula brings to the Board over 35 years of experience in corporate finance, financial planning and operations, tax, strategic planning and risk management.&#160; Since
        2020, he has served as Chief Financial Officer at Colgate-Palmolive Company (NYSE: CL), where he oversees its global finance, global IT and mergers and acquisitions teams.&#160; He was previously at Pitney Bowes Inc. (NYSE: PBI), where he served as
        Executive VP and Chief Financial Officer, and spent 28 years at IBM Corporation (NYSE: IBM) in various financial management roles, including as its Vice President and Controller. 
        
       
       &#8220;We are excited to welcome Stan to the Board as he adds deep financial and strategic expertise to our already expansive board capabilities. His leadership in these areas
        will benefit Casey&#8217;s, the Board and its shareholders immensely,&#8221; said Darren Rebelez, Casey&#8217;s Board Chair, President and CEO. 
        
       
       Mr. Sutula graduated from Northeastern University with a degree in Finance &amp; Management and holds an MBA in Finance from Fordham University&#8217;s Gabelli School of
        Business. 
        
       
       Mr. Sutula&#8217;s addition to the Board will temporarily bring the number of directors from eleven to twelve, as director Cara Heiden has decided to retire from the Board
        effective September 2, 2026.&#160; &#8220;On behalf of the Board and the entire Casey&#8217;s team, I want to extend a sincere thank you to Cara for nearly a decade of distinguished service and leadership on the Board and its Audit Committee.&#160; She helped build
        Casey&#8217;s into the great organization it is today and we wish her nothing but the best in her retirement from the Board in September,&#8221; said Rebelez.
