    Boosted by higher gas prices at the pump, but also building on strong operational discipline, Murphy USA saw a big leap second quarter income, and CEO Mindy West suggested the second half of the year could be even stronger. 
 &#8220;Given strong year-to-date results, while we remain in line with most of our guided performance metrics, market conditions remain supportive of healthy retail margins and suggest the business is on pace to deliver significantly higher net income and adjusted EBITDA in 2026,&#8221; said Murphy USA CEO Mindy West. &#8220;If we assume second half all-in fuel margins average 35 cents per gallon, versus 37.9 cents per gallon in the first half, we expect the business to deliver net income of approximately $636 million and adjusted EBITDA of approximately $1.25 billion.” 
 
    
		
 For the quarter, the El Dorado-based gas and convenience store company posted total revenue of $6.8 billion, compared to $5 billion one year ago. Quarterly net income was $209.1 million, or $11.27 per diluted share, compared to net income of $145.6 million, or $7.36 per diluted share, in the second quarter of last year. 
 &#8220;Murphy USA delivered another quarter of strong financial and operational performance, demonstrating the earnings power and durability of our low-cost, high-volume business model,&#8221; said West. 
 There were a number of high-performance metrics including: 
 
 Total fuel contribution for Q2 2026 was 40.6 cpg, compared to 32.0 cpg in Q2 2025; 
 Total retail gallons increased 3.9%; and 
 Volumes on a same store sales basis increased 0.5% in Q2 2026 compared to Q2 2025. 
 
 &#8220;Retail fuel margins were reflective of persistent volatility throughout the second quarter, as the company delivered year-over-year and sequential improvement in both same-store and total fuel volumes, which were up 0.5% and 3.9%, respectively,&#8221; said West. 
 While earnings and revenue beat estimates from Wall Street, Murphy USA&#8217;s stock (NYSE: MUSA) was flat closing the day at $580.94. In after hours trading, shares declined about 1.5%. 
 Over the last year, Murphy USA stock (NYSE: MUSA) has traded between a low of $349.83 per share and a high of $636.05 per share.
