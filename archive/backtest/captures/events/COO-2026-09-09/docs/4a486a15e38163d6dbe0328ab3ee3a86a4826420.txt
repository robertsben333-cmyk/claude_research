Excerpt from our historical coverage   May 6, 2026   Cooper Companies prepares divestment of women’s health and fertility medical device unit CooperSurgical business  Press Release   Cooper Companies is set to divest its women’s health and fertility medical device unit, CooperSurgical, with Wells Fargo estimating a deal price of USD 4.1bn. The sale is expected to bring in net proceeds that will be used primarily for share repurchases and debt reduction. CooperSurgical, which has manufacturing facilities in US, UK, Denmark and Costa Rica, is known for creating tools and technologies used in doctor&#x27;s offices, hospitals, and fertility clinics. Cooper Companies has expressed a preference to sell the business as a whole, with several parties showing interest. Wells Fargo believes the divestiture is strategically sound, with a financial buyer, such as a private equity firm, likely to be involved.
