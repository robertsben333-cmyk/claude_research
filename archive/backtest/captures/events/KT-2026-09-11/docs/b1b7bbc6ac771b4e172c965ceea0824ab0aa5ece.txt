SK Telecom Q2 profits soar 67% on AIDC business growth   SKT's operating profit grew 67.3% year-on-year in Q2 2026 to $397.31 million as its AI data center business gained momentum.                Gigi Onag ,  Senior Editor, APAC ,  Light Reading  August 5, 2026       2 Min Read          (Source: Thomas Kyhn/Alamy Stock Photo)                                        SK Telecom (SKT) has posted its consolidated earnings for the second quarter of 2026, with operating profit growing 67.3% year-on-year to 566 billion Korean won (US$397.31million), reflecting growing momentum at its AI data center (AIDC) business.    The South Korean telecom operator finished the quarter with a net profit of KRW466 billion ($327 million), marking a 459.8% year-on-year increase. Meanwhile, revenues increased by 0.5% year-on-year to KRW4.36 trillion ($3.06 billion).    SKT attributed its improved financial performance to tighter cost controls and the expansion of its data center business. The company also cited favorable base effects from one-off expenses recorded last year, including the    cost of replacing USIMs and compensation for retail store losses    resulting from the cybersecurity incident in 2025.    &quot;The telecommunications business, which has remained focused on restoring customer trust, showed a clear turnaround driven by efforts to enhance customer value and improve profitability. The AI business also recorded robust growth as execution accelerated under the company&#x27;s strategy of focus and prioritization,&quot; SKT said in a    statement    on Wednesday.    Related:  SK Telecom commits $510M to new AI data center subsidiary   Rapidly expanding AIDC business   SKT&#x27;s AIDC revenue soared to KRW136.2 billion ($95.55 million) in the second quarter of 2026, marking a staggering year-on-year surge of 92.2%, propelled by the company&#x27;s broadened operations. It is currently the company&#x27;s fastest-growing business segment.    &quot;In the first half of the year, we focused on strengthening our fundamentals based on stable telecommunications business profitability while laying the foundation for the rapid expansion of our AI data center business,&quot; said SKT&#x27;s CFO Park Jong-seok.    &quot;Going forward, we will make every effort to play a leading role in enabling Korea to emerge as an AI infrastructure hub in Asia,&quot; he added.    Two weeks ago, SKT    established a new subsidiary   , SK Hyper, which will focus solely on developing the South Korean AIDC business. It has committed to investing a total of KRW750 billion ($526.18 million) in this new venture by 2030.    SK Hyper will be responsible for securing sites, constructing and operating substations, acquiring customers, and commercializing the AIDC business. It plans to open 5GW of AIDC capacity in phases, beginning in 2029.    The company will develop AI infrastructure in stages, beginning with a GW-class AIDC cluster in Ulsan and subsequently adding more GW-class AIDCs in the Chungcheong and Honam regions.    Considering market demand and other factors, SK Hyper is expected to expand in stages toward a total AIDC capacity of 15GW by 2035.
