 LG Uplus achieved its highest-ever quarterly operating profit, fueled by concurrent growth in its AI infrastructure and services segments. 
 During the second-quarter earnings presentation, CFO Yeo Myung-hee emphasized, “Consolidated operating profit grew 13.1% year-on-year to ₩344.5 billion, marking a record quarterly high. Even excluding one-off factors, operating profit exceeded approximately ₩320 billion, clearly demonstrating fundamental improvement in profitability.” 
 Consolidated service revenue reached ₩3.1 trillion, while standalone service revenue came in at ₩2.9 trillion, representing year-on-year growth of 2.0% and 2.8%, respectively. EBITDA increased 5.9% to ₩1.03 trillion (approximately $725.1 million), reflecting enhanced cash generation capability.

 AI Infrastructure Drives Growth… AIDC Revenue Surges 29% 
 By business segment, the enterprise infrastructure division stood out. Jung Young-hoon, head of enterprise AI business, stated, “Enterprise infrastructure revenue in Q2 reached ₩464.4 billion (approximately $325.4 million), up 8.6% year-on-year. In particular, AIDC revenue surged 28.9% driven by colocation expansion, leading overall growth.” 
 For the smart home segment, Oh In-ho, head of media business, explained, “Revenue grew 4.3% year-on-year to ₩663.8 billion (approximately $465.1 million), with IPTV revenue up 2.1% and internet revenue up 7.6%.” The mobile segment maintained stable growth, with service revenue increasing 1.2% to ₩1.6 trillion (approximately $1.1 billion). The 5G penetration rate climbed to 84.9%. 
 The composition of service revenue by business segment is visualized below. 
      
 “Eliminating the Hassle of Choosing Plans”… Simply 2.0 and All-in-One Launch 
 Kang Jin-wook, head of the mobile digital business group, highlighted concrete achievements in customer experience innovation. “We consolidated the previous 53 types of 5G and LTE plans into 18 with the launch of Simply 2.0, applying age-based additional data provisions and automatic benefit switching,” he said. “This allows customers to use services conveniently without complex plan comparisons.” 
 The company also launched an “All-in-One” product that streamlines the process by allowing customers to apply for combined discounts with a single application when subscribing to both mobile and internet services simultaneously. These efforts resulted in LG Uplus securing first place in the mobile phone category of the 2026 National Customer Satisfaction Survey for the first time, while also ranking first in the IPTV category for the fifth consecutive year. 
 The ixi-O roaming call service has been launched in 170 countries, enabling voice calls without international calling charges when connected via Wi-Fi or subscribed to a roaming plan. Recently, the company unveiled the world’s first iPad-exclusive IPTV app, “Uplus TV Free 5 for iPad,” leading to a significant increase in TV Free subscribers.     
 Paju AIDC to Open Sequentially from 2027… “Profitability Improvement via Hybrid Self-Investment and DBO Model” 
 Regarding the highly anticipated AIDC business, Jung Young-hoon stated, “We are constructing a 200MW-class hyperscale AIDC in Paju, with plans to sequentially open four buildings between 2027 and 2028.” The strategy targets inference service demand in the Seoul metropolitan area and training/non-real-time inference demand in regional areas, with total investment amounting to approximately ₩2 trillion (approximately $1.4 billion). 
 On the profitability outlook for the new data center, he expressed confidence: “Leasing rates will be set higher due to increased investment for AI server operations such as liquid cooling. Electricity charges will also increase in total amount due to metered billing combined with rising AI utilization rates, leading to improved profitability.” 
 Addressing concerns about financial soundness, CFO Yeo Myung-hee responded, “While CAPEX will increase in 2026, we maintain a policy of executing it within the EBITDA range to secure free cash flow. By combining asset-light models such as DBO, we are enhancing capital efficiency and will maintain our mid-to-long-term corporate value enhancement plan without external financing.” 
 Shareholder Returns Expanded… Interim Dividend of ₩270 and ₩90 Billion Share Buyback and Cancellation 
 Based on improved earnings and cash generation, the scale of shareholder returns has also increased. The interim dividend was set at ₩270 per share, an 8% increase year-on-year, and the share buyback program was expanded to ₩90 billion. CFO Yeo stated, “The repurchased treasury shares will be cancelled, thereby increasing per-share value and enhancing the effectiveness of shareholder returns. We will maintain a policy of gradual expansion based on profit growth.” 
 Regarding full-year guidance, she emphasized, “Standalone service revenue in the first half grew 3.4% year-on-year, already exceeding the approximately 2% level presented at the beginning of the year. While the growth rate may moderate somewhat in the second half, we are fully confident in achieving our annual guidance.” 
 Q&#x26;A: “Growing the Market with One LG Solutions, Not Competing with LG CNS” 
 KB Securities analyst Kim Jun-seop questioned the AI strategy within LG Group and the potential overlap of roles with LG CNS. CSO Kevin Cho explained, “Under the One LG strategy, the group is consolidating capabilities in cooling, power, operations, and networks to strengthen AI infrastructure competitiveness, while advancing the value chain by linking with AI technologies such as EXAONE. LG Uplus serves as a core axis of AI infrastructure operations, providing AIDC capabilities in prime metropolitan locations, network expertise, and operational know-how.” 
 On business differentiation from LG CNS, Jung Young-hoon added, “As a telecommunications operator providing integrated network services, LG Uplus is securing the largest data center capacity by combining self-invested and operated centers with external DBO projects. Given the rapid market growth, rather than competing, we are growing the market together with One LG solutions while strengthening each company’s capabilities.”
