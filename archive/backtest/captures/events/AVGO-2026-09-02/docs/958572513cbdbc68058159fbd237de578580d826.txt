 \n\n**Broadcom Revenue Breakdown FY2025: $63.9B Split by Segment**\n\nBroadcom (AVGO) closed fiscal year 2025 with $63.9 billion in net  revenue , a 24% year-over-year increase from $51.6 billion in FY2024. That headline number alone makes Broadcom the fastest-growing mega-cap semiconductor company on the planet. But the real  story  is not the topline &#8212; it is how that  revenue  splits between two increasingly powerful engines: custom AI silicon and  enterprise  infrastructure — as explored in  the economics of AI compute infrastructure  —   software  built on the VMware stack.\n\n**Revenue by Segment: Two $30B+ Businesses Under One Roof**\n\nBroadcom operates through two reportable segments, each of which has crossed the threshold into standalone mega-cap territory.\n\n*Semiconductor Solutions: $36.9 billion (58% of  revenue )*\n\nThe semiconductor division generated approximately $36.9 billion in FY2025, up 22% year-over-year from $30.1 billion in FY2024. This segment encompasses Broadcom&#8217;s networking, broadband, wireless, and storage semiconductor products &#8212; but the  growth  engine within it is unmistakable: AI.\n\nBroadcom&#8217;s AI semiconductor  revenue  reached roughly $15.7 billion in FY2025, up from $12.2 billion in FY2024, representing a 29% increase.
