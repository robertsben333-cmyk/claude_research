SUMMARY   Guidewire   ( GWRE   +3.86% )  management confirmed that fully ramped ARR continues to outpace standard ARR growth, which they expect to drive durable upward momentum into the next fiscal year. The sales pipeline was repeatedly described as robust, with management expressing &quot;a tremendous amount of confidence in the sales organization&quot; and in strong close rates for Q4. Customers are showing heightened demand for embedded AI and workflow automation solutions, as evidenced by rapid ProNavigator and PricingCenter adoption. Field-engineering programs and Agentic development tools are credited with shortening migration timelines, directly supporting an estimated 35% efficiency improvement in on-prem-to-cloud migrations. The imminent launch of Underwriting Center is being pursued with select design partners and has generated &quot;a tremendous amount of interest&quot; among commercial lines underwriters. Guidewire emphasized that international and multi-product cloud expansions are key to its growth strategy, as exemplified by the quarter&#x27;s European, Brazilian, and U.S. insurer wins.   Management stated that deal slippage in the quarter was unrelated to macroeconomic or AI-induced headwinds, describing it as a timing effect with no lasting impact on demand trends.  Jeffrey Cooper noted that &quot;fully ramped ARR growth at 22%&quot; last year sets a precedent for potentially similar or higher rates, driven by larger commitments and strong pipeline visibility.  Guidewire’s sales cycles for ProNavigator are significantly shorter than legacy core modernization projects, leading to faster recognition of new revenues.  Chief Executive Officer Rosenbaum explained that AI-enabled products are priced on a percentage of direct written premium (DWP), with technical and contractual guardrails applied to manage resource utilization and protect margins.  Engineering and services hiring has become more measured, partly due to slower corporate headcount growth outside of services and &quot;productivity gains that we&#x27;re seeing with some of the AI tooling&quot; reducing the need for incremental resources.  The transition to cloud and new Agentic tools is now resulting in quantifiable operational savings, with Mullen reporting &quot;about 35% improvement in migration&quot; and expectations of up to 55% as the curve matures.  PricingCenter demand is directly tied to seamless integration with PolicyCenter, aligning new product launches and rate adjustments with increased client competitiveness and efficiency.  Regional variation in AI adoption persists, with all customer segments &quot;want[ing] to get on the learning curve,&quot; but adoption pace and operational exposure differs by country and carrier.                           INDUSTRY GLOSSARY    ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue) : The predictable value of contracted recurring software revenue, annualized for run rate comparability.   DWP (Direct Written Premium) : The total premiums written by insurers, used as a basis for pricing software solutions and value-based contracts at Guidewire.   Agentic tools : AI-driven software agents embedded within insurance workflows to automate decision support and enhance development or operations productivity.   LLM (Large Language Model) : Advanced AI models, such as those powering Guidewire’s AI/Agentic capabilities, utilized for code generation, workflow automation, and enhanced analytics.   MCP server : Guidewire’s Managed Cloud Platform server infrastructure enabling API-driven integrations and deployment of advanced AI models and developer tools. Overall, we&#x27;re seeing increasing platform gravity around Guidewire, and that&#x27;s translating into healthy adoption of new offerings across our portfolio. These trends and the broader momentum in the business were reinforced at recent insurance forums we hosted in Europe, Australia, Japan and Canada. As we&#x27;ve said before, Guidewire sits at the center of the insurance enterprise. With ProNavigator, we have embedded AI decision support directly into the applications and workflows insurers use every day.
