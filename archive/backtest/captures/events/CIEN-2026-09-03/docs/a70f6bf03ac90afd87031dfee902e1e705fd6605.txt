Email  [email&#160;protected]                           TAKEAWAYS    Revenue  -- $1.57 billion, up 40% year-on-year, exceeding prior guidance by $71 million and marking a new quarterly record.   Adjusted Gross Margin  -- 44.9%, up 4 percentage points from Q2 2025 and 90 basis points above guidance.   Adjusted EPS  -- $1.64, nearly quadrupling the prior year and demonstrating profit leverage.   Free Cash Flow  -- $219 million, or 13.9% of revenue, supported by a 20-day improvement in cash conversion cycle from Q1.   Backlog  -- Increased by over $600 million sequentially to $7.7 billion, with management anticipating higher levels by year-end.   Optical Networking Segment  -- Revenue grew 42%, led by &quot;over 55%&quot; year-on-year growth in RLS and Waveserver product lines.   Routing and Switching Segment  -- Revenue rose 88%, primarily from DCOM ramp; customer base continues to expand beyond Meta.   Direct Cloud Customer Revenue  -- Increased 70% year-on-year; service provider revenue grew 28%, with India more than doubling.   Top Two Customers  -- Both cloud providers, each accounting for more than 10% of revenue, collectively representing about one-third of total sales.   Pluggable Optics  -- Revenue on track to more than double from 2025, bolstered by strong demand for 400G and 800G pluggables.   Blue Planet/Nubis Portfolio  -- Nitro Linear Redriver general availability expected this summer, and Vesta 200 6.4T optical engine seeing increased demand for CPO use cases.   Stock Buyback  -- $83 million repurchased at an average price of $371 per share in the quarter.   Fiscal 2026 Guidance Update  -- Raised midpoint revenue outlook to $6.3 billion (32% growth) with gross margin guidance at 44.5%-45%, operating expenses at approximately $1.61 billion, and operating margin of 19%, plus or minus 50 basis points.   Q3 2026 Guidance  -- Revenue expected at approximately $1.625 billion, gross margin at 45% (plus or minus 50 basis points), and operating expenses around $410 million, producing 19%-20% operating margin.   Hyper-Rail Launch  -- Industry&#x27;s first multi-rail award secured from a hyperscaler, with initial multi-hundred-million-dollar contract rollouts beginning in 2027 and multiple pipeline engagements underway.   SUMMARY  Management highlighted the doubling of the addressable network market to an estimated $50 billion by 2029, encompassing both traditional WAN and emerging data center opportunities. Strong customer co-creation and early adoption of next-generation solutions, such as RLS Hyper-Rail and DCOM, are positioning  Ciena   ( CIEN   -2.37% )  ahead of previously anticipated adoption curves for hyperscaler and service provider deployments. Executives reported more than 80% of current hardware backlog is expected to convert to revenue within 12 months, providing elevated visibility into 2027 revenue streams. The back-end supply environment remains constrained, but engineering initiatives and vertically integrated operations are largely mitigating inflationary and component risks. Management anticipates future operating leverage as revenue growth continues to outpace rising OpEx requirements tied mostly to variable compensation and supply chain investments.   CFO Marc Graff stated, &quot;$6.4 billion of that is hardware roughly, and we would expect about 80% of that to be delivered in the next 12 months.&quot;  CEO Gary Smith noted, &quot;The addressable market will approximately double over the next several years to roughly $50 billion by 2029,&quot; with &quot;$8 billion to $10 billion&quot; from in and around the data center and &quot;in excess of about $20 billion&quot; from long-haul metro optical transport.  Executive Adviser Scott McFeely emphasized, &quot;certainly has been happening consistently over the last decade. And it&#x27;s really gotten down to a few folks that have the capability and scale to address the needs of these large customers.&quot;  Management confirmed that the RLS Hyper-Rail launch is expected to drive step-function margin improvement and linear top-line growth from 2027 as hyperscalers standardize on multi-rail architectures.  Management addressed supply chain pressures, noting modem constraints and ongoing pump laser shortages, but said, &quot;as soon as we get them, they&#x27;re going out the door,&quot; underscoring immediate fulfillment of demand.  Ciena’s largest cloud customers are absorbing the majority of incremental supply, resulting in comparatively subdued year-on-year growth rates for service providers outside cloud, even as all segments rebounded from five years of underinvestment.  DCOM and Nubis platform wins with a second and third hyperscaler confirm rising penetration outside Meta, signaling multi-year expansion of Ciena&#x27;s presence in data center architectures.                           INDUSTRY GLOSSARY    RLS (Reconfigurable Line System) : Ciena&#x27;s intelligent optical line system platform for high-capacity, low-latency, and power-efficient data transport.   Hyper-Rail : Next-generation multi-rail line system platform, designed for parallel fiber transmission of high-volume AI workloads over extended distances.   DCOM (Data Center Out-of-Band Management) : Solution combining routing and switching hardware with PON technology to support remote management in hyperscale data centers.   Nubis : Acquired technology platform including Nitro Linear Redriver and Vesta high-speed optical engines, targeting active copper cable and co-packaged optics markets.   MOFN (Managed Optical Fiber Networks) : Dedicated managed fiber deployments for cloud and hyperscale operators, enabling bespoke high-bandwidth interconnects.   CPO (Co-Packaged Optics) : Integration of optical engines directly with switch ASICs for increased data rates and improved power and density performance in data centers.   Pluggables : Optical transceiver modules, such as 400G and 800G, offering flexible, high-speed connectivity options for networking systems.   Neoscaler : Emerging cloud or AI infrastructure providers scaling networks rapidly and representing a new customer segment beyond traditional hyperscalers.   Value Exchange : Management&#x27;s term for pricing strategies balancing supply security, product optimization, and working capital management amid inflationary pressures.
