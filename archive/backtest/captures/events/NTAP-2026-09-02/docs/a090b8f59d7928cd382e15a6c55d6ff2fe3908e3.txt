 NetApp Company Profile 
  ( Get Free Report ) 
  NetApp, Inc (NASDAQ: NTAP) is a data management and storage company that delivers hybrid cloud data services for applications and data. Founded in 1992 as Network Appliance and rebranded as NetApp in 2008, the company is headquartered in Sunnyvale, California. NetApp&#8217;s offering focuses on enabling organizations to store, manage, protect and move data across on-premises environments and major public clouds. 
 The company&#8217;s product portfolio centers on the ONTAP data management software and a range of storage systems and services built around it.
