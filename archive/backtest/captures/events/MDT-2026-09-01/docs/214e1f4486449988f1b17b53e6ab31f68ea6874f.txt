With a focus on addressing the evolving needs of increasingly complex transcatheter aortic valve replacement patients and procedures, Medtronic secures an option for its structural heart portfolio through this strategic investment, supporting the next generation of innovation. The transactions are subject to regulatory approval and customary closing conditions.
