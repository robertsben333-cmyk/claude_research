          Medtronic (MDT) to Announce Q1 FY2027 Earnings on August 18
                            GuruFocus News         07/20/2026 10:14            + GuruFocus.com   on                                                On July 20, 2026, Medtronic PLC  MDT , a leading player in the medical technology sector, announced that it will release its financial results for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 on August 18, 2026, before the U.S. stock market opens. The company's management will hold a conference call to discuss the earnings report and answer analysts' questions.    GF Value™ verdict: $99.87 vs Current Price $83.44 = 16.5% undervalued   GF Score™: 73/100, indicating a solid performance across key financial metrics.   Key financial signal: P/E (TTM) of 22.37x compared to a 5-year median P/E of 26.92x, suggesting potential undervaluation.    What's Behind the News?   Medtronic's upcoming earnings report is significant as it will provide insights into the company's performance amidst a challenging economic landscape marked by inflationary pressures and fluctuating hospital capital expenditures. Analysts are particularly focused on the anticipated quarterly revenues, which are expected to range between $8.2 billion and $8.5 billion, reflecting a year-over-year growth of approximately 4% to 6%. This growth is crucial for assessing the company's ability to meet its previously projected 5% revenue growth for the full fiscal year 2027.   As one of the world’s largest independent medical device companies, Medtronic operates in four key areas: cardiovascular, neurological, surgical, and diabetes care. With a market capitalization of approximately $106.8 billion, the company has a diverse portfolio that includes pacemakers, defibrillators, and advanced energy systems. Approximately 50% of its sales come from international markets, highlighting its global reach and the importance of emerging markets in its growth strategy.   Is MDT Overvalued or Undervalued?   According to GuruFocus, Medtronic's GF Value™ is calculated at $99.87, indicating that the stock is currently undervalued by approximately 16.5% compared to its current trading price of $83.44. This margin of safety suggests that investors may have an opportunity to acquire shares at a discount relative to the company's intrinsic value. The P/E (TTM) ratio stands at 22.37x, which is significantly lower than its 5-year median P/E of 26.92x, reinforcing the notion that Medtronic may be undervalued at its current price point.
