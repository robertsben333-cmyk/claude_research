     The Adobe 2026 increase, a list move you renew into or negotiate past 
     Adobe raised 2026 list prices on most Creative Cloud and Acrobat plans, 12 to 25 percent, with the steepest moves on Experience Cloud and generative credits. The increase is a list move, not a contract right: it applies at renewal, an active ETLA holds its price to term end, and with a usage audit and a credible alternative most enterprises hold the renewal to a low single digit uplift. 
     Prepared by  Redress Compliance  &middot; August 6, 2026 &middot; Adobe advisory. Based on 30 to 40 renewals benchmarked 2024 to 2026. 
     
       What is going up, and when 
        Creative Cloud  List increases of 12 to 25 percent by plan, at the next renewal under per vehicle rules  
        Acrobat  Increases across the family, at renewal, with Foxit resetting the PDF walk away  
        Experience Cloud  The steepest moves in the wave, applied at contract events and negotiated case by case  
        Firefly generative credits  The consumption upsell riding the wave, landing inside quotes wherever nobody separates the line  
       It is a list move you renew into rather than a bill that arrives. What you do before the renewal event is the whole exercise. 
     


     
       Executive summary 
        The increase applies at renewal, not mid term.  An active ETLA holds its pricing to term end, which converts the announcement from an invoice event into a negotiation deadline: the renewal is where the 12 to 25 percent lands, and the renewal is precisely where the counter levers work. 
       The buyers who treat the increase as inevitable pay it; the file says most do not have to. 
        The seats are the first recovery.  Across our renewals,  a ninety day usage pull reclaimed 10 to 20 percent of paid seats  that nobody had touched, and the opening quote almost never reflected real usage. 
       The deployed user audit is the increase's natural offset: a smaller honest base absorbs a higher rate at a flat total. 
        The alternatives finally reset the walk away.  Affinity on design, Figma on product design, and Foxit on PDF made the alternative quote credible for real population slices, and  an alternative on the table moved the final uplift by 6 to 12 points  in the benchmarked renewals. 
       The vehicle matters too: VIP, VIP Marketplace, and ETLA price and renew on different rules, and the increase lands differently on each. 
        The generative credits are the quiet second increase.  
       Firefly add on credits inflate the quote where they ride in unexamined, the same consumption pattern every AI line in the estate now carries: sized to the population that demonstrably generates, priced as its own line. 
       Or absorbed as a stealth uplift on top of the visible one.  Open the renewal six months out or Adobe owns the clock , and the clock is most of the leverage.
