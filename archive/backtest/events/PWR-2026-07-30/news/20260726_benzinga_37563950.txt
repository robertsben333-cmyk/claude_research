It is one of the most important weeks of the second-quarter 2026 earnings season.
Yet the biggest expected mover of the week is none of them. It is a fuel cell company.
Screening Benzinga Pro data for U.S.-listed companies worth more than $50 billion, 12 names carry double-digit implied moves this week.
An implied move Implied is the percentage swing in either direction that options traders expect immediately after earnings.
Which Stocks Could Swing The Most this week?
12. Equinix.: 10.36% Implied Move
The data center operator reports July 29 after the close. Consensus calls for $2.57 billion in revenue and $11.18 per share.
Watch adjusted funds from operations guidance, the cash-flow measure data center REITs report in place of earnings, and the pace of new bookings.
Shares of Equinix Inc. (NASDAQ:EQIX) are up 34.9% this year.
11. Robinhood Markets.: 10.96% Implied Move
The online brokerage reports July 29 after the close.
Analysts model $1.28 billion in revenue and 44 cents per share. Crypto trading revenue, net deposits and prediction market volumes are the swing factors here.
Robinhood Markets Inc. (NASDAQ:HOOD) is the only name on this list down for the year, off 17.8%.
