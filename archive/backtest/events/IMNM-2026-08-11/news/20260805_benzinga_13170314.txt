SpaceX, Teradata, Pinterest And Other Big Stocks Moving Lower In Wednesday’s Pre-Market Session
U.S. stock futures were mixed this morning, with the Dow futures gaining around 100 points on Wednesday.
SpaceX reported second-quarter revenue of $7.81 billion, up 92% year-over-year. The revenue total beat a Street consensus estimate of $6.93 billion according to data from Benzinga Pro. The company reported a loss of nine cents per share, beating a Street consensus estimate of a loss of 24 cents per share.
SpaceX shares dipped 10.3% to $112.48 in pre-market trading.
