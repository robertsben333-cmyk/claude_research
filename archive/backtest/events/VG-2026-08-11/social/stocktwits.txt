If global gas prices crash in Europe (TTF) or Asia (JKM), $VG’s profit margin on contracted volumes remains unaffected. The customer, not $VG, bears the downside risk of global market gluts. Because feedgas costs pass directly through to the buyer, $VG never suffers from squeezed spreads between domestic pipeline gas and international markets.
