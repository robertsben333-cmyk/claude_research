## 5. Thesis
**Bull case:** Technical Solutions and Aviation segment mix (ATS +27%, Aviation +19.5% last quarter, WGNSTAR semiconductor deal) keeps delivering AI-adjacency growth the market has rewarded even through EPS misses; a reaffirmed or narrowed-up guide plus strong bookings and free cash flow could produce the same +6.67% reaction as last quarter regardless of the margin outcome.
**Bear case:** the FY26 guide requires a margin inflection (~90bp sequential) that two straight quarters have failed to deliver, against a largest segment (B&I) that is flat to declining and thin options liquidity offering no dealer-flow cushion.
**Base case:** a mixed print — decent revenue/bookings optics, margin still short of the bar — producing a move inside the 3.7%–11.8% band with a modest downward tilt, consistent with the panel's tight but imperfectly independent bearish lean.

## 6. What flips it
The red-team's strongest reversal case: consensus wants ~+26.8% YoY EPS growth on ~4% revenue growth after two consecutive quarters of margin contraction and two EPS misses, against a fully back-half-loaded guide — this is specific and sourced (segment-margin arithmetic, historical guide-and-miss precedent from Q3 FY25). No persona directly disputes it, but every persona who scored it separately noted ABM's own reaction function has repeatedly not punished exactly this kind of miss (Q2 FY26 missed EPS by 2.2% and the stock still rose 6.67%; three of the last four large misses produced flat-to-up sessions). A clean beat-and-raise on segment margin, by contrast, breaks the bear case outright.
