## ABM — the re-panel moved the wrong way

This is the most useful single comparison in the ledger so far. Same event, two
independent forecasts, three days apart:

| Run | Panel consensus | Panel call | Prelim score | Actual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-09-04 (4 days out) | -8.9 (mixed, disparity 31.2) | Neutral | -18 (wrong sign) | |
| 2026-09-07 (1 day out) | **-16.7** (aligned, disparity 6.6) | Neutral | **+12 (right sign)** | **+7.55%** |

The panel got **more bearish** as the print approached (-8.9 → -16.7) while the cheap
single-researcher preliminary read flipped from wrong (-18) to right (+12) over the same
three days, picking up fresher information the panel's synthesis did not fully credit.
Both panel calls stayed labelled Neutral (neither crossed the ±25 Lean threshold), so the
published call did not change — but the panel's own confidence moved further from the
truth while the cheap read moved toward it.

The chair review itself flagged the mechanism: four of seven personas (options-positioning,
sentiment, red-team, forensics) leaned in part on the same single Ortex article
("ABM Industries: ERP Scars Meet a Critical Print") for the "shorts already covered, no
squeeze fuel" framing, which is exactly why the chair downgraded certainty_tier from the
mechanically-computed High to Med despite the unusually tight 6.6-point disparity. That
override was the right call — the tight agreement was partly an artifact of shared
sourcing, not seven independent reads converging — but it is also a sign the underlying
bearish case picked up an extra push from a single shared source between the 09-04 and
09-07 runs, in the wrong direction.

Reported adjusted EPS $1.04 (vs ~$1.01 consensus), record revenue $2.3B (+4.2% YoY), FY26
EPS guidance narrowed to $3.95-4.10 with the floor raised. Close-to-close $47.05 → $50.60,
**+7.55%**. This run's red-team persona stated, verbatim, the exact scenario that would
break their own bearish -20 call: *"A Q3 print at or above $1.04 with segment operating
margin up year over year, plus a narrowing of FY26 guidance into the upper half and a
third straight quarter of positive FCF."* EPS landed at precisely $1.04 and guidance
narrowed upward. That is the second run in a row where this persona named the specific
falsifying scenario for its own call and that scenario is what happened — worth watching
whether the red-team's "what would break my case" field is systematically better
calibrated than its own headline direction score.

## UNFI — the second read agreed with the first, both wrong-sided but immaterial

Panel consensus moved from -4.4 (09-04) to -11.7 (09-07), still Neutral, still aligned.
Preliminary score was identical both times (-8). Actual move was +2.28%, close-to-close
$43.93 → $44.93 — one of the quietest reactions in UNFI's own 8-print history (mean abs
9.69%). Reported adjusted EPS $0.69 (vs $0.61 consensus, a clear beat), revenue $7.64B
(slightly light), first FY2027 guide of $3.00-3.50 adjusted EPS.

Both runs' red-team personas flagged reversal risk in the high-60s-to-70s (the day's
ceiling both times) on the "brand-new CFO's first FY27 guide" catalyst, and in both runs
the specific large-magnitude scenarios on *either* side (June-2026-style guide-miss
sell-off, or a 15-20%+ squeeze on a strong guide) failed to fire. The catalyst both sides
built their case around turned out to matter far less to the stock than either side
modeled — a genuine miscalibration of the "this is a fat-tail event" framing, not a
directional miss, since the Neutral call and the below-expected-move convention both
score this a hit.

## What this pair says about re-panelling

Re-panelling the same event closer to the print did not improve the panel's call here —
if anything it entrenched a bearish lean that turned out wrong on ABM, apparently pulled
by a single shared source the chair itself flagged as a dependency risk. The cheap
single-researcher read, by contrast, picked up the correct signal between the two runs.
With n=1 event this is an anecdote, not a pattern, but it is a directly-comparable,
same-event data point and should be weighted accordingly the next time this happens
(any Routine-schedule gap that causes a re-panel of an already-panelled event).
