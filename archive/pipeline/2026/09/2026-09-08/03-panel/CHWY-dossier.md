## 5. Thesis

**Bull case.** The bar was reset once already in June (FY26 sales cut to $13.40–15.55bn,
active-adds guided to the low end), and CHWY has since de-rated to ~14.6x forward earnings
from ~24x a year ago — most of the multiple risk has already been taken. This season's pet-
category peers all beat and were bought (Petco +8.8–10.3% on a reaffirmed guide, Freshpet
+7.6–8% on a raised guide), evidence the category held up better than feared through the
same window. Management is funding a buyback with a new $600m term loan — a real bid under
the stock at $19–23 — and short interest, while rebuilt to 9.6% of float, sits on a
near-zero borrow cost, i.e. discretionary rather than trapped shorts who would cover fast on
a clean beat-and-hold.

**Bear case.** Consensus revenue ($3.32bn) sits at the very top of management's own
$3.30–3.33bn guided range, so there is no cushion for even a modest miss to read badly. Q2
gross margin was pre-guided to contract y/y (lapping a prior-year pricing benefit plus a
fuel-surcharge headwind), pre-neutralising the margin-beat lever that has carried recent
quarters. Chewy's own history is "beat-then-cut" — Q1 FY26 beat EPS and the stock still fell
~11% on the guide cut — and the underlying demand substrate (per peer read-through) is soft:
IDEXX clinic visits down, Petco flagging dog-adoption weakness persisting into 2027. A large
Class B sponsor stake with a history of selling into strength is a standing source of supply
on any relief rally.

**Base case.** A modestly negative-to-flat print — guide most likely reaffirmed rather than
cut again, given no pre-warning signal, but with too little cushion in the numbers for a
clean beat — producing a realised move in roughly the 6–9% range (below the rich 10.65%
implied), with direction close to a coin flip. That is what the panel actually voted for: a
seven-way split resolving to essentially no edge.

## 6. What flips it

Up: net sales at or above $3.33bn with active customers ≥21.7m and the FY26 sales guide
reaffirmed or raised turns the "bar already reset" bull case into a clean re-rating,
especially against a discretionary, low-cost-to-borrow short base that would cover fast. Down:
a second consecutive FY26 sales-guide trim, or a Q2 print at the low end of the $3.30–3.33bn
range with EBITDA margin below the guided 6.3–6.4%, reproduces the exact "beat-then-cut"
pattern that has produced two of CHWY's six double-digit historical moves. Reversal risk
sits at 59/100 (Med) — the red-team persona itself scored only -8 (near the panel's weakest
magnitude, matched almost exactly by the independent base-rate persona's -9), and its case
was directly contested rather than confirmed by the macro/peers and forensics personas, who
read the same reset-bar evidence as bullish. This is a case where the reversal case did not
win the panel outright — it produced the split, not the consensus.

## 7. Positioning & sentiment

- **Options/skew:** conflicting signals — 30-day put IV sits modestly above call IV (skew
  pays for downside) while call trading volume dominates (flow leans upside), a genuinely
  two-sided setup rather than a one-way positioned book like TTAN's.
- **Short interest/crowding:** ~9.6% of free float, rebuilt ~13% in the week into the print,
  but cost-to-borrow near-zero (~0.46%) with loose availability — discretionary short
  interest, not a trapped position primed for a mechanical squeeze, though it is real
  covering fuel on a clean beat-and-hold.
- **Run-up/momentum:** +15–18% over the 30 days into the print off a $17.40 52-week low, down
  ~26–27% year-to-date. The bounce was fading into event eve (-2.4% same day). Little cushion
  left from the pre-print rally if the print disappoints.
- **Social/retail tone:** thin and event-driven rather than a pump — Reddit mentions rose
  roughly 3x into the print but off a low base, no coordinated promotion or meme-volume
  signature, sentiment readings mixed/neutral across sources. No retail bid large enough to
  reliably absorb a disappointment, but also no crowded-long unwind risk.

## 8. Insider / communication / alt-data findings

No informative insider signal in either direction — all 2026 insider activity traced to
routine 10b5-1 plans or RSU-vest tax withholding, with zero open-market purchases, including
by a director who bought openly in 2024 and has not since despite the stock trading ~45%
lower. That absence is a mild negative tell but weak evidence on its own. The stronger
forensic signal is capital allocation: a previously unlevered Chewy added a $600m term loan
in June specifically to fund an expanded buyback — management borrowing to buy its own stock
at $19–23 is a genuine, verifiable bid under the name. On communication: the CEO has a
demonstrated pattern of telegraphing bad news ahead of prints (he warned publicly about
consumer stress three weeks before the June guide cut) and has made no public appearance or
filing since that cut, with his next scheduled appearance after tomorrow's print — consistent
with, but not proof of, no second cut coming. "Nothing unusual" on the filing cadence is
itself the finding, reported as one rather than treated as silence.

## 9. Analyst & revisions read

Consensus revenue $3.32bn sits at the top of the $3.30–3.33bn company guide; consensus
adjusted EPS $0.36. No Sell ratings among covering analysts; mean price target ~$30.3–30.6,
roughly 30% above spot, though targets have been cut on the way in rather than raised
(Goldman $46→$34, Mizuho $40→$32, Morgan Stanley $42→$37, Rosenblatt initiating Neutral at
$25). That divergence — bullish ratings, falling targets — is itself a signal the sell side
is hedging its own bar. Guidance setup: the FY26 sales range ($13.40–13.55bn) was already cut
once in June on a weakening consumer; holding it through H2 (implying +5.5% to +7.8% growth)
requires no further deterioration in exactly the trend management flagged as worsening.
