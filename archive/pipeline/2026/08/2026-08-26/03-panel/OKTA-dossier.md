## 2. What the market cares about
The print trades on cRPO growth, not EPS. Q1 FY27 guided cRPO to +10% and beat it by 2 points
(+12.2%); Q2 is guided to +11% ($2.505–2.515B). Whether Q2 beats meaningfully and whether the Q3 guide
accelerates past 11% is the entire question — EPS consensus of $0.96 (+5.5% YoY) is a low bar that has
been cleared four straight quarters.

## 5. Thesis
**Bull case:** Okta habitually guides conservatively and beats — Q1 FY27 cRPO beat its own +10% guide
by 2 points, NRR is inflecting up, new products (Agent SSO, GA'd 24 Aug) are 25% of bookings, and the
stock is already ~14–18% off its July high, so a beat has room to run without fighting a crowded long.

**Bear case:** the multiple (~7.2x EV/S, ~33x forward for 9–10% growth) demands more than an in-line
beat; a Q3 cRPO guide held at ~11% (no acceleration) reopens the valuation debate exactly as it did in
the −16.16% (May-25) and −17.64% (Aug-24) prints, and short interest is too thin (3.85% of float) to
supply a mechanical squeeze cushion either way.

**Base case:** a modest cRPO beat with an unchanged-to-slightly-better Q3 guide, landing inside the
implied move with no decisive directional resolution.

## 6. What flips it
Red-Team's strongest reversal case: an in-line beat plus a Q3 cRPO guide held near 11% (no
acceleration) against a rich multiple — the same failure mode behind two of the last eight prints. It
would be broken by Q2 cRPO printing ~13%+ with a Q3 guide of 12%+, FY27 revenue raised toward $3.23B+,
and a quantified AI-agent/Auth0 bookings contribution with NRR reaching 108%. This case shares its core
logic with Fundamental's and Options & Positioning's independent −18 scores — three personas
converging from different lenses. It does not overturn the panel: it is answered symmetrically by
Sentiment/Base-Rates/Macro's independent +8 to +12 reads built on de-risked positioning and Okta's own
beat-the-guide history.

- **Short interest/crowding:** thin — 3.85% of shares outstanding, ~2.3 days to cover; no mechanical

- **Run-up/momentum:** stock is +70% YTD but ~14–18% off its 15 July four-year high of $157, i.e.
  already partially de-risked into the print rather than freshly crowded.
- **Social/retail tone:** TipRanks Crowd Wisdom shows retail holdings declining 0.8%/7d and 2.6%/30d —
  retail is de-risking, not piling in; no coordinated meme/pump pattern found.

## 8. Insider / communication / alt-data findings
Forensics found nothing unusual: all insider sales trace to pre-scheduled 10b5-1 plans adopted months
earlier, with no discretionary or defensive selling. Guidance language is verbatim-stable quarter to
quarter, and no KPI has been dropped or redefined. The $200M all-cash Permiso Security acquisition,
announced 27 days pre-print, is an offensive capital-allocation move — not the pattern of a management
bracing for a miss. Corroborating counter-signal: $100k+ ACV customer count grew only 6% YoY versus 11%
revenue growth, meaning growth is increasingly expansion/price-led rather than new-logo-led.
