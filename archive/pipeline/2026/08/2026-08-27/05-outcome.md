## AFRM — hit, but the close-to-close number hides a violent round trip

GAAP EPS $4.62 vs. ~$0.33–0.35 consensus, but the beat is almost entirely a one-time
$1.45B tax valuation-allowance release, not operating strength — adjusted operating
margin of 30% is the cleaner read. Revenue $1,165.96M beat consensus by ~3.3%. Q1
FY2027 revenue guided to $1.19–1.22B, in line to modestly ahead of expectations. Shares
gapped up 11% at Friday's open, ran as high as +16.7% intraday, then gave essentially
all of it back to close +0.35%. The Neutral call scores a hit under the below-expected-
move convention (0.35% is nowhere close to the 10.8% expected move), but it undershoots
even the *low* end of its own 5.4–22.9% band — the panel correctly avoided a directional
call but sized the possible range too wide for what actually happened. The red-team's
specific reversal mechanism (a credit-provision-driven miss, repeating Feb 2026) did not
fire; there was no sign of deteriorating credit quality in what actually moved the
stock. Stage-2 preliminary (−18) called the wrong direction, though on a move this close
to zero that verdict carries little information.

direction, breaking a streak in which the cheap preliminary read had gone 3-for-3
against a hedging panel (WOLF, OKTA, DLTR) — a reminder that the earlier "prelim beats
panel" pattern was a small sample, not a law.

## Where the process failed

**Both panelled names this run round-tripped hard intraday, and the panel's magnitude
sizing missed on both sides of the same coin.** AFRM undershot its own band on the low
side (called too wide a range for a move that ended up near zero); ESTC blew through the
top of the expected move entirely. Two panelled names, two different magnitude failure
modes — this is not yet a directional bias in the bands, but it is a second and third
data point (after OKTA/DLTR on 08-26) that the band width itself may not be well
calibrated to how these names actually trade around a print.

**The preliminary read went 3-for-3 on the non-panelled names again (S, MRVL, RBRK),
all on names where fundamentals and price reaction diverged (MRVL and RBRK both beat and
raised, and both still fell double digits) — but it went 0-for-2 on the two panelled
names, both by wide margins (AFRM's call was directionally too close to matter; ESTC was
a clean miss).** Combined with the certainty-tier note below, the pattern so far is not
"prelim beats panel" so much as "prelim and panel are both roughly coin-flip accurate on
direction, and the earlier three-run streak for prelim was small-sample noise."

**Certainty tiering keeps running backwards.** ESTC was tagged High-certainty and
missed; AFRM was tagged Med-certainty and hit (on a razor-thin move). Across all
panelled calls scored to date, High-certainty names (OKTA miss, DLTR hit, ESTC miss — 1
of 3) are hitting *less* often than Med-certainty names (WOLF hit, AFRM hit — 2 of 2).
The sample is still small, but the direction of the effect has not flipped once since it
was first flagged, and that is worth taking seriously rather than waiting for a larger n
to explain it away.
