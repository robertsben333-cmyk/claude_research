## 2. What the market cares about

The Q1 FY27 (September-quarter) revenue guide, not the Q4 print itself. Consensus Q4
EPS ($3.85) and revenue (~$1.27–1.28B) sit at or above the top of Fabrinet's own
$3.72–3.87 / $1.25–1.29B guidance — a beat is close to already required. FN has said
demand for its 800G/1.6T datacom modules "far exceeded what we were able to ship" and
is capacity-constrained (Building 10 ramping through FY27), so the real question is
whether the forward guide shows the supply constraint easing toward the ~$1.40B level
the recent rally implies, or merely holding at the ~$1.33–1.36B run-rate the FY27
Street number implies.

## 5. Thesis

**Bull case:** FN is supply-constrained, not demand-constrained, on exactly the
800G/1.6T modules the sector's other prints (Lumentum, Coherent) show accelerating
demand for. Building 10 capacity comes online through FY27; a guide that shows the
constraint easing sends the stock through its implied band, as it has in 4 of the last
5 August prints.

**Bear case:** The stock already ran ~43% in three weeks pricing in the sector's good
news (Lumentum's 11 Aug beat). Consensus EPS sits at the top of guidance against a thin
2.64% average four-quarter surprise. Gross margin (~12.1%) has FX pressure flagged as
persisting into Q4. The immediately preceding quarter (4 May 2026) beat on both lines
and still sold off ~9–13%. Needham cut its target from $800 to $650 on 22 July; Zacks
Rank is #4 (Sell).

**Base case:** An in-line-to-modest beat on Q4 with a guide that neither confirms nor
denies the bull thesis — the market has to decide with incomplete information, which is
consistent with a High-reversal-risk, no-net-directional-edge outcome either way.

## 6. What flips it

Per the red-team verdict: the reversal case against a bearish read is an FQ1 FY27
revenue guide at or above ~$1.40B with gross margin held flat-to-up — that "would break"
the bear case outright. Conversely (per multiple personas), a guide that merely repeats
the ~$1.33–1.36B run-rate the FY27 Street number implies, with continued FX/margin
pressure, breaks the bull case. The panel could not resolve this in advance because it
is the exact information the print will reveal — hence Neutral, not a coin-flip lean.
