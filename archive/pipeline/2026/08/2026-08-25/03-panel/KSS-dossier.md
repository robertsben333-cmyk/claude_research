## 5. Thesis

**Bull case:** the compare is easy (Street models flat-to-down EPS off a Q2 FY25 that
itself missed), Kohl's has beaten four straight quarters, and ~$190M of un-guided
tariff refunds sit against a $2.0B market cap. Layer on ~27.6% of float short at ~9.2–
9.5 days to cover and the mechanism that produced +42.5%, +24.0% and +20.6% moves in
the last four prints is still loaded.

**Bear case:** the stock is already +36% off its pre-Q1 close, trading at/above the
average sell-side price target with JPMorgan's raised target ($17) still below spot,
Zacks Earnings ESP at 0.00%, and all 14 ratings at Hold. This week's retail tape has
punished in-line-to-good prints outright (Walmart beat and raised guidance and still
fell ~9%, its worst reaction in ten quarters; Dick's missed and fell ~26–28%) — a merely
reaffirmed FY range, the Q1 playbook, is the exact configuration that has sunk this
stock twice in eight quarters.

**Base case:** an in-line quarter with reaffirmed (not raised) guidance, leaving the
squeeze setup and the raised bar to roughly offset — consistent with the panel's own
near-zero consensus and the wide, right-skewed unsigned band rather than a clean
directional call.
