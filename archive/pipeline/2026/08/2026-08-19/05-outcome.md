## What the process got right

- **The Neutral / No Edge call on WOLF was defensible.** The realised move (9.4%) landed
  comfortably inside the panel's 7.7–24.6% band and below the 15.4% unsigned expected
  move, and the panel's own stated reason for hedging — a genuinely two-sided setup
  between deteriorating fundamentals and an extreme short base — was correct: both
  forces were live, and this time the fundamentals side won. Scored as a hit under the
  ledger's Neutral convention.
- **Three of four preliminary direction scores called the closing sign correctly**
  (WOLF −20, BABA +10, HOV −15), including on HOV's thin-evidence dossier and on BABA's
  name where the intraday tape moved against the score before reversing.
- **The intraday-reversal instruction in the skill paid off twice.** Both BABA and HOV
  gapped or dipped materially against their eventual close (BABA down 4–5% intraday
  before closing +1.26%; HOV down as much as 15.2% intraday before closing −7.47%). A
  close-to-close-only number would have obscured how much each stock actually moved
  against the position at some point in the session — worth carrying into the panel
  process as a magnitude sanity check, not just a footnote.

## What the process got wrong or should flag

- **The panel added no visible value over the single deep researcher on the one name
  it saw.** WOLF's panel produced "no edge" while its own stage-2 preliminary score
  (−20) called the actual direction. This is a sample of one — nowhere near enough to
  conclude the seven-persona stage isn't earning its cost — but it is the first data
  point in the ledger for exactly the question stage 4 exists to ask, and it does not
  flatter the expensive stage. Needs several dozen more panelled names before the
  panel-vs-preliminary comparison means anything either way.
- **BILL's preliminary score (+10) called the wrong sign on a genuine beat.** The
  magnitude was tiny (−0.65%), so this reads more like noise than a real miss, but it
  is a reminder that a mild positive preliminary lean does not reliably predict even
  the sign of the reaction when the print is a clean beat that the market had already
  priced in.
- **Certainty tiering is not yet testable.** This run only produced one panelled call,
  at Med certainty. High and Low tiers have zero calls in the ledger so far — the
  "do High-certainty calls hit more than Low-certainty ones" question cannot be
  answered from one data point in one tier. Flagging so it isn't silently skipped in
  future summaries.
- **Stage 2's dropped names (COTY, FUTU) are simply absent from this scorecard**, per
  the day's own degradation log — they were never researched, so there is nothing to
  score. Not a scoring failure, just a reminder that this day's sample is 4 names, not
