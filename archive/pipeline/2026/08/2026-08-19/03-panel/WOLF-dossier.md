## 5. Thesis

**Bull case:** an ~80%-of-float short base (24.5M shares, 5.81 days to cover) is fully
loaded into a stock already down ~20% in the two sessions before the print. The identical
setup resolved violently upward in May on a 28% revenue miss, driven by AI-datacenter
narrative (LITEON 800VDC partnership, Aug 6) rather than the P&L. A merely-not-worse
quarter with any credible FY27 framing likely forces covering.

**Bear case:** revenue has now declined for three consecutive quarters ($197M → $168.5M
→ $150.2M), non-GAAP gross margin is still negative (−20.6%), Q4 is guided flat-to-down
($140–160M), and this is the first fiscal-year-end print with no FY2027 frame yet
offered. Cash burn (TTM FCF ≈ −$775M against $1.16B cash and $1.72B debt) means the
"beat the whisper" framing this stock has traded on since emergence has a shrinking
runway.

**Base case:** an in-line, unremarkable Q4 with a Q1 FY27 guide that neither confirms nor
kills the AI-datacenter inflection — the panel's own consensus — leaving the short base
and the fundamental deterioration to fight to a standstill, consistent with the wide,
right-skewed unsigned band rather than a clean directional call.

## 6. What flips it

The red-team case (specific, sourced, and weighted 1.5× per the synthesis rules) is a
covering squeeze into the ~80%-of-float short base, mirroring May. It is offset by a
symmetric downside case the same persona names: a realistic FY2027 outlook that keeps
gross margin negative on a sub-$600M revenue run-rate, or any new equity/ATM raise
announced alongside the print, would hand shorts a fresh fundamental catalyst rather than
a reason to cover — and ~24M shares are already registered for resale (Renesas stake,
2031 converts, pre-funded warrants), a latent supply overhang on any rally. Both personas
who found upside (Options, Red-Team) and the five who leaned down converge on the same
answer: the FY2027 guide, not the Q4 print itself, is the actual catalyst.
