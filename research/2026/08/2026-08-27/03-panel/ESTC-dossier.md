## 2. What the market cares about

Consensus revenue ($469.5M) and the FY2027 revenue/EPS guide sit at the literal midpoint
of management's own May guidance — an in-line print is a de facto miss. The metric that
actually trades: CRPO (current remaining performance obligation) growth and cloud/
committed-mix, which needs to hold near or above the Q4 pace of ~20% to sustain the
AI-search narrative that has driven the run-up. A 23-June restructuring (~7% of
headcount, $22-25M of charges) is not embedded in the existing margin guide, making an
EPS/margin beat the more probable — but lower-quality — positive surprise.

## 5. Thesis

**Bull case:** Six straight quarters of revenue-guide beats, un-modelled restructuring
savings, a closed Deductive AI acquisition (2026-08-24), an OpenAI collaboration, and a
software/AI tape that just re-rated NVDA, CrowdStrike and Dynatrace higher on beat-and-
raise prints. A CRPO re-acceleration above 20% with an FY27 raise could squeeze a
6.7%-short-float stock hard.

**Bear case:** The exact same setup broke the stock on 2026-05-27 — a clean beat, and a
FY27 growth guide of only ~14.6% took it down 26.49%. Even a Buy-rated bull (Rosenblatt)
is on record expecting only an in-line-to-marginal beat this quarter, and spot is already
above the sell-side mean target after a +6.8% same-day pop that no persona could trace to
ESTC-specific news.

**Base case:** A likely-clean quarterly beat with a guide that roughly holds the existing
(decelerating) growth framework — the market has shown twice this year that it will sell
that combination, and the panel's mild net-bearish tilt reflects that base case rather
than a strong conviction call either way.

## 6. What flips it

The red-team case (score -28, reversal risk 72, weighted 1.5x for specificity) is the
sharpest downside driver: the May-2026 beat-and-fall precedent, corroborated
independently by the fundamental and base-rate personas from separate angles (guidance
math and historical base rate respectively) without citing each other. It survives
comfortably as the panel's central risk. What breaks the bear case: Elastic Cloud
re-accelerating above ~28-30% y/y on genuine AI/vector-search consumption alongside an
FY27 revenue raise toward $2.03B+ with no margin walk-down from the Deductive AI deal —
that is the configuration that produced ESTC's +37% and +14.8%/+12.3% historical up-gaps.

## 8. Insider / communication / alt-data findings

No insider buying in either direction through the entire drawdown-to-rally cycle; the
only Form 4 activity found is plan-mandated RSU sell-to-cover with zero information
content — a genuine absence, not a data gap. Communication forensics cut modestly

FY27 guide, with no guidance reaffirmation in the accompanying 8-K, and CEO language

for the opposite interpretation. Alt-data (elevated Aug-14 call volume, ~208 open roles
post-RIF) is thin and only mildly corroborative either way.
