# RBRK — Rubrik, Inc.

**What this print is about.** Rubrik reports fiscal Q2 2027 (quarter ended 31 July 2026) after the close on Thursday 27 August 2026, and it does so as one of the most extended, most crowded long positions in security software. The stock closed at $96.13 on 26 August — up 28.6% in seventeen sessions, up ~80% from its 13 March 2026 low of $53.43, and 8.5% below its all-time closing high of $105.09 set on 13 August — and was indicated $100.30 pre-market on the morning of the print. Between 19 and 26 August at least eleven sell-side firms raised price targets, most of them from the $90–$100 band into $105–$135, with Truist explicitly naming Rubrik a preferred name and explicitly forecasting "a beat-and-raise quarter." Overnight, CrowdStrike and Okta both beat and raised and both gapped higher, pulling the whole cyber tape up with them. The headline numbers are not the event: consensus revenue of $396.41M sits exactly on the guidance midpoint of $395–$397M, and consensus non-GAAP EPS of $0.04 sits exactly at the guide midpoint of $0.03–$0.05. What actually trades is **net-new subscription ARR and the size of the FY27 subscription-ARR guidance raise**, against a full-year guide ($1,854–$1,862M) that mathematically implies sequential net-new ARR *decelerating* from the ~$106M booked in Q1 to roughly $96M a quarter — a bar management has beaten nine times out of nine as a public company, and which the market has now largely paid for in advance. The tension is straightforward: the fundamental setup is good and the guide looks sandbagged; the positioning setup is stretched, the raise is telegraphed, and the options market is pricing a ~13% move.

## 3. The one metric that matters

**Net-new subscription ARR in Q2, and the magnitude of the FY27 subscription-ARR guidance raise.**

Management said it plainly on the Q1 call: reported revenue growth carries "a meaningful headwind" from the run-off of cloud-transition material rights (~$8.5M in Q1, ~$17M for the full year), so **subscription ARR is "the primary top line metric to evaluate our business performance"** ([Q1 FY27 transcript][19]). That is why revenue growth optically halves from 39% in Q1 to a guided 27–28% in Q2 while the business is not actually decelerating anything like that much — a nuance that, if the market forgets it in the first ten minutes after the tape, is itself a source of gap risk in both directions.

**What the market expects, and how I know.** Published sell-side ARR consensus is $1,639.4M ([Zacks via Yahoo][12]). Against Q1 FY27's $1.57B ([Q1 PR][13]), that is only ~$69M of net-new ARR. Here is the sequence of net-new subscription ARR I compute from the company's own quarterly ARR disclosures (Q4 FY25 $1,092.6M → Q1 FY26 ~$1.18B → Q2 FY26 $1.25B → Q3 FY26 $1.35B → Q4 FY26 $1.46B → Q1 FY27 $1.57B; sources [Q4 FY25 PR][20], [Q2 FY26 PR][21], [Q3 FY26 PR][22], [Q4 FY26 PR][15], [Q1 FY27 PR][13]):

| Quarter | Subscription ARR | Approx. net-new ARR |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q4 FY25 | $1,092.6M | >$90M (company: record) |
| Q1 FY26 | ~$1.18B | ~$88M |
| Q2 FY26 | $1.25B (+36%) | ~$71M |
| Q3 FY26 | $1.35B (+34%) | ~$98M |
| Q4 FY26 | $1.46B (+34%) | **$115M (company: record)** |
| Q1 FY27 | $1.57B (+32%) | ~$106M |
| Q2 FY27 consensus | $1,639M (+31%) | **~$69M** |

Two things fall out of this, and they are the crux of the print (both are my inference from company-sourced figures):

1. **The published ARR consensus is a low bar.** ~$69M of net-new would be *flat year over year* against Q2 FY26's ~$71M, in a business whose net-new ARR grew ~30% YoY in Q4 and Q1. Q2 is Rubrik's seasonally softest net-new quarter, but flat YoY would be a genuine break in trend, not a continuation.
2. **The FY27 guide is arithmetically conservative.** The $1,858M midpoint requires only ~$288M of net-new ARR across Q2–Q4 — about $96M a quarter — versus $106M delivered in Q1 alone and $115M in Q4 FY26, in a business where Q4 is by far the strongest quarter. Unless something has broken, the guide has to go up. The question the panel should weigh is not *whether* Rubrik raises but whether it raises enough to justify a stock that has already moved 28%.

**Secondary metrics that will be scrutinised on the call:** subscription NRR (~120% in Q1, and management stressed all expansion vectors are contributing — [transcript][19]); cloud ARR mix (89% of subscription ARR at Q1, $1.39B, +43% — the cloud transition is essentially complete, so the migration tailwind to cloud-ARR growth is ending); identity-resilience ARR (crossed $50M in Q1 after +38% sequential growth — the fastest-growing attach and the one most directly validated by Okta's blowout last night); and any first disclosure of Agent Cloud / Agent Identity / Agent Rewind contribution.

**Balance sheet and dilution — the structural bear point.** Cash and short-term investments $1.75B ($429.4M cash + $1.32B ST investments). Deferred revenue $1.89B ($1.12B current + $766.4M non-current). Non-cancellable RPO ~$2.44B, 53% recognisable within twelve months. Against that sits **$1.15B of 0.00% convertible senior notes due June 2030** (net carrying $1.13B, effective rate 0.19%), which produce a **stockholders' deficit of $(481.3)M**. Guided FY27 weighted-average share count of ~228M versus 203.7M GAAP diluted in Q1 is roughly 12% of creep. There is **no buyback**. ([10-Q][6], [Q1 PR][13])

**Customer concentration.** Two channel partners accounted for 29% and 27% of Q1 revenue (26% and 24% of A/R). These are distributors/resellers, not end customers, so the economic concentration is far lower than it looks — but it is a real operational dependency and it is disclosed. ([10-Q][6])

**Geography.** Americas 72%, EMEA 24%, APAC 4% ([10-Q][6]) — so FX exposure is modest, and the sovereign-cloud opportunity management flagged in Europe and the Middle East is upside rather than base case.

**What changed since the last print (2026-06-04):**
- **Rubrik Forward 2026 (June 9)** — launched Rubrik AI, Autonomous Business Recovery, expanded Identity Resilience, Rubrik Annapurna AI-ready data platform, and GSI partnerships for Agent Cloud including Anthropic ([Yahoo][24]).
- **4 August 2026** — launched **Rubrik Agent Identity**, governing AI-agent access and permissions in real time on top of Okta and Microsoft Entra ID; simultaneously **Wipro launched "Enterprise Resilience as a Service"** embedding Rubrik inside a GSI-managed program ([Simply Wall St][25]).

- Earlier in FY27: Google Cloud SQL protection, Agent Cloud for Gemini Enterprise, Google Workspace data protection ([Rubrik newsroom][28]).

**What is at stake.** At 13.46x EV/revenue and 11.52x forward sales ([StockAnalysis stats][5]) — roughly 10.6x EV to Q1-annualised subscription ARR on my arithmetic — Rubrik is priced as a durable ~30% ARR compounder with a visible path to mid-teens FCF margins. The stock has re-rated ~80% in five months on that thesis. A quarter that shows net-new ARR flattening, or a guide raise that merely trues up the beat, removes the marginal buyer without removing the multiple.

## 9. Bull case / bear case / base case

**Bull case.** The FY27 subscription-ARR guide of $1,854–$1,862M implies only ~$96M of net-new ARR per quarter across Q2–Q4, against $106M delivered in Q1 and a record $115M in Q4 FY26 — arithmetic drawn from the company's own quarterly ARR disclosures ([Q1 PR][13], [Q4 FY26 PR][15]). Published sell-side ARR consensus of $1,639.4M ([Zacks via Yahoo][12]) implies net-new ARR flat year over year, a bar Rubrik has not come close to missing in nine quarters of beats ([MarketBeat][17]). Overnight, CrowdStrike beat its own net-new-ARR guide by ~17% and raised net-new ARR growth by 630bps, and Okta beat and raised on identity — the two most direct demand proxies Rubrik has, both confirming that AI-agent proliferation is converting into budget ([SiliconANGLE][53]). Rubrik's fastest-growing attach, identity resilience, crossed $50M ARR on +38% sequential growth ([transcript][19]), and it shipped Agent Identity and a Wipro GSI partnership in early August ([Simply Wall St][25]). Margins are inflecting — non-GAAP operating income positive since Q3 FY26, LTM sub-ARR contribution margin 8.0% → 13.2%, FCF guided to $293–$303M ([Q1 PR][13], [Q3 FY26 PR][22]). With 8–10% of the float short ([StockAnalysis stats][5], [Fintel][31]) and a 12.9% implied move ([OptionSlam][7]), a genuine blowout plus a $30M+ FY ARR raise takes this through the $106.50 high and squeezes; the precedents are the +22.49% and +27.79% gaps.

**Bear case.** The stock is up 28.5% in seventeen sessions ($74.82 on 27 July → $96.13 on 26 August) and +36.7% over one month versus +2.3% for the S&P ([StockAnalysis history][35], [Zacks via Yahoo][12]), and it opens the day another ~4.3% higher pre-market ([StockAnalysis][4]). Every one of the eleven analyst actions in the eight sessions before the print was an upward target revision, with Truist publicly forecasting "a beat-and-raise quarter" ([TipRanks][14], [Truist via Yahoo][18]) — the good news is not just expected, it is written down. The average target of $107.11–$107.61 sits only ~7% above the pre-market price, so a beat-and-raise merely validates the marks. Positioning is four-to-one long in options open interest ([Barchart P/C][29]) with retail sentiment at 94/100 ([AltIndex][38]). And Rubrik's own record is unforgiving here: the three prints preceded by a >28% one-month run-up returned +20.44%, +0.67% and −4.66%, and the most recent — Q1 FY27 on 4 June, a beat on every metric with every FY27 guided line raised — still fell 4.66% ([Nasdaq closes][9], [Benzinga][59]). Two structural drags compound any disappointment: reported revenue growth halves optically from 39% to a guided 27–28% on the material-rights run-off ([transcript][19]), giving headline-readers a decel story; and cloud ARR at 89% of subscription ARR ([10-Q][6]) means the migration tailwind that flattered cloud-ARR growth to +43% is ending. Underneath, ~$1.07B of unrecognised stock comp and a guided share count moving from 203.7M to ~228M is ~12% dilution with no buyback ([10-Q][6], [Q1 PR][13]).

**Base case.** My central expectation is that Rubrik beats: revenue somewhere around $400–405M against a $396.41M consensus, non-GAAP EPS above the $0.03–$0.05 guide, subscription ARR in the $1.66–1.68B area (net-new ~$90–110M, comfortably above the ~$69M the published consensus implies), and an FY27 subscription-ARR guide lifted to roughly $1.88–1.90B. The evidence for that is the arithmetic conservatism of the standing guide, a nine-for-nine beat record, and last night's CRWD/OKTA confirmation of the demand backdrop. The harder question is what that is worth to a stock that will likely trade near $100 by the close after an ~8% two-day pull-forward on peer results. I think the distribution is genuinely fat-tailed and close to symmetric with a mild negative skew on the *modal* outcome: an in-line-to-good beat with a normal-sized raise most likely produces a flat-to-down session in the −5% to +3% band, echoing 4 June; a large raise (FY ARR ≥ $1.90B) or a first quantified Agent Cloud/identity ARR disclosure produces a +10% to +20% gap; and net-new ARR at or below ~$80M with an unchanged FY guide produces a −12% to −20% gap, echoing 10 September 2025. Historical base rate is 4 up / 5 down with a median absolute move of 8.91% and a mean of 11.73% ([Nasdaq closes][9]).

## 10. What would flip the consensus view

The single most credible reversal of my mildly cautious read: **Rubrik quantifies Agent Cloud / Agent Identity / Agent Rewind as a distinct ARR line for the first time and it is material.** Identity resilience crossed $50M ARR on +38% sequential growth in Q1 ([transcript][19]); if management puts a number on agentic AI resilience — say a combined identity-plus-agent ARR above $100M growing >30% sequentially — with Wipro and Anthropic named as distribution, then Rubrik stops being valued as a 30% backup compounder and starts being valued on a second S-curve, exactly the re-rating Okta got last night for the same narrative (+19% after hours on identity-for-AI-agents). In that case the 28% run-up is not a ceiling, it is the first leg, and $135 (Truist) becomes the anchor rather than the outlier. Concretely, what would flip me: **FY27 subscription-ARR guidance raised to ≥$1.90B (vs $1,854–1,862M now) together with a disclosed AI/identity ARR figure above $100M.** That combination would make the current 12.9% implied move too cheap in the up direction, and I would move to a clear Lean Up.

The mirror-image reversal, which would make me far *more* negative: **Q2 net-new subscription ARR at or below ~$80M with the FY27 ARR guide raised only to the level of the beat.** That would confirm the deceleration the published consensus is implicitly forecasting, strip the "conservative guide" premise out of the bull case, and — on a stock priced at 13.5x EV/sales after a 28% run — reproduce 10 September 2025's −18%.
