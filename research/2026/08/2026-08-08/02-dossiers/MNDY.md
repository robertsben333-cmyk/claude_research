# MNDY — monday.com Ltd.

**What this print is about.** This is not the "high-multiple SaaS name" the triage note described — that framing is stale and I am flagging it as wrong. MNDY has already de-rated ~62% over twelve months [16] and trades at roughly 1.9x EV/revenue and ~9.5x EV/adjusted-FCF on its own FY26 guidance (my calculation from sourced inputs, see Fundamentals). The debate is no longer multiple compression; it is whether the growth decline (33% FY24 → 26% FY25 → 19–20% FY26 guided) stabilises, and whether the July 22 restructuring — a 20% workforce cut, an AI Work Platform pivot, and a raise of FY26 non-GAAP operating margin to ~15% from ~13% [7][8] — is a credible re-acceleration story or a cost story dressed as one. Critically, the company **soft pre-announced** in that same 6-K: it said it expects full-year 2026 results "to be in-line with or above" its Q1 guidance, excluding restructuring charges [7]. That materially de-risks the Q2 print itself. What it does **not** de-risk is the Q3 guide — and MNDY's last four prints show a consistent failure mode of beating the quarter and then being destroyed on forward guidance (-29.8%, -12.3%, -20.8% on three of the last four). Set against that, the stock has already run **+29.8% in eleven sessions** into the event [own calc from 15], short interest sits near 16% of shares outstanding [12], and the peer tape is the best it has been all year, with Atlassian +37.4% on August 6 [21][22]. The event is a high-variance collision between a de-risked quarter, a crowded short, and a very extended entry price.

## 3. The one metric that matters

**It is the Q3 FY26 revenue guide, read as the implied FY26 exit growth rate — with the seats-plus-credits AI monetisation contribution to net-new ARR as the swing qualifier.**

Why, and how I know:
- Every one of MNDY's three double-digit *down* prints in the last four was caused by guidance, not the quarter. 2025-08-11: beat, guided Q3 to $311–313M below the buy-side bar, −29.8% [15][26]. 2025-11-10: beat (revenue +26.2% to $316.9M, EPS $1.16 vs consensus), guided Q4 to $329M, 1.4% below Street, −12.3% [15][27]. 2026-02-09: beat ($333.9M vs $329.7M; EPS $1.04 vs $0.60), guided FY26 to $1,452–1,462M vs $1.48bn Street **and withdrew the $1.8bn FY27 target**, −20.8% [15][18].
- The company has now removed the margin variable from the debate by pre-raising FY26 op margin to ~15% [7]. That was a cost action. The only remaining unknown is demand.
- **What the market expects:** consensus FY26 revenue is $1.47bn [19], effectively the top of the company's $1,466–1,475M band [1]. The Street therefore expects the guide to be at least reaffirmed at the high end. Cantor's field checks into the print were described as "relatively mixed," with the standout positive being **"relative stability in churn"** and no pickup in churn [24][29].
- **The AI qualifier:** management disclosed at Q1 that the new seats-plus-credits model contributed **~10% of net new ARR** [9][25], with agent-driven revenue explicitly excluded as too early. A Q2 figure materially above 10% is the single cleanest proof that the AI pivot monetises; a flat or lower figure reframes the 20% headcount cut as pure cost-out and invites the bear case.

Secondary metrics that will move the stock: **net dollar retention** (110% in Q1, a stated historical high, which management guided to "slightly decline by year-end" after lapping prior pricing actions [1][9]) and **cRPO growth** (see below).

## 7. Forensics

Direct EDGAR review of monday.com's full filing history (CIK 0001845338) [23]:

- **Filer status.** MNDY remains a **foreign private issuer** — it files 6-K and 20-F, not 8-K/10-Q [7][10][23]. There is therefore no 8-K cadence to read, and quarterly disclosure is voluntary in form. Its last 20-F was filed 2026-03-13 [23].
- **Insider transactions — the standout signal.** MNDY has 195 Form 144 filings in its history, **29 of them in 2025**. There have been **zero Form 144 filings in all of 2026**; the most recent is **2025-12-09** [23]. Insider selling stopped completely as the stock collapsed and has not resumed during the 30% rally. *Inference (mine): strongly non-bearish, though absence of selling is weaker evidence than presence of buying.*
- **Form 4 activity.** Only two Form 4s (plus one 4/A) exist in recent history, filed 2026-06-03 and 2026-06-16. The 2026-06-16 filing is CRO **Casey George**: RSU vesting of 1,858 shares (2026-06-12) and a sale of **838 shares at $78.77** on 2026-06-15, explicitly footnoted as **"a mandatory sale to cover taxes associated with the vesting of equity awards"** — i.e. non-discretionary, not 10b5-1 discretionary selling. Residual holding 1,020 shares direct plus 27,873 RSUs [23a]. Twelve Form 3s were filed on 2026-03-18 [23].
- **Anti-dilution actions.** On **2026-07-01** the board cancelled **10,875,000 unissued shares** from the 2021 Plan reserve, describing it as "cancelling automatic 'evergreen' increases… managing shareholder dilution" [10]. Combined with the ~7.27M-share, $553M Q1 buyback [1], management is aggressively shrinking the share count and the equity overhang. Options + RSUs outstanding are just 3,755,167 [10].
- **Pre-announcement signalling.** The 2026-07-22 6-K contains an explicit forward statement — the company "expects full-year 2026 financial results to be in-line with or above its guidance" [7]. Volunteering this 19 days before the print, alongside a margin *raise* but a revenue growth *reaffirmation only*, is the single most important forensic detail in this dossier. *Inference (mine): a company with material Q2 revenue upside would more plausibly have raised the revenue range too; reaffirming the range while raising a cost-driven margin line reads as demand-neutral.*
- **Litigation / restatement.** A **securities fraud class action** is pending, class period **2025-09-17 to 2026-02-06**, alleging concealment of decelerating new-customer growth, weak expansion within existing accounts, longer enterprise sales cycles, and inadequate AI investment, centred on the withdrawn **$1.8bn FY27 revenue target**; lead-plaintiff deadline was 2026-05-11 [18][32]. *This is a live tail risk and a constraint on how promotional management can be about forward targets.* **No auditor change, no restatement, and no going-concern or internal-control issue was found** in the EDGAR record [23].
- **Executive/director changes.** No CEO/CFO departures found. Casey George joined as CRO effective 2025-05-15 [33]. The 2026-08-06 AGM re-elected Eran Zinman, Aviad Eyal and Petra Jenner as Class II directors with 97.7%/95.5%/98.4% approval, and approved the executive compensation policy (98.85% of non-controlling shareholders) — routine, no dissent [23b].
- **Filing-language / tone shift.** Q1 (2026-05-11): headcount to remain "largely flat," AI framed as a productivity tailwind [9]. Q3-to-date (2026-07-22): 20% of the workforce cut, "leaner, more focused operating model," office-space impairments [7]. That is a sharp ten-week tonal escalation. Management insists it is strategic rather than cost-driven and that most savings will be reinvested [8][30] — a claim the Q3 margin guide will either corroborate or contradict.
- **8-K/6-K cadence.** Nothing anomalous. The 2026-08-06 6-K is the AGM result [23b]; 2026-07-17 and 2026-07-02 are proxy documents [10]. No unscheduled disclosure in the blackout window.

## 9. Bull case / bear case / base case

**Bull case.** The company has already told the market the quarter is fine — the July 22 6-K states it expects FY26 to come in "in-line with or above" guidance [7] — and simultaneously raised FY26 non-GAAP operating margin to ~15% from ~13% [7]. cRPO growing 26% against a 18–19% forward revenue guide [1] says the guide is conservative. The >$500K ARR cohort grew 74% and >$100K grew 39% [1]; enterprise is inflecting even as the tail stalls. AI already contributes ~10% of net new ARR before agents are monetised, and AI credits became mandatory in the purchase flow from 2026-05-06 [9][25][28] — Q2 is the first quarter that captures it. Management has retired ~10% of the shares for $553M and cancelled 10.875M plan shares [1][10], while insiders have filed **zero** Form 144s in 2026 [23]. The asset trades at ~1.85x EV/sales and ~9.5x EV/adjusted-FCF on guidance (my calc). Against 16.3% of shares outstanding short and un-covered [10][12], and an Atlassian print two sessions ago that moved +37.4% on exactly this category [22], a reaffirmed-or-raised guide plus a rising AI-ARR number produces a squeeze that clears the +17% implied move.

**Bear case.** The stock has already gone up 29.8% in eleven sessions [15]; the restructuring, the margin raise, the peer beats and the valuation argument are all in the price, and the entry is no longer washed out. MNDY's documented failure mode is precisely this print: beat the quarter, guide the next one 1–3% light, lose 12–30% in a session — three times in the last four quarters [15][18][26][27]. Consensus revenue of $355.55M already sits at the top of the $354–356M guide [3][1], so in-line is a miss. Growth is decelerating relentlessly (33% → 26% → 19–20%), the >10-user customer count grew just **7%** [1], NDR was explicitly guided to decline slightly by year-end [1][9], and management withdrew the $1.8bn FY27 target only two quarters ago [18] — a withdrawal that produced a live securities class action [32]. The July 22 reaffirmation raised only the *cost* line while leaving the revenue range untouched, which is what a company with an in-line-not-better demand picture does. Cutting 20% of staff — including go-to-market — mid-year is a genuine risk to H2 bookings capacity, and the ~$45–55M of charges lands in H2 [7]. The upside-call crowd now on the tape [17] is the fuel for a downside air-pocket, and there is no protective put skew to cushion it [17].

**Base case (mine).** The quarter beats — that is close to pre-announced and the Street knows it. The stock's reaction is decided in the first ten minutes by the Q3 guide and the AI-ARR disclosure. My central expectation is revenue of ~$358–362M (modest beat on $354–356M guided), a Q3 guide around $370–375M, FY26 revenue reaffirmed at or slightly above the top of $1,466–1,475M, non-GAAP op margin confirmed at ~15%, NDR at 109–110%, and AI contribution to net-new ARR disclosed at or slightly above 10%. That combination is a modest positive, and against a 16% short base with a supportive sector tape it should be enough for a positive reaction — but the 29.8% run-up means much of it is discounted, and I would expect a realised move well inside the ±17% implied on that path. The distribution is genuinely fat-tailed and asymmetric in *shape*: the downside scenario (guide below ~$370M, or a decelerating AI-ARR contribution) is lower-probability but much larger in magnitude, given the −29.8%/−20.8% precedents and an extended entry price. Net: a modest directional lean up, with high reversal risk and low conviction in the magnitude.

## 10. What would flip the consensus view

The single most credible reversal is a **Q3 revenue guide with a midpoint below roughly $368M** — i.e. implying sub-17% year-over-year growth and forcing an unbelievable Q4 to reach the reaffirmed FY26 range — **delivered alongside an AI contribution to net-new ARR that is flat at or below the ~10% disclosed in Q1** [1][9][25].

That combination would convert the entire July 22 restructuring narrative from "strategic AI pivot funded by reinvestment" into "defensive cost-cut in a decelerating business," retroactively validate the class-action allegation that expansion within existing accounts is weakening [32], and strip the reaffirmation of its credibility. In that state, the stock has no valuation floor to lean on because the floor is a multiple of a growth rate that would just have been re-based; the 2026-02-09 analogue (−20.8% on precisely a guidance reset) is the template. A secondary, lower-probability flip in the same direction: NDR printing at or below 107% versus 110% in Q1, which would mean the "slight decline by year-end" arrived a quarter and a half early.

The mirror-image flip — the one that would make the bulls right violently rather than modestly — is a **Q3 guide above ~$378M together with an AI net-new-ARR contribution disclosed at 15%+**. Against 6.9M shares short that have not covered [12] and an ATM straddle at 20% of spot [17], that scenario is the one where the realised move exceeds the implied.
