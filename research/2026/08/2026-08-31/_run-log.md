## Edge hunt — 2026-08-31 bmo — calendar rows are not schedules
- Logged at 2026-08-30 09:12 UTC

Aimco has reported on a liquidation basis since a shareholder vote on 2026-02-06, filed its Q2 10-Q on 2026-08-07 with no accompanying item 2.02 8-K, and has issued no earnings release since 2026-03-02. stockanalysis.com simultaneously shows 'Next Earnings Date: August 31, 2026' and 'Last Reported: August 7, 2026' - an aggregator projecting the pre-liquidation cadence forward.
- The baseline had reported AIV as tier partial on six prior reactions. Those six came from a reporting regime the company had abandoned six months earlier, and priced_in.py presented them as current. That is the same silent-and-flattering failure shape as the five bugs in backtest/FINDINGS.md: no error, no gap, just a number that looks like evidence.
- Fixed by adding event_plausibility to priced_in.py: compare the gap since the last real earnings filing against the median gap between the ones before it. Outside 0.5x to 1.8x the cadence the event is marked suspect, and a suspect event zeroes the usable history so the tier drops.

THREE of the four partial names are suspect: AIV 182 days against a 95-day cadence, CHRN 195 against 80, FRGT 375 against 93. The day is not 1 full / 4 partial / 7 thin as the heartbeat recorded. It is 1 full (SAIC, 91 days against a 77-day cadence, fits), 1 partial (SY), 10 thin.
- Nine of twelve rows on this calendar day carried time-not-supplied, and at least three of them are stale projections rather than unconfirmed timings. Nothing upstream in this repo checks that - stage 0 inherits the same calendar. Worth carrying into earnings-universe.
