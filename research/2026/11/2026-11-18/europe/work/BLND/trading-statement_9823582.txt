 British Land Company PLC has reported a strong first half for the six months ended 30 September 2026, with expected underlying EPS of 16.1p, a 5% year-on-year increase, and like-for-like net rental growth of 12%.  British Land Co PLC
 15 October 2026
 The British Land Company PLC ("British Land") 
 British Land trading update 
 Strong momentum continues in campuses and retail parks 
 15 October 2026 
 British Land issues an unaudited trading update for the six-month period ended 30 September 2026, ahead of its half year (HY27) results announcement scheduled for 18 November 2026. 
 Jo McNamara, Chief Executive, said: “Since joining British Land in mid-September, my views on the quality of the portfolio, the expertise of our teams and the opportunities ahead of us have been reinforced and this is reflected in a strong first half. 
 Despite the ongoing macroeconomic volatility, demand for our space has driven double-digit like-for-like net rental income growth and an excellent leasing performance across the portfolio. Our campus developments continue to lease up at record rents, with 1 Broadgate and One Triton Square now fully let and a further 119,000 sq ft under offer at 2 Finsbury Avenue. Meanwhile, our retail parks remain effectively full, with leasing continuing ahead of previous passing rents. 
 I am focused on building on this momentum to capture demand and drive rents forward, while ensuring the business is as efficient as possible and allocating capital to deliver the best returns. We have a strong platform from which to deliver sustainable earnings growth and attractive, income focused returns for shareholders.” 
 Continued delivery across both core markets 
 Expect HY27 Underlying EPS of 16.1p, up 5% year on year 
 HY27 like-for-like net rental growth of 12% 
 Values up +1.5% driven by ERV growth of +3.0% across the portfolio 
 £247m of assets sold at a net initial yield of 4.7%, broadly in line with book value, with a further £202m under offer 
 Reiterating FY27 Underlying EPS guidance of at least 30.5p (6% growth year on year) 
 Across campuses, 78 deals covering 689,000 sq ft were completed during the first half, at rents 14.8% ahead of ERV. Occupier demand remains strong for the best quality space in core locations, and development leasing has continued to progress well. At 2 Finsbury Avenue, following the final agreement with Citadel to take 33% of the building, a further 119,000 sq ft is under offer, bringing the scheme to 52% let or under offer. At Broadgate Tower, a further 6 deals across 49,000 sq ft have completed, taking the scheme to 62% let and at Regent’s Place, One Triton Square is now fully let. 
 Retail park leasing remained strong, with 438,000 sq ft leased, at rents 6.8% ahead of ERV and 9.6% ahead of previous passing rents. The portfolio remains virtually full with occupancy at 99%, with a further 533,000 sq ft under offer, demonstrating sustained retailer demand for the format. 
 For the six-month period ended 30 September 2026, subject to external review, the Group expects: 
 Earnings 
 −          Underlying Profit of £166m (HY26: £155m) and Underlying EPS of 16.1p (HY26: 15.4p). This reflects like-for-like net rental growth of 12%, development lease-up and cost efficiencies, which more than offset the impact of lower surrender premiums and higher interest rates. 
 Portfolio valuation and balance sheet 
 −          Six-month values +1.5%, with Campuses +1.6% and Retail & London Urban Logistics +1.2%, of which retail parks +2.4%. 
 −          ERV growth of +3.0%, with Campuses +3.9% and Retail & London Urban Logistics +2.0%, of which retail parks +2.7%. 
 −          NEY +3 bps at 6.1%. 
 −          EPRA Net Tangible Assets per share of 599p (FY26: 590p) and a TAR of 3.4% for the six months. 
 −          Loan to value c.39.6% (FY26: 39.2%) and Group Net Debt to EBITDA c.7.0x (FY26: 7.7x). 
 Operational update 
 −          1.4m sq ft of leasing across the portfolio, 10.4% ahead of ERV and 7.9% ahead of previous passing rent, with a further 1.1m sq ft under offer, 11.6% ahead of ERV. 
 −          Like-for-like net rental growth of 12%. 
 Campuses 
 −          689k sq ft of leasing, 14.8% ahead of ERV and 18.3% ahead of previous passing rent, with a further 396k sq ft under offer, 11.2% ahead of ERV.   
 −          Occupancy of 95% 1 , and 93% occupancy on an EPRA basis. 
 −          Like-for-like net rental growth of 25%, reflecting strong occupational fundamentals and lease up of previously vacant space. 
 ​ ​ 1 Excluding developments completed in the previous 12 months . 
 Retail and London Urban Logistics 
 −          739k sq ft of leasing, 7.3% ahead of ERV and 5.3% ahead of previous passing rent, with deals specifically on retail parks 9.6 % ahead of previous passing rents. 
 −          Further 674k sq ft under offer, 12.1% ahead of ERV and 9.0% ahead of previous passing rent.   
 −          Occupancy of 98%, including 99% on retail parks, and 97% occupancy on an EPRA basis. 
 −          Like-for-like net rental growth of 2%, reflecting virtually full retail park occupancy and burn off of historical overrent. 
 Capital recycling 
 −       £247m of assets sold, at a net initial yield of 4.7%, broadly in line with book value, with a further £202m under offer. 
 −     Acquisition of Telford Bridge Retail Park for £30m at a topped-up NIY of 8.3%. 
 −     Acquisition of Life Science REIT completed on 20 April 2026. 
 Outlook 
 −          Reiterate guidance for FY27 Underlying EPS of at least 30.5p and expectation for 3-6% p.a. EPS growth over the medium term. 
 −          Reiterate guidance for future ERV growth of 3-5% p.a. across the portfolio.

 Notes to editors 
 About British Land 
 British Land is a UK commercial property company focused on real estate sectors with the strongest operational fundamentals: London campuses and retail parks. We own or manage a portfolio valued at £15.8bn (British Land share: £10.1bn) as at 31 March 2026. 
 Our purpose is to create and manage Places People Prefer – outstanding places that deliver positive outcomes for all our stakeholders on a long term, sustainable basis. We do this by leveraging our best in class platform and proven expertise in development, repositioning and active asset management. 
 Read more about us at www.britishland.com .
