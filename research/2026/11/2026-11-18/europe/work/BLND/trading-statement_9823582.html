British Land Company PLC has reported a strong first half for the six months ended 30 September 2026, with expected underlying EPS of 16.1p, a 5% year-on-year increase, and like-for-like net rental growth of 12%. The company's portfolio values increased by 1.5%, driven by ERV growth of 3.0%, and £247 million of assets were sold at a net initial yield of 4.7%, with a further £202 million under offer. Campuses saw significant leasing activity with rents 14.8% ahead of ERV, while retail parks maintained high occupancy at 99% with leasing 6.8% ahead of ERV. The company reiterates its full-year FY27 underlying EPS guidance of at least 30.5p.
