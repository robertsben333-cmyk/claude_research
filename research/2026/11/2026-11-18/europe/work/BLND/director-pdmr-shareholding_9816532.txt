 British Land Company PLC announced that Non-Executive Directors Mark Aedy and Raj Shah received ordinary shares on October 9, 2026, as full satisfaction of their quarterly fees. Mark Aedy was allotted 2,411 shares and Raj Shah received 2,777 shares, with each share valued at 392.32 pence. These transactions occurred outside of a trading venue and are in compliance with UK Market Abuse Regulation.
 Disclaimer* 
 British Land Co PLC
 09 October 2026
 Non-Executive Directors' fees 
 The British Land Company   PLC (“the Company”) announces that the Non-Executive Directors listed below were allotted ordinary shares of 25 pence each in the Company at a price of 392.32 pence per share on 9 October 2026 :     
 Non-Executive Directors   
 Shares     
 Mark Aedy 
 2,411 
 Raj Shah 
 2,777 
 The allotment of shares is pursuant to their instruction to receive shares in full satisfaction of their quarterly Non-Executive Director fees. 
 The transactions referred to above took place outside a trading venue.   
 This notification is made in accordance with the UK Market Abuse Regulation. The Identification Code for ordinary shares of 25 pence each in the Company is GB0001367019.
