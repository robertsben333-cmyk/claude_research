 On October 14, 2026, executive directors and persons discharging managerial responsibilities at British Land Company PLC, including the Chief Financial Officer, purchased ordinary shares at 409.4 pence each and received corresponding matching shares under the Company's Share Incentive Plan. Each individual acquired 37 partnership shares, and was awarded 37 matching shares, with the matching shares being awarded outside a trading venue at no cost. This transaction, involving the purchase of ordinary shares of 25 pence each, is an initial notification made in accordance with UK MAR.
