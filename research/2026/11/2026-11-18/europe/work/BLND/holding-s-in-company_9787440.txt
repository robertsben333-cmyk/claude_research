 Janus Henderson Group Ltd has crossed a notification threshold for British Land Company PLC, now holding a total of 5.87% of voting rights, comprising 5.06% attached to shares and 0.81% through financial instruments like CFDs. This increase, which occurred on September 18, 2026, resulted in Janus Henderson Investors UK Limited holding 5.81% and Janus Henderson Investors US LLC holding 0.027% of the voting rights. The disclosure was made on September 22, 2026, following an increase in voting rights attached to shares.
