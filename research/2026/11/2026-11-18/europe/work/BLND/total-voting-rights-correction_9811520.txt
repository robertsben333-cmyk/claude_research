 The British Land Company PLC has corrected a previous announcement regarding its issued share capital. As of September 30, 2026, the correct issued share capital is 1,036,448,806 ordinary shares, not 1,036,448,806 as initially stated, with 11,266,245 shares held in treasury. Crucially, the total number of voting rights remains unchanged at 1,025,182,561, which shareholders should use for their notification calculations under FCA rules.
 Disclaimer* 
 British Land Co PLC
 07 October 2026
 Voting Rights and Capital Correction 
 In conformity with the FCA’s Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rules’ ongoing disclosure requirements for issuers, The British Land Company PLC (the ‘Company’) notifies the market of the following. 
 The announcement released on 30 September 2026 incorrectly stated the Company's issued share capital as 1,036,488,806 ordinary shares. The correct issued share capital as at 30 September 2026 was 1,036,448,806 ordinary shares. 
 Accordingly, as at 30 September 2026, the Company's issued share capital comprised 1,036,448,806 ordinary shares of 25 pence each, of which 11,266,245 shares were held in treasury. 
 The total number of voting rights in the Company remains unchanged at 1,025,182,561, as previously announced. 
 The above figure of 1,025,182,561 may be used by shareholders and others for the calculations by which they will determine if they are required to notify the Company and the FCA of the percentage of voting rights they hold, or a change in this percentage, under the FCA’s Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rules.
