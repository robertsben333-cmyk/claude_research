ABOUT US

PFISTERER is a globally leading and independent technology company with head offices in Winterbach near Stuttgart, Germany. We develop, produce and sell technology for insulating and connecting electrical conductors for power grid interfaces - from the generation and transmission to the distribution of electrical energy. With state-of-the-art production facilities, high quality standards, and technological expertise, we contribute to a safe and reliable energy supply for the future.
