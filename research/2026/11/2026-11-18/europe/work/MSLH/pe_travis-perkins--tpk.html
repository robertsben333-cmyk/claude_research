Travis Perkins: third quarter trading update

Summary by AI BETAClose X

Travis Perkins plc reported a 1.6% revenue decline in the third quarter of 2026, reflecting subdued UK construction output and persistent macroeconomic uncertainty, with Merchanting revenue down 2.6% and Toolstation Benelux revenue down 3.8%, though Toolstation UK saw 3.7% growth. The company is managing its transaction mix to protect profitability and is focused on passing through input inflation. Despite challenging market conditions, Travis Perkins is implementing measures to enhance operational performance and free cash flow generation, with plans for further store openings in 2027 for Toolstation UK.
