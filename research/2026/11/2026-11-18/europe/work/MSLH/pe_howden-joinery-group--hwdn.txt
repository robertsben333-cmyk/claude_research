International sales 3 in local currency increased by 6.2% in Periods 7 to 11 (10.2% ahead excluding the impact of the planned French depot closures 2 ) as customer appreciation of our differentiated in-stock, trade model continued to increase. We remain on track with our outlook for 2026 and expect to deliver Group underlying profit before tax in line with current market expectations 4 . Our on-going productivity and efficiency programmes are supporting competitive pricing across all our kitchen and joinery categories and are helping to mitigate inflationary pressures. About Howden Joinery Group plc Howdens is the UK’s number one trade kitchen supplier. The Company sells kitchens and joinery products to trade customers, primarily local builders, through 893 depots in the UK and 82 depots in France, Belgium and the Republic of Ireland. In 2025, the Group reported revenues of £2.4 billion and profit before tax of £344.9 million. Howdens is a proud UK-based manufacturer, with a significant proportion of its kitchen and joinery ranges manufactured in-house at its two principal factories in Runcorn, Cheshire, and Howden, East Yorkshire. In June 2026, the Group acquired DIY Kitchens, a vertically integrated business which sells its products exclusively on-line, principally to end users looking to self-manage the purchase of their kitchen. DIY’s on-line self-service model focussed on non-trade customers is quite distinct from Howden Trade’s relationship based full-service trade only model and operationally it is managed independently of Howden Trade. 2.
