Convocation of the Extraordinary General Meeting of Cyfrowy Polsat S.A. on December 14, 2026 and draft of resolutions

Date
16 Nov 2026

The Management Board of Cyfrowy Polsat S.A. (the “Company”), in connection with the request to convene an extraordinary general meeting and place certain matters on the agenda of this meeting, submitted by a shareholder representing at least one-twentieth of the share capital, acting pursuant to Article 400 §1 and 2 of the Code of Commercial Companies in connection with the Articles 398 and 399 § 1 in conjunction with Articles 402(1) and 402(2) of the Commercial Companies Code (the CCC”) and in accordance with the provisions of Article 24 Section 2 of the Company’s Articles of Association, convenes the Extraordinary General Meeting of the Company, which will be held on December 14, 2026 at  12:00 p.m. CET in Warsaw, in the registered office of the Company at ul. Łubinowa 4a.

Agenda of the the Extraordinary General Meeting incorporating the shareholder’s request and expanded to include an additional items, no. 7 and 8, on the agenda, as proposed by the Company:

1. Opening of the Extraordinary General Meeting.
2. Election of the Chairperson of the Extraordinary General Meeting.
3. Confirmation of the proper convening of the Extraordinary General Meeting and its capacity to adopt resolutions.
4. Determination of the number of members of the Supervisory Board.
5. Changes to the composition of the Supervisory Board
6. Amendments to the Company’s Articles of Association.
7. Adoption of a resolution regarding the approval of the consolidated text of the Company’s Articles of Association.
8. Adoption of a resolution regarding the approval of a gender balance policy.
9. Adoption of a resolution regarding the coverage of costs associated with convening and holding the Extraordinary General Meeting.
10.Closing of the Extraordinary General Meeting.

The Company’s Management Board further announces that, in connection with the request to convene an Extraordinary General Meeting submitted on November 3, 2026, by a shareholder representing at least one-twentieth of the share capital, which includes on the agenda an item concerning changes in the composition of the Company’s Supervisory Board, the shareholder REDDEV INVESTMENTS LIMITED, in response to the Company’s statement dated November 5, 2026, nominated Mr. Bartosz Krużewski as a candidate for membership on the Company’s Supervisory Board on November 12, 2026.

To enable shareholders to review the qualifications and professional experience of the nominated candidate, the Company is attaching the candidate’s professional resume, received from the shareholder, to this current report.

The Management Board attaches to this Current Report as follows:

  • Notice of the convening of an Extraordinary General Meeting on December 14, 2026, prepared in accordance with Article 4022 of the Commercial Companies Code (Appendix No. 1);
  • draft resolutions to be considered at the Extraordinary General Meeting of the Company convened for December 14, 2026 (Appendix No. 2);
  • request to convene an Extraordinary General Meeting (Appendix No. 3);
  • a response from the shareholder REDDEV INVESTMENTS LIMITED, indicating a candidate for the Company’s Supervisory Board (Appendix No. 4).

The Company publishes information in accordance with Article 4023 of the Commercial Companies Code on its website at https://grupapolsatplus.pl/pl/lad-korporacyjny/walne-zgromadzenia/materialy.

Legal basis: 

Article 56(1)(2) of the Act on Public Offerings and the Conditions for Introducing Financial Instruments to an Organized Trading System and on Public Companies, in conjunction with § 20(1), items 1 and 2, of the Regulation of the Minister of Finance dated June 6, 2025, on current and periodic information disclosed by issuers of securities and the conditions for recognizing as equivalent information required by the laws of a non-Member State.
