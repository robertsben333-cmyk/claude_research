●   Polsat Plus Group revenue amounted to PLN 3,695m, posting an increase of PLN 104m (+2.9%)
    YoY. The level of total revenue was mainly influenced by:

    −    Higher retail revenue by PLN 52m (+2.9% YoY), driven by very good sales of
         telecommunications services and higher ARPU, particularly in the B2C contract customer
         segment;

    −    Increase in other revenue by PLN 51m (+24.4% YoY), primarily due to higher revenue from
         the sale of hydrogen buses, reflecting a greater volume of deliveries completed in Q2’26;

    −    Higher revenue from sale of equipment by PLN 13m (+3.4% YoY), reflecting stronger demand
         for more advanced and higher-priced smartphone models, despite lower sales volumes. The
         margin on handset sales amounted to PLN 69.2m and was lower by PLN 16.8m YoY;

    −    Stable wholesale revenue, supported by higher revenues from cable and satellite TV
         operators, as well as roaming services, which offset lower advertising and sponsorship
         revenues;

    −    Lower energy revenue by PLN 10m (-3.3% YoY), mainly as a result of a lower volume of
         generated electricity. The decline in generation was due to unfavourable conditions in the
         biomass market and weaker weather conditions.
●   Group costs amounted to PLN 3,280m and increased YoY by PLN 97m (+3.1%) YoY. Their level
    was mainly influenced by the following factors:

    −    Increase in other costs by PLN 71m (+36.1% YoY), mainly as a result of higher costs of
         hydrogen bus sales, resulting from a higher volume of deliveries executed in Q2’26;

    −    Higher cost of energy sold by PLN 36m (+16.0% YoY), primarily driven by the commencement
         of depreciation of the Drzeżewo wind farm following the receipt of its operating permit at the end
         of Q1’26, as well as higher cost of biomass feedstock;

    −    Higher cost of equipment sold by PLN 30m (+9.9% YoY), partly due to higher handset prices;

    −    Higher salaries and employee-related costs by PLN 25m (+7.9% YoY), due, among others,
         to a change in the method of recognising part of remuneration provisions since Q1’26, resulting
         in a more even distribution of these costs throughout the year and eliminating their concentration
         in Q4;

    −    Higher technical costs and cost of settlements with telecommunication operators by
         PLN 18m (+2.0% YoY), primarily due to the recognition of higher roaming-related costs and
         higher costs of wholesale access to fixed-line networks of other operators associated with very
         good sales of Internet services;

    −    Lower distribution, marketing, customer relation management and retention costs by PLN
         44m (-15.0% YoY), mainly due to the normalisation of marketing expenditure following the
         launch of the new multiplay offering in Q2’25, as well as lower distribution and logistics costs
         following the discontinuation of activities related to the sale of photovoltaic panels;

    −    Lower content costs by PLN 16m (-3.0% YoY), primarily driven by lower internal production
         costs and sports licenses expenses, resulting in part from a different sports broadcasting
         schedule.
●   Adjusted EBITDA2 amounted to PLN 853m, up PLN 30m YoY (+3.6% YoY), with a margin of
    23.1%. EBITDA growth was generated in the core business segments – the B2C and B2B services
    segment and the media segment, and was primarily driven by higher retail revenue, combined with
    maintained cost discipline.
●   EBIT amounted to PLN 443m (+15.3% YoY).

Contract services for B2C customers
       ●    B2C contract customer base at 5,570k (-2.1% YoY). The main reason behind the decrease
            was the declining popularity of the satellite technology as well as the continued process of
            merging contracts under one common contract for the household within our base.
       ●    ARPU per B2C contract customer at PLN 83.5 (+6.5% YoY), as a result of very good sales
            of mobile telephony and both fixed and mobile Internet access services, as well as successful
            upselling of services as part of the multiplay offer.
       ●    Churn at a low level of 7.7% per annum, mainly as a result of the high loyalty of our bundled
            service customers.
       ●    B2C contract services base at 13,369k (+2.3%):

            −      The number of Internet RGUs increased by 208k (+10.6%) YoY, to 2,174k;

            −      The number of mobile telephony RGUs increased by 182k (+2.8%) YoY, to 6,708k;

       −   Pay-TV RGUs decreased by 87k (-1.9% YoY), to 4,487k, mainly as a result of a lower
           number of provided satellite TV services, which is partially compensated by an increasing
           number of TV services offered in online technologies (IPTV/OTT).
   ●   3,039k customers used the multiplay offer (+36k YoY), this translates into a 55% saturation
       of our contract customer base with multiplay services.
Prepaid services
   ●   The prepaid RGU base amounted to 2,197k, recording a decrease of 179k (-7.5%) YoY. The
       main reasons of the decline were:

       −   251k decrease in pre-paid mobile telecommunications services due to the high level of
           market competitiveness in this market segment and the migration of customers to contract-
           based tariffs;

       −   72k increase in prepaid pay-TV services, driven primarily by strong interest in new
           packages introduced in September 2025 on the Polsat Box Go platform and an expanded
           programming offer.
   ●   Prepaid ARPU at a high level of PLN 18.4 (+4.0% YoY), which is the result of expanding the
       pay-TV and streaming offer, encouraging customers to choose higher-value packages.
Contract services for B2B customers
   ●   High contract B2B customers base at 67.3k (-1.0% YoY);
   ●   ARPU per B2B customer increased to PLN 1,588 per month (+2.8% YoY).
Financial results
   ●   Revenue growth was primarily driven by higher retail revenues, supported by very strong sales
       of services and growing ARPU, as well as higher equipment sales;

   ●   Cost discipline, mainly in distribution, marketing, customer relation management and retention,
       partially offset higher technical and employee-related costs;

   ●   In Q2’26, adjusted EBITDA at the level of PLN 617m (+1.9% YoY), primarily driven by growing
       retail revenues and effective cost control.

Energy production
●   Total volume of green electricity produced decreased by 10.9% YoY to 280 GWh, incl.:

    −    132 GWh (-24.3% YoY) from biomass, reflecting lower production volumes following the
         Group’s adjustment to market conditions related to feedstock availability and prices;

    −    126 GWh (+13.9% YoY) from wind farms, following the launch in the second half of last year of
         the Group’s largest wind farm, Drzeżewo, which doubled installed wind capacity;

    −    22 GWh (-25.1% YoY) from photovoltaics, due to less favourable weather conditions than in
         Q2'25 and a greater scale of curtailment by the power grid operator during periods of excess
         renewable energy generation, which particularly affected photovoltaic farms.
●   The execution of strategic investments in the construction of renewable energy sources has been
    completed. As a result, the Group’s total installed capacity in low- and zero-emission sources
    reached 485 MW.
Financial results
    ●    Revenue increased by 16.1% YoY, primarily driven by a higher volume of hydrogen bus
         deliveries;
    ●    Operating costs increased mainly due to higher costs of hydrogen buses sold and higher cost
         of energy sold, driven by increased biomass purchase costs;
    ●    Segment EBITDA remained stable at PLN 65 million (-0.3% YoY).
    ●    Capital expenditure in the green energy segment declined significantly to PLN 31 million
         (-79.4% YoY) following the completion of capital-intensive renewable energy projects delivered
         under the Strategy 2023+.
