Third Quarter Update and Results

Summary by AI BETAClose X

Ithaca Energy PLC reported strong financial and operational results for the nine months ended September 30, 2025, with YTD average production reaching 115 kboe/d and adjusted EBITDAX of $1,501.2 million, significantly up from $758.5 million in the prior year. The company has successfully reduced operating expenditure per barrel to $19.1/boe from $28.9/boe and maintained a strong balance sheet with $1.7 billion in liquidity and a leverage ratio of 0.50x, bolstered by a €450 million bond issuance and a $300 million RBL facility upsizing. Ithaca Energy reaffirms its 2025 outlook and dividend target of $500 million, with an expected production exit rate of approximately 145 kboe/d in Q4 2025, and continues to advance its growth strategy through organic investments and acquisitions, including a farm-in to the Tobermory gas field.
