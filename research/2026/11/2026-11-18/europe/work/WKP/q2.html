WORKSPACE GROUP PLC SECOND QUARTER BUSINESS UPDATE

Summary by AI BETAClose X

Workspace Group PLC reported a second quarter business update ending September 30, 2026, noting resilient occupancy and rents despite slower customer activity, with 18 unfurnished deals completed at 8% above ERV and 18 under offer at 14% above ERV. The company has completed £138 million in disposals since March 2025, progressing towards its £200 million target by March 2027, though some transactions are taking longer due to market conditions. Total rent roll decreased by 0.6% to £126.6 million, with total portfolio occupancy at 79.2%. The company is investing approximately £1.5 million in 24 furnished units, targeting mid-teen incremental yield on cost, and expects over 100 more furnished units in 2027. Net debt stood at £754 million as of September 30, 2026, with cash and undrawn facilities totaling £246 million and a proforma LTV of 36%.
