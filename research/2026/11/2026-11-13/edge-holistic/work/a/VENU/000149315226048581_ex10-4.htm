Section 1.12. Demising Elevation. The surface, wherever located and at whatever actual elevation relative to finished grade, that separates the Horizontal Improvements from the Vertical Improvements, it being agreed that (a) any element whose primary structural function is to support, anchor, or bear the load of a Vertical Improvement – including any foundation, footing, pier, caisson, drilled shaft, grade beam, pile cap, or slab – constitutes a Vertical Improvement and is deemed to lie above the Demising Elevation, and (b) all other site and infrastructure elements constitute Horizontal Improvements and are not deemed to lie above the Demising Elevation.
