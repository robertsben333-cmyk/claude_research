<P STYLE="margin: 0; text-align: right"><FONT STYLE="font-family: Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; font-size: 8pt"><B>Exhibit 99.1</B></FONT></P>

<P STYLE="margin: 0; text-align: right">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="margin: 0; text-align: left">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="margin: 0; text-align: left">&nbsp;<IMG SRC="logo.jpg" ALT=""></P>

<P STYLE="font: bold 24pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0"></P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: bold 24pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">Ballard FCmove&reg;-SC fuel cell engine makes
series-production debut in next-generation Solaris Urbino 12 hydrogen bus</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">New purpose-built hydrogen bus platform and transit
fuel cell engine combine compact design, improved performance and tailored service support to lower lifecycle costs for public transit
operators.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">VANCOUVER, BC, Nov. 5, 2026 /CNW/ -- Ballard Power
Systems (NASDAQ: BLDP) (TSX: BLDP) today announced that its FCmove<SUP>&reg;</SUP>-SC fuel cell engine will make its series-production
debut in the new-generation Solaris Urbino 12 hydrogen transit bus. This milestone follows the extension of the companies' long-term supply
agreement, broadens the scope of cooperation and reflects their continued commitment to advancing hydrogen bus technology.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">Designed for city transit duty cycles, the FCmove<SUP>&reg;</SUP>-SC
combines efficient power generation with a compact architecture that simplifies vehicle integration and servicing when in operation. Its
design draws on Ballard's extensive operating experience to help manufacturers and operators improve performance and reduce lifecycle
costs.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0"><IMG SRC="image_001.jpg" ALT=""><BR>
New-generation Solaris Urbino 12 Hydrogen &copy; Solaris</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">&quot;The FCmove<SUP>&reg;</SUP>-SC's first integration
into series production brings our latest fuel cell technology into Solaris's new bus platform designed around everyday operational needs,&quot;
said Oben Uluc, Chief Revenue Officer, Ballard Power Systems. &quot;For manufacturers, that means simpler integration and greater design
flexibility. For operators, it means efficiency, serviceability and long-term value - making hydrogen buses an increasingly competitive
option for zero-emission public transport.&quot;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0"><B>A new vehicle architecture for hydrogen-powered
transit</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">The new-generation Solaris Urbino 12 hydrogen is built
on the unified platform shared by the latest Urbino buses. Removing the conventional engine tower creates space for additional rear seating,
while the flexible positioning of roof-mounted components improves service access.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">The fuel cell serves as the bus's primary energy source,
generating electricity for a centrally mounted traction motor. A supporting traction battery supplies additional power during peak demand
and stores energy recovered through regenerative braking.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0"><B>Fuel cell innovation focused on lifecycle value</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">The FCmove<SUP>&reg;</SUP>-SC introduces several improvements
for bus manufacturers and operators:</P>

<UL STYLE="list-style-type: disc">

<LI STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0pt 0 12pt"><B>More power in a compact package</B>: A 57% increase in volumetric
power density through integrated DC/DC packaging.</LI>

<LI STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0pt 0 12pt"><B>Simpler vehicle integration</B>: An internal DC/DC converter
and power controller reduce external interfaces and consolidate functionality.</LI>

<LI STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0pt 0 12pt"><B>Improved thermal management</B>: Maximum radiator outlet temperature
increases from 60&deg;C to 75&deg;C, supporting reduced cooling requirements and greater use of waste heat for cabin heating.</LI>

<LI STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0pt 0 12pt"><B>Easier servicing</B>: A 40% reduction in total part count simplifies
the system and helps reduce maintenance requirements.</LI>

<LI STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0pt 0 12pt"><B>Enhanced safety</B>: Revised internal stack-enclosure geometry
provides passive protection against hydrogen-related risks.</LI>

</UL>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">The FCmove<SUP>&reg;</SUP>-SC delivers up to 80kW
of rated power and 57% peak efficiency, with a specified lifetime exceeding 25,000 operating hours. It also supports freeze-start operation
down to &minus;30&deg;C and remote diagnostics to help identify maintenance needs.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0"><IMG SRC="image_002.jpg" ALT=""><BR>
Ballard FCmove&reg;-SC transit bus fuel cell module</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0"><B>Building on hydrogen bus leadership</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">As the leading supplier of fuel cell engines for transit
vehicles, Ballard has powered more than 2,500 fuel cell buses worldwide, collectively exceeding 300 million kilometres of operation. This
experience informs product development and support for manufacturers and fleet operators.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0"><B>Supporting fleets throughout their operating life</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">Alongside its fuel cell products, Ballard offers lifecycle
support through Ballard Fleet360&deg;. The service offering combines remote performance monitoring, data analytics, maintenance planning
and service expertise to help customers anticipate service needs, reduce unplanned downtime and manage fleet performance. Fleet360&deg;
availability and coverage are agreed separately for individual fleets in coordination with the vehicle manufacturer.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">Ballard's capabilities extend beyond fuel cell engines
to include hydrogen production, logistics, refuelling and integrated Energy-as-a-Service solutions. These expanded capabilities strengthen
Ballard's ability to provide the technology, services and hydrogen solutions customers need to deploy and operate zero-emission fleets
at scale.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0"><B>About Ballard Power Systems</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">Ballard Power Systems (NASDAQ: BLDP; TSX: BLDP) is
a global leader in zero-emission hydrogen energy solutions and an integrated Energy-as-a-Service (EaaS) provider. Together, with its wholly
owned subsidiary, GeoPura Ltd., Ballard delivers turnkey, vertically integrated hydrogen ecosystem solutions spanning hydrogen production,
logistics, refuelling, zero-emission fuel cell engines, and stationary Hydrogen Power Units.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">Ballard powers critical off-grid, stationary applications
&ndash; including construction, events, film sets, healthcare, defense, temporary power infrastructure, and supplies fuel cell engines
for heavy-duty mobility applications such as bus, rail, and marine. With an EaaS model, combined with cutting-edge technology across the
hydrogen value chain, Ballard decarbonizes mission-critical operations worldwide. To learn more about Ballard, visit www.ballard.com.</P>
