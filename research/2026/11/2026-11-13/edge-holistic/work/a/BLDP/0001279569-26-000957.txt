<P STYLE="font: bold 24pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0"></P>

<P STYLE="font: bold 24pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">Ballard Secures 4.8MW Order from Siemens Mobility
to Power Hydrogen Trains in Romania</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">24 FCrail<SUP>&trade;</SUP> fuel cell modules will
power 12 Mireo Plus H trains, Romania's first hydrogen fleet and the platform's largest deployment to date.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">VANCOUVER, BC, Oct. 6, 2026 /CNW/ - &nbsp;Ballard
Power Systems (NASDAQ: BLDP) (TSX: BLDP) today announced an order for 24 x FCrail<SUP>&trade;</SUP> 200 kW fuel cell modules from Siemens
Mobility GmbH (&quot;Siemens Mobility&quot;; www.mobility.siemens.com), a leader in rail automation and electrification.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">The order, totaling 4.8 MW, is expected to power 12
Mireo Plus H hydrogen passenger trains for Romania's Railway Reform Authority (ARF).&nbsp; This represents the largest commercial deployment
of the Mireo Plus H platform to date, the first outside of Germany, as well as the first fleet of Mireo trains in Eastern Europe. Fuel
cell engine deliveries are scheduled for 2027 and 2028, with the fleet expected to enter passenger service in 2029.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">This order deepens the long-standing collaboration
between Ballard and Siemens Mobility, building on a multi-year supply commitment established in 2022. As Siemens Mobility expands its
hydrogen rail fleet, this new order leverages the proven success of earlier Mireo Plus H deployments in Germany, reflecting the trusted
performance and reliability of Ballard's fuel cell technology in the demanding rail sector.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">&nbsp;<IMG SRC="image1.jpg" ALT=""><BR>
A Siemens Mobility Mireo Plus H hydrogen train,
powered by Ballard FCrail&trade; fuel cell engines. Photo credit: Siemens Mobility GmbH</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">&quot;We are grateful for our partnership with Siemens
Mobility and their passionate commitment to expanding zero-emission rail technology across Europe. Together, we are innovating and delivering
clean transport solutions that are leading the industry. This order is the largest deployment of the Mireo Plus H fleet and we are proud
to work side by side with Siemens Mobility to deliver the future - today.&quot; Marty Neese, CEO, Ballard Power Systems</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">&quot;With this order, we will introduce our proven
and trusted Mireo trains as well as hydrogen- powered rail transport to Eastern Europe. The 12 Mireo Plus H trains will make up Romania's
first hydrogen-powered train fleet, based on our reliable Mireo platform and the successful deployment of this technology in Germany,&quot;
said Thomas Graetz, Head of Regional and Commuter Rail at Siemens Mobility.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">Trains powered by Ballard fuel cells offer safe and
reliable zero-emission operations, including long range, fast refueling and flexible operation across a wide variety of routes and environmental
conditions, acting as a 1:1 replacement of diesel engines on non-electrified rail lines.&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0"><B>About Ballard</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">Ballard Power Systems (NASDAQ: BLDP; TSX: BLDP) is
a global leader in zero-emission hydrogen energy solutions and an integrated Energy-as-a-Service (EaaS) provider. Together,&nbsp;with
its wholly owned subsidiary, GeoPura Ltd., Ballard delivers turnkey, vertically integrated hydrogen ecosystem solutions spanning hydrogen
production, logistics, refuelling, zero-emission fuel cell engines, and stationary Hydrogen Power Units.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 15pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">Ballard powers critical off-grid, stationary applications
- including&nbsp;construction, events, film sets, healthcare, defense, temporary power infrastructure, and&nbsp;supplies fuel cell engines&nbsp;for
heavy-duty mobility applications such as bus, rail, and marine. With an EaaS model,&nbsp;combined with cutting-edge technology across
the hydrogen value chain, Ballard decarbonizes mission-critical operations worldwide. To learn more about Ballard, visit&nbsp;www.ballard.com.</P>
