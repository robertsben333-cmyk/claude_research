<P STYLE="font: bold 14pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">Review of the Ballard Forum</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">Ballard is targeting to become adj. EBITDA positive
by the end of 2027 and full year 2028.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">VANCOUVER, BC, Oct. 22, 2026 /CNW/ - Ballard Power
Systems&nbsp;(NASDAQ: BLDP) (TSX: BLDP) today held the Ballard Forum. Notable highlights included:</P>

<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 12pt"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 18pt"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 18pt">&bull;</TD><TD><FONT STYLE="font-family: Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif"><B>Expanded Business Model</B>: Ballard's business model has grown to add
Energy-as-a-Service to existing equipment sales and services.&nbsp;</FONT></TD></TR></TABLE>

<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 12pt"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 18pt"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 18pt">&bull;</TD><TD><FONT STYLE="font-family: Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif"><B>Accelerating Revenue Growth</B>: Ballard is targeting a 30% Compound Annual
Growth Rate (CAGR) in corporate revenue over the next two fiscal years.</FONT></TD></TR></TABLE>

<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 12pt"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 18pt"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 18pt">&bull;</TD><TD><FONT STYLE="font-family: Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif"><B>Expanding Margin Profile</B>: The Company anticipates gross margins exceeding
20% in 2027, scaling to greater than 25% by 2028.</FONT></TD></TR></TABLE>

<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 12pt"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 18pt"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 18pt">&bull;</TD><TD><FONT STYLE="font-family: Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif"><B>Strategic Fleet Expansion</B>: Ballard plans to allocate $80 million to
$120 million in capital expenditures to expand its Hydrogen Power Unit (HPU) fleet, with optionality in pace of investment. This investment
is expected to be financed through asset-backed finance at greater than 80% loan to value, minimizing the cash outlay.</FONT></TD></TR></TABLE>

<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 12pt"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 18pt"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 18pt">&bull;</TD><TD><FONT STYLE="font-family: Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif"><B>Prudent Capital Allocation and Cash Management</B>: There is no immediate
need to invest in manufacturing expansion or hydrogen production. Cash use for operating costs is expected to decrease over time as margins
and revenue grow.</FONT></TD></TR></TABLE>

<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 12pt"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 18pt"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 18pt">&bull;</TD><TD><FONT STYLE="font-family: Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif"><B>Path to Profitability</B>: Ballard is targeting Adjusted EBITDA profitability
by the end of 2027, sustaining full-year profitability throughout 2028.</FONT></TD></TR></TABLE>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0"><B>About Ballard Power Systems<BR>
</B>Ballard Power Systems (NASDAQ: BLDP; TSX: BLDP) is a global leader in zero-emission hydrogen energy solutions and an integrated Energy-as-a-Service
(EaaS) provider. Together, with its wholly owned subsidiary, GeoPura Ltd., Ballard delivers turnkey, vertically integrated hydrogen ecosystem
solutions spanning hydrogen production, logistics, refueling, zero-emission fuel cell engines, and stationary Hydrogen Power Units.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">Ballard powers critical off-grid, stationary applications
- including construction, events, film sets, healthcare, defense, temporary power infrastructure, and supplies fuel cell engines for heavy-duty
mobility applications such as bus, rail, and marine. With an EaaS model, combined with cutting-edge technology across the hydrogen value
chain, Ballard decarbonizes mission-critical operations worldwide. To learn more about Ballard, visit www.ballard.com.</P>
