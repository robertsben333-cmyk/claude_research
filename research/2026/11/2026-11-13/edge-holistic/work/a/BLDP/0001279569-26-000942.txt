<P STYLE="margin: 0; text-align: right"><FONT STYLE="font-family: Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; font-size: 8pt"><B>Exhibit 99.1</B></FONT></P>

<P STYLE="margin: 0; text-align: right">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="margin: 0; text-align: left">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="margin: 0; text-align: left">&nbsp;<IMG SRC="logo.jpg" ALT=""></P>

<P STYLE="font: bold 24pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0"></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0"></P>

<P STYLE="font: bold 14pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">Ballard launches Fleet360 services for new North
American FCmove&reg;-HD+ fleets at APTA EXPO 2026</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0"><I>End-to-end service program combines fuel cell module
performance guarantee with committed service response and resolution times, to enable transit operators to plan maintenance and fuel cell
lifecycle costs. </I></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">CHICAGO, Oct. 1, 2026 /CNW/ -- Ballard Power Systems
is launching the first-of-its-kind fuel cell lifecycle programme&nbsp;&ndash; Ballard Fleet360&nbsp;&ndash; at APTA TRANSform&nbsp;&amp;
EXPO 2026 in Chicago.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">Available with new FCmove<SUP>&reg;</SUP>-HD+ module
sales in North America, the program connects intelligent fuel cell data, tailored technical support, parts assurance, technician training
and refurbishment options. Fleet360's FCcare+ coverage package provides a fuel cell module performance guarantee for power output up to
250,000 miles of fuel cell operation.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">&quot;Transit operators want confidence in how their
fuel cells perform today and as their fleets age,&quot; said Ralph Robinett, Chief Operating Officer of Ballard. &quot;With Fleet360,
we are backing our technology with a defined performance guarantee, supported by the information, parts and technical support maintenance
teams need. This capability gives customers a clearer basis for planning fuel cell maintenance and lifecycle budgets, with performance
commitments from Ballard.&quot;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0"><B>Turning fuel cell data into maintenance decisions</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">At the center of Fleet360 is FCpulse, Ballard's digital
platform for visibility into fuel cell health, performance and service-part trends. Its state of health dashboard helps maintenance teams
proactively identify modules needing attention, prioritise investigations and prepare workshop resources. Its data visualizer access supports
deeper technical investigation, while quarterly performance reports inform service and lifecycle planning.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0"><B>Defined guarantees for module performance</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">The FCcare service package combines full parts coverage
with commitments for response, spare part delivery and resolution, alongside FCpulse state of health dashboard access. The FCcare+ package
adds a fuel cell module performance guarantee for power output power up to 250,000 miles of fuel cell operation. Guarantees and service
commitments are defined by the package and agreement selected. Core+ refurbishment, Ballard Academy training and the full version of FCpulse
are available alongside either package.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0"><B>Service support built around fleet operations</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">The program is supported by Ballard's Remote Operations
Centre in Vancouver, which coordinates triage, diagnostics and engineering escalation, together with expanded field service capability
and US parts inventory in Bend, Oregon. The Ballard Academy builds customers' in-house maintenance capability and diagnostic skills, while
Core+ connects stack condition assessment with refurbishment planning.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">&quot;Data creates value when it helps a maintenance
team decide what to do next,&quot; Robinett added. &quot;By connecting FCpulse insights with parts support, technical expertise and refurbishment
planning, we can help operators prepare for maintenance and budget for the fuel cell module over its life. Our role is to give their teams
the information and practical support to make those decisions with greater confidence.&quot;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0"><IMG SRC="image_001.jpg" ALT="">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">Ballard Fleet360 fuel cell lifecycle program</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">Fleet360 builds on Ballard's experience across more
than 186&nbsp;million miles (300&nbsp;million kilometers) of operational service and more than 3,000 fuel cell engines on the road. Today,
Ballard has deployed nearly 330 fuel cell buses across North America, including over 225 buses in California and a fleet of more than
30 in Nevada.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">Together, the market-leading FCmove<SUP>&reg;</SUP>
range of fuel cell engines and Ballard Fleet360 demonstrate Ballard's focus on the complete customer journey &ndash; from vehicle integration
and deployment to reliable operation throughout the fleet lifecycle.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">Ballard's presence at APTA EXPO 2026 also comes just
weeks after the completion of its acquisition of GeoPura. The acquisition broadens Ballard's capabilities beyond fuel cell engines to
include hydrogen production, logistics, refueling, and integrated Energy-as-a-Service solutions. These expanded capabilities strengthen
Ballard's ability to support customers with the technology, services, and hydrogen solutions needed to deploy and operate zero-emission
fleets at scale.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0"><B>Meet Ballard at APTA EXPO 2026</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">Transit operators and bus manufacturers can meet Ballard's
team at APTA EXPO 2026 to discuss Fleet360 coverage and service options. Visit us at Booth&nbsp;462 from October&nbsp;5-7.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0"><B>About Ballard</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">Ballard Power Systems (NASDAQ: BLDP; TSX: BLDP) is
a global leader in zero-emission hydrogen energy solutions and an integrated Energy-as-a-Service (EaaS) provider. Together,&nbsp;with
its wholly owned subsidiary, GeoPura Ltd., Ballard delivers turnkey, vertically integrated hydrogen ecosystem solutions spanning hydrogen
production, logistics, refuelling, zero-emission fuel cell engines, and stationary Hydrogen Power Units.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin-right: 0; margin-left: 0">Ballard powers critical off-grid, stationary applications
- including&nbsp;construction, events, film sets, healthcare, defense, temporary power infrastructure, and&nbsp;supplies fuel cell engines&nbsp;for
heavy-duty mobility applications such as bus, rail, and marine. With an EaaS model,&nbsp;combined with cutting-edge technology across
the hydrogen value chain, Ballard decarbonizes mission-critical operations worldwide. To learn more about Ballard, visit&nbsp;www.ballard.com.</P>
