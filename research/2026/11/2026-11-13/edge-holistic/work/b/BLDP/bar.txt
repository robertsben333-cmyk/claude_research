GeoPura, founded in 2019 and headquartered in Newcastle, United Kingdom, is a provider of zero-emission, hydrogen-based power solutions. GeoPura designs, manufactures, operates, leases and sells its Hydrogen Power Units (“ HPUs ”), which use the Company’s fuel cell modules to convert hydrogen into electricity and provide clean, low-noise, off-grid power. GeoPura also produces green hydrogen, generated via electrolysis, at its dedicated production sites, including through its 50% ownership interest in UK-based HyMarnham Power, and holds a contract under the UK government’s inaugural Hydrogen Allocation Round 1 (HAR1), a subsidy mechanism guaranteeing hydrogen production revenues over fifteen years. 2.2 Acquisition Date August 28, 2026. 2.3 Consideration Consideration for the Acquisition consisted of (i) upfront consideration of £269.6 million (the “ Upfront Consideration ”), being the headline purchase price of £275.0 million as adjusted in accordance with the terms of the Purchase Agreement, and (ii) contingent consideration of up to £27.5 million (the “ Contingent Consideration ”) payable in cash and/or shares pursuant to the terms of the Purchase Agreement if GeoPura achieves certain specified financial milestones following the closing of the Acquisition (“ Closing ”). Any Contingent Consideration that becomes payable may be satisfied, at the Company’s sole discretion, in cash, through the issuance of common shares of the Company at a price per share equal to the 30-day trailing volume-weighted average price of the common shares on the business day immediately preceding the applicable payment date, or a combination thereof. Any cash portion of the Contingent Consideration is expected to be funded from the Company’s cash on hand. The Upfront Consideration was satisfied on Closing through a combination of: (a) a cash payment of £77.1 million (the “ Closing Payment ”), funded from the Company’s cash on hand; (b) the issuance of 49,584,212 common shares of the Company (the “ Consideration Shares ”) to the Sellers at a deemed price of US$5.02 per share, based on the Company’s 30-day volume-weighted average share price prior to the date of the Purchase Agreement (the “ 30-Day VWAP ”); and (c) the grant of 1,084,540 rollover restricted share units (the “ Rollover RSUs ”) under the Company’s GeoPura Restricted Share Unit Plan (and not under the Company’s existing Consolidated Share Distribution Plan) in consideration for the release and cancellation of certain GeoPura options held by GeoPura optionholders. The Rollover RSUs represent 1,084,540 common shares of the Company reserved for issuance, having a deemed issue price of US$5.02 per share based on the 30-Day VWAP, and are expected to vest twelve months following Closing. The Consideration Shares and the common shares underlying the Rollover RSUs represented approximately 14.1% of the Company’s outstanding common shares on a pro forma basis. For accounting purposes, the Consideration Shares and the Rollover RSUs are measured at their estimated fair value as of Closing, based on the closing price of the common shares on the date of Closing, and the total preliminary purchase consideration, including the estimated fair value of the Contingent Consideration, is approximately US$230.7 million, as described in the unaudited pro forma condensed consolidated financial statements attached as Appendix C. 2.4 Effect on Financial Position The effect of the Acquisition on the Company’s financial position is presented in the unaudited pro forma condensed consolidated financial statements of the Company attached as Appendix C to this business acquisition report. Revenue from the sale of hydrogen power units and hydrogen production equipment is recognised over time where the criteria in IFRS 15 are met, in particular where the company’s performance does not create an asset with an alternative use and the company has an enforceable right to payment for performance completed to date, including through contractual termination-for-convenience compensation providing cost plus a reasonable margin, using an input method to measure progress. Where those criteria are not met, revenue is recognised at the point in time when control of the equipment transfers to the customer, assessed with reference to title, physical possession, acceptance, and the transfer of significant risks and rewards. Joint venture revenue Revenue from services provided to the HyMarnham Power joint venture is recognised as the services are performed. Revenue relating to the supply of a production facility is recognised at a point in time when control of the facility transfers to the joint venture. Variable consideration is included in the transaction price only to the extent that it is highly probable that a significant reversal will not occur. Amounts invoiced in advance of performance are recognised as contract liabilities; amounts where performance precedes invoicing are recognised as contract assets. Government grants and other income Government grants, including funding associated with the Hydrogen Allocation Round, are recognised when there is reasonable assurance that the company will comply with the conditions attaching to them and that the grants will be received. Leases At inception of a contract the company assesses whether the contract is, or contains, a lease. For each lease the company recognises a right-of-use asset and a lease liability, except for short-term leases and leases of low-value assets, for which the lease payments are recognised as an expense on a straight-line basis. The lease liability is initially measured at the present value of the unpaid lease payments, discounted using the rate implicit in the lease or, if that rate cannot be readily determined, the company’s incremental borrowing rate; the right-of-use asset is initially measured at cost and depreciated over the shorter of the lease term and the useful life of the asset, except where ownership transfers at the end of the term (including hire purchase contracts), in which case the asset is depreciated over its useful life. Sale and leaseback Where the company sells plant and leases it back under asset-backed financing arrangements, the company first assesses whether the transfer is a sale under IFRS 15. Where control of the plant does not pass to the financier, as is the case for the company’s finance-and-buy-back arrangements, the transfer is not a sale: the plant continues to be recognised at its previous carrying amount and a financial liability equal to the proceeds is recognised within borrowings and measured at amortised cost. General Information and Description of the Transaction On August 28, 2026, Ballard Power Systems Inc. (the “Company” or “Ballard”) completed the acquisition (the “Transaction”) of GeoPura Ltd. (“GeoPura”), a UK-based provider of zero-emission, hydrogen-based power solutions. GeoPura designs, manufactures, and operates Hydrogen Power Units™, or HPUs™, that offers clean, low-noise, off-grid power. Utilizing Ballard fuel cell modules at their core, these advanced systems convert hydrogen into clean electricity. GeoPura also produces green hydrogen, generated via electrolysis, at its dedicated production facilities and transports it with the UK’s largest compressed hydrogen distribution fleet.
