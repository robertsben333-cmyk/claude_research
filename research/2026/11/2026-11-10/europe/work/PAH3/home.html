Investment strategy

Porsche SE's investment strategy aims to create sustainable value for its shareholders based on the increase in value of the assets under management as well as dividend distributions. Porsche SE's investments are classified into two categories. The first category includes the long-term core investment in Volkswagen AG and Porsche AG. The second category includes portfolio investments that are generally held by Porsche SE on a temporary basis. Such investments are typically characterized by high growth and value appreciation potential during the holding period. In both categories, the focus is on promising companies in the areas of mobility and industrial technology whose growth is supported by macro trends.
