    About DNB Carnegie Access

    DNB Carnegie Access aim is to connect listed companies with the capital markets. Through top ranked public equity research and broad distribution via written reports, seminars, video, and podcasts, we increase visibility and investor understanding for small- and medium-sized companies, both among institutional and private investors.

    Along with corporate finance, market making and certified advisory we provide a complete product offering to our clients.
