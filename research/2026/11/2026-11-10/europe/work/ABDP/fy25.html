Final Results

Summary by AI BETAClose X

AB Dynamics plc reported final results for the year ended 31 August 2025, with revenue increasing by 3% to £114.7 million and adjusted operating profit rising 15% to £23.3 million, slightly exceeding expectations. The company achieved a 15% increase in adjusted diluted earnings per share to 80.3 pence and proposed a 20% increase in total dividend per share to 9.16 pence. Gross margin improved to 62.0% and the operating margin expanded by 210 basis points to 20.3%, driven by operational improvements and a richer revenue mix, though the latter is not expected to recur in FY2026. Net cash stood at £41.4 million, up from £28.6 million in the prior year, following £6.5 million invested in acquisitions and capital expenditure. The company maintains a positive outlook, expecting FY2026 adjusted operating profit to be in line with current expectations, with a bias towards the second half of the year, supported by strong underlying demand drivers and a £32 million order book.
