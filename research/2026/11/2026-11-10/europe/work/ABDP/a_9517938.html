Half-year Report

Summary by AI BETAClose X

AB Dynamics plc reported interim results for the six months ended 28 February 2026, with revenue decreasing by 16% to £48.8 million compared to £58.0 million in the prior year, while gross margin improved to 63.7% from 60.2%. Adjusted EBITDA fell 12% to £11.3 million, and adjusted operating profit decreased 16% to £9.1 million, though the adjusted operating margin remained stable at 18.6%. The company experienced a statutory operating loss of £12.1 million, a significant shift from a £6.7 million profit in the prior period, largely due to £16.8 million in exceptional items related to the VadoTech Testing Services business in China. Despite the revenue decline, the interim dividend per share increased by 10% to 3.08p, reflecting confidence in the group's financial position and prospects, with net cash at period end strengthening to £39.3 million from £27.2 million. The company anticipates full-year adjusted operating profit to be in line with market expectations, with approximately 55-60% of revenue expected in the second half.
