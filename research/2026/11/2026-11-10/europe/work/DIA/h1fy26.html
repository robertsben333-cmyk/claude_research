FY26 Interim Results

Summary by AI BETAClose X

Dialight PLC reported unaudited interim results for the six months ended 30 September 2025, showing a decrease in group revenue to $86.4 million from $90.3 million in the prior year, primarily due to a 9.4% decline in the Lighting segment, though Signals & Components saw a 10.2% increase. The company achieved a significant improvement in gross margin to 35.3% from 33.0%, contributing to a six-fold increase in underlying operating profit to $5.5 million from $0.9 million, alongside a doubling of underlying operating cash flow to $13.9 million. Statutory operating profit improved to $5.9 million from a loss of $19.3 million, and profit before tax rose to $4.5 million from a loss of $20.8 million. Net bank debt reduced to $10.2 million from $15.4 million, and an agreement was reached in October 2025 to accelerate remaining Sanmina payments, settling $5.7 million by December 2025 to remove contingent financial risk.
