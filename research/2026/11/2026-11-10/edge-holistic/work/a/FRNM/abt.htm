Transcript: Abbott Laboratories (ABT) Q3 2026 Earnings Call 

  • October 21, 2026 10:06 pmOctober 21, 2026 10:06 pm
  • by Pangambam S
  • Business & Finance (category)

Abbott Laboratories (NYSE: ABT) reported third quarter 2026 sales growth of 7% and earnings per share growth of 9%, with adjusted EPS of $1.42, and announced plans to separate its nutrition business into an independent publicly traded company led by Daniel Salvadori. In this Q3 2026 earnings call transcript, CEO Robert Ford and CFO Phil Boudreau discuss the nutrition spin-off, electrophysiology momentum with the Volt PFA catheter, the Libre Duo approval, Exact Sciences integration, and the outlook for Q4 2026 and 2027.

Nutrition Business Separation

Robert Ford, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer – Abbott Laboratories: Thanks, Mike. Today marks an important milestone in Abbott’s history.

Along with reporting our third quarter results, we announced our intention to separate our nutrition business into an independent publicly traded company. This decision follows a comprehensive strategic review and reflects our strong belief that both Abbott and the nutrition business can create greater value and achieve their maximum potential as independent organizations.

Abbott entered the nutrition business more than sixty years ago. Since that time, we built a strong foundation anchored by a talented global organization, iconic brands and established market leadership positions across the pediatric and adult nutrition categories. The nutrition market has evolved over time with the pace of change accelerating significantly in recent years.

What was once predominantly viewed as a healthcare market almost exclusively shaped by healthcare professionals has increasingly become a market shaped by changing consumer preferences, wellness trends and retail execution. While this market evolution is not inherently positive or negative, it has fundamentally changed the environment in which we operate. So to best position our nutrition business for maximum long term success, we must evolve and adapt accordingly.

At the same time, Abbott has strategically repositioned its portfolio over the past decade into attractive high growth markets that require sustained investment to drive innovation and support long term growth. So we believe both businesses will be better positioned for the future as independent organizations, each with a distinct identity reflecting their different customer bases, growth profiles, investment requirements and innovation cycles.

And this separation will enable both companies to sharpen strategic and operational focus, direct resources more effectively and enhance commercial and R&D effectiveness. As an independent company, Nutrition will be uniquely positioned to capitalize on the growing demand for healthy nutrition as consumers increasingly recognize the importance of metabolic health nutrition plays in disease prevention and overall wellness.

The new nutrition company will be created from a position of strength. The team has done an exceptional job building a balanced growth model supported by sustainable mix of volume and price. The portfolio includes several iconic brands including Similac, PediaSure, Ensure, Glucerna and Pedialyte, which have market leading positions across the pediatric and adult nutrition categories.

With approximately $8.5 billion in annual sales, 16,000 employees and 14 manufacturing sites, this will be the largest globally scaled pure play company in the healthy nutrition market. Given its scale and financial profile, we would expect this new company to qualify for inclusion in the S&P 500.

I’m pleased to share that Daniel Salvadori, Executive Vice President and Group President of Established Pharmaceuticals and Nutrition Products will become Chief Executive Officer of the new nutrition company. Daniel joined Abbott in 2014 as Senior Vice President in our Established Pharmaceuticals business for Latin America and also served as Executive Vice President of Nutrition Products for four years before being appointed to his current position in 2021.

Throughout his career at Abbott, Daniel has demonstrated outstanding strategic insight and operational excellence.

His broad international experience and proven track record of delivering results as a business operator make him exceptionally well suited for this role and I’m confident he will be a tremendous success. Daniel will be joined by an experienced leadership team with deep industry experience. And this is a battle tested team that has demonstrated exceptional resilience and dedication while navigating a dynamic and evolving operating environment.

Following the separation, Abbott will remain one of the largest, most diversified and fastest growing healthcare companies in the world. The Abbott portfolio will consist of even stronger mix of higher growth, higher margin businesses powered by technology innovation. As a result, we expect Abbott sales growth to accelerate and margin profiles to expand.

While the Abbott portfolio will evolve, maintaining balance across products, payers and geographies will remain a key strength and source of resilience for the company. We built this portfolio to hold market leading positions in three of the most attractive areas of healthcare, med tech, diagnostics and established pharmaceuticals.

The portfolio was also intentionally built to align with durable healthcare trends, including an aging population, increasing prevalence of chronic disease and a growing demand for earlier diagnosis and more personalized treatment. With market leading positions and industry leading pipeline and a proven ability to consistently generate strong earnings growth and cash flow, Abbott is exceptionally well positioned to deliver sustainable growth and create long term shareholder value in the years ahead.

Third Quarter 2026 Business Review

Sales grew 7% in the third quarter and earnings per share grew 9% representing an acceleration in growth on both top and bottom lines compared to our results in the first half of the year.

Diagnostics

Turning to diagnostics, where sales grew 6.5% driven by performance in our Core Laboratory and Cancer Diagnostic businesses. Core Lab sales grew 5.5% driven by strong performance in the U.S., Europe, Japan and Latin America.

On our earnings call in July, I highlighted that test volume data sourced directly from thousands of instruments located across the U.S. and around the world showed a pattern of strong and stable demand. We view this as a positive indicator of the underlying demand for healthcare more broadly. During the third quarter, our testing data continued to support that view with testing demand remaining healthy and at durable levels.

In cancer diagnostics, sales growth of 14% was driven by Cologuard and contributions to growth from our precision oncology and international businesses.

In September, we launched our first blood based colorectal cancer screening test. As the only company to offer both stool based and blood based screening options, we are expanding the options available to healthcare providers and patients to help achieve the goal of getting more people screened.

Nutrition

Turning to nutrition, sales grew 5% reflecting broad based growth across all four categories, U.S. pediatric, international pediatric, U.S. adult and international adult. As I mentioned earlier, the team has done an exceptional job executing a more balanced growth strategy, a more sustainable mix of volume and price driven growth.

In pediatric nutrition, sales grew 9% led by growth of 11% in the U.S. As I noted last quarter, the strong performance in the U.S. includes the benefit from WIC contract wins secured earlier this year, which helped Abbott regain its market leadership position in both the WIC and non WIC segments.

In adult nutrition, sales increased 2% driven by growth of 3.5% in the U.S. as retail consumption data continues to trend in a positive direction. We also continue to deliver strong growth on a sequential basis with worldwide nutrition sales increasing more than $100 million compared to the second quarter.

And finally, we continue to make good progress advancing our innovation pipeline. During the quarter, we launched a new version of our Similac infant formula made with whole milk. This new product is the first and only commercially sterile ready to feed liquid infant formula made with whole milk available in the United States.

Established Pharmaceuticals

Turning to EPD where sales increased 8.5% in the quarter. Our broad portfolio of prescription and over the counter medicines tailored to address local healthcare needs continues to drive strong growth across emerging markets.

Growth in the quarter was broad based across geographies and therapeutic areas and this included double digit growth in several countries across Asia, Latin America and the Middle East, highlighting the strong and durable demand that has long been a hallmark of this business.

Medical Devices

And I’ll wrap up with Medical Devices where sales grew 8%. Growth was well balanced across the portfolio led by double digit growth in electrophysiology and rhythm management.

In electrophysiology, our third quarter results reflect the early beginning of our growth acceleration phase. Growth in the quarter was mid teens or high teens when excluding LAA. In the U.S., growth was in the high teens and exceeded 20% excluding LAA.

This performance was driven by increasing adoption of our new Volt PFA catheter with momentum accelerating in September following the transition to a full U.S. market release in mid August. International growth also accelerated as we continue to capture market share in Europe driven by increasing uptake of our new PFA catheters and growth in other categories across our broad portfolio.

In September, we received FDA approval earlier than expected for our TactiFlex Duo catheter. This catheter enables physicians to seamlessly switch between using RF and PFA energy during a procedure. Given physicians familiarity with the TactiFlex platform, we expect to execute a broad U.S. launch starting in the fourth quarter.

In the third quarter, we also received CE Mark for our new Amulet 360 left atrial appendage device designed to help reduce the risk of stroke in patients with AFib. We look forward to bringing this new technology to the U.S.

In Rhythm Management, sales grew 10% driven by increased adoption of AVEIR across both single and dual chamber segments of the pacemaker market. This expanding use of AVEIR is occurring in both the U.S. and international markets.

And in diabetes care, continuous glucose monitoring sales grew 8% in the quarter. In August, we received FDA approval for Libre Duo, the world’s first dual glucose ketone biowearable sensor. This marks the first time that people with diabetes will be able to monitor ketones without the need for traditional blood or urine tests. And we expect to launch this new sensor in the U.S. in December.

So in summary, we delivered an acceleration in growth on both top and bottom lines. Innovation continues to be a key differentiator for Abbott highlighted by multiple new product approvals and launches that further strengthen our ability to deliver sustainable growth in the years ahead. And we took an important strategic step towards shaping our future through the planned creation of a new market leading nutrition company.

Financial Overview and Separation Details

Phil Boudreau, Executive Vice President, Finance and Chief Financial Officer – Abbott Laboratories: Thanks Robert. Let me begin my remarks with an overview of some of the key aspects of our planning process for the separation.

The new nutrition company will become a publicly traded company through distribution to Abbott shareholders. Upon completion of the separation, Abbott shareholders will own shares of both Abbott and the new nutrition company enabling our shareholders to participate in the value creation opportunities of both businesses. The final share distribution ratio will be communicated prior to the separation, which we anticipate completing by the end of next year, subject to the required approvals.

We expect to incur separation related costs as we progress through the separation process. These costs will be treated as specified items and excluded from adjusted earnings. As a result, we do not expect the separation to have an impact on plan to provide in January.

As we prepare for the separation, we intend to establish capital structures that ensure both companies have a strong financial foundation. This includes ensuring adequate capital to support long term growth objectives and capital allocation priorities, while maintaining strong investment grade credit ratings for both companies.

As part of that process, we are planning for the new company to raise debt with a portion of the proceeds to be distributed to Abbott. The amount of debt to be raised and the amount to be distributed to Abbott will be determined at a later date. Finally, we expect that both Abbott and the new nutrition company will pay a dividend that when combined will equal Abbott’s dividend at the time of the separation.

Following the separation, we expect Abbott’s financial profile to reflect an acceleration in the sales growth and increased profitability. Excluding the current profile of the Nutrition business from the current profile of the Abbott portfolio, we would expect sales growth to accelerate by 50 basis to 100 basis points, gross margin as a percent of sales to increase by more than 200 basis points and an operating margin as a percent of sales to increase by approximately 100 basis points.

Now turning to our third quarter results. Sales increased 7% on a comparable basis and adjusted earnings per share of $1.42 was in line with our guidance and the consensus estimate. Foreign exchange had an unfavorable year over year impact of 0.4% on third quarter sales, which was a slight improvement compared to our expectations at the time of our earnings call in July.

Regarding other aspects of the P&L, the adjusted gross margin profile was 57.2% of sales, representing an increase of 140 basis points compared to the prior year. The improvement in gross margin was broad reflecting favorable business mix within the legacy Abbott portfolio and from the addition of Exact Sciences, as well as the continued operational improvements and disciplined execution of our margin expansion initiatives.

Adjusted R&D was 6.9% of sales and adjusted SG&A was 26.9% of sales. Our operating margin of 23.4% expanded by 40 basis points compared to the prior year.

And based on current rates, we expect exchange to have a negative impact of approximately 1% on fourth quarter sales. For the fourth quarter, we forecast adjusted earnings per share of $1.62 to $1.67.
