About Freenome, Inc. (FRNM)

Before we jump into Freenome, Inc.’s stock price, history, target price and what caused it to recently dip, let’s take a look at some background.

Freenome, Inc., a biotechnology company, develops blood-based screening tests to detect cancer in the United States. The company offers an early cancer detection platform that integrates multiomics, artificial intelligence, and machine learning to uncover complex biological signals associated with cancer. It develops SimpleScreen™ CRC, a blood-based test, including a test for colorectal cancer screening, as well as tests for multi-cancer and multi-disease detection, such as lung, breast, cervical, liver, pancreatic, and esophageal cancers. The company was founded in 2014 and is headquartered in Brisbane, California.
