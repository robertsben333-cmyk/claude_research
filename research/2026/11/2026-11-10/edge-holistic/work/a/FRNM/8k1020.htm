Item 8.01.
Other Events

On October 20, 2026, the Company announced the launch of SimpleScreen™ Lung and a first-of-its-kind offering that enables eligible patients to be screened for both lung and colorectal cancer (“CRC”) through a single blood draw, using two independently validated, indication-specific tests. The Company’s Personalized Cancer Detection approach combines risk-based screening with a portfolio of single-cancer and multi-cancer tests that are developed specifically for each population and to meet individual patient screening needs.

SimpleScreen Lung was clinically validated in a case-control study of adults ages 50 to 80 with a ≥20 pack-year smoking history who meet American Cancer Society (“ACS”) lung cancer screening eligibility. In an independent cohort of 172 lung cancer cases and 436 controls, the test achieved 85% sensitivity at 50% specificity and a 99.8% negative predictive value (“NPV”). This NPV means that approximately two of every 1,000 people with a negative result would be expected to have lung cancer. Sensitivity was 82% for stage I, 75% for stage II, 91% for stage III, and 94% for stage IV.

Performance estimates were adjusted to reflect target distributions of age, sex, lung cancer stage and histologic subtype. The intended use population includes former smokers regardless of how long ago they quit, consistent with the 2023 ACS lung cancer screening guideline update.

In the Company’s PREEMPT CRC study, which enrolled more than 48,000 participants, the test demonstrated 81% sensitivity for CRC, 90% specificity for advanced colorectal neoplasia, and a 99.9% NPV in an analysis adjusted to the 2020 U.S. Census age and sex distribution.
