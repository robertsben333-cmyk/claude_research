Item 5.02    Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.
On November 6, 2026, the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of Evolv Technologies Holdings, Inc. (the “Company”) appointed Jason Blessing, age 55, to the Board, effective November 6, 2026, to serve as a Class III director until the Company’s 2027 annual meeting of stockholders.
The Board also appointed Mr. Blessing to the Audit Committee and the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee. In approving the appointment, the Board concluded that Mr. Blessing satisfies the independence requirements of Rule 10A-3 under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, and the applicable rules of The Nasdaq Stock Market LLC, including the requirements applicable to audit committee membership. The Board also determined that Mr. Blessing is an “audit committee financial expert” as that term is defined by applicable Securities and Exchange Commission regulations. There are no arrangements or understandings between Mr. Blessing and any other person pursuant to which Mr. Blessing was selected as a director, and there are no related-party transactions involving Mr. Blessing that would require disclosure under Item 404(a) of Regulation S-K. Mr. Blessing will be eligible to receive compensation as a non-employee director in accordance with the Company’s Non-Employee Director Compensation Policy, as amended.
Jason Blessing has a strong track record as an executive and operator with more than 30 years of experience, including service as a public company chief executive officer and director. Mr. Blessing currently serves as Executive Chair of Mosai and as a director of eClinical Solutions and Parsec Automation, both privately held software companies, and as an advisor to the CEO of AptEdge. His experience includes leading go-to-market, product and technology organizations. From May 2018 to September 2024, Mr. Blessing served as President and Chief Executive Officer of Model N, Inc. (formerly NYSE: MODN), and served as a director of Model N from May 2018 until August 2026. From January 2013 to December 2017, Mr. Blessing served as President, Chief Executive Officer and a director of Plex Systems, now part of Rockwell Automation. Mr. Blessing previously held executive roles at Taleo, Oracle and PeopleSoft and began his career in the management consulting practice of Price Waterhouse. Mr. Blessing holds a B.A. from the University of Michigan.
