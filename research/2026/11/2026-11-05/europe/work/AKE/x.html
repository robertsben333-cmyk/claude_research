Consensus and Analysts

Consensus

The earnings consensus is an average of estimates of Arkema’s financial results prepared by financial analyst teams.

Last Update: October 2026
  • Download the consensus (.pdf)

This information is supplied by Arkema's Investor Relations Department. The consensus is calculated on the basis of data published by financial analysts who cover the Arkema share or are directly collected from them.

The consensus is provided by Arkema for information purposes only. The fact that this consensus can be found on Arkema’s website is in no way a validation or approval by Arkema of any or all of the estimates presented.

This information should not be construed as an invitation to buy or sell shares or to enter into any type of transaction whatsoever. Before any transaction, please contact your broker.
