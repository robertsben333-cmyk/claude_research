Capital structure
As of 30 September 2026, cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments and margin receivables amounted to SEK 84.2
billion, an increase of SEK 28.9 billion compared to SEK 55.2 billion as per 31 December 2025. Vattenfall’s credit facilities consist
of a EUR 2.0 billion Revolving Credit Facility maturing 2028 and a EUR 1.0 billion facility maturing in November 2027. As per 30
September 2026, available liquid assets, including the credit facilities amounted to 53.1% of net sales. Vattenfall’s policy is to
maintain liquidity sources to cover the higher of 10% of the Group’s annual turnover, or 45-days’ stressed liquidity needs.

Net debt decreased by SEK 23.1 billion to net cash of SEK 11.4 billion as per 30 September 2026 compared to net debt of SEK
11.7 billion as per 31 December 2025. Adjusted net debt amounted to SEK 64.6 billion, a decrease of SEK 8.8 billion compared to
31 December 2025. The decrease is mainly explained by positive cash flow from operating activities before changes in working
capital (SEK 32.4 billion) partly offset by negative cash flow from investments excluding short-term investments (SEK 23.0 billion).

Vattenfall has hybrid bonds of SEK 21.5 billion outstanding. One of the hybrid bonds, amounting to EUR 1 billion (SEK 11.3
billion), has its first call date on 19 March 2027. Vattenfall intends to redeem the hybrid bond at its first call date using cash on
balance sheet, after which SEK 10.1 billion of hybrid capital will remain outstanding. The planned reduction in hybrid capital is a
result of Vattenfall’s continued balance sheet strengthening. While reducing the amount outstanding, Vattenfall continues to
consider hybrid capital a permanent part of its capital structure.
