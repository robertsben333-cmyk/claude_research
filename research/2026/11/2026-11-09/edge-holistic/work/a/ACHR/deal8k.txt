Closing Conditions Under the terms of the Purchase Agreement, the completion of the Acquisition is subject to customary closing conditions, including, among other things: (i) the expiration or termination of any waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, as amended, and the receipt of certain approvals under laws regulating national security or foreign direct investment (the “Regulatory Condition”); (ii) the absence of legal restraints prohibiting the consummation of the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement and the Related Agreements (as defined in the Purchase Agreement) (the “Transactions”); (iii) the accuracy of representations and warranties, subject to specified materiality qualifications; (iv) compliance with covenants in all material respects; (v) the absence of a “Material Adverse Effect” with respect to the Company or the Target Companies; and (vi) the approval of the Consideration Shares for listing on the New York Stock Exchange (the “NYSE”). Representations, Warranties and Covenants The Purchase Agreement contains customary representations and warranties made by the Company, Boeing and the Target Companies, in each case generally subject to customary materiality qualifiers. Each representation and warranty of each party will survive for a period of eighteen (18) months following the Closing Date. The Purchase Agreement also contains customary pre-Closing covenants. Each of the Target Companies has agreed to preserve its present business and to not take certain actions. Boeing and the Target Companies have agreed not to solicit alternative transactions. Each of the parties has agreed to use reasonable best efforts to consummate the Transactions as promptly as practicable, and to obtain the regulatory approvals required in connection with the Transactions. Boeing has also agreed to provide audited and unaudited financial statements of the Target Companies no later than sixty (60) days after Closing to support the Company’s reporting obligations. The Purchase Agreement also contains certain post-Closing covenants regarding, among other things, record retention, employee transfers, public announcement, confidentiality, insurance, tax matters, the Lock-Up, supplemental listing and legend removal with respect to the Consideration Shares, director and officer indemnification and release of claims. Indemnification The Purchase Agreement contains reciprocal indemnification obligations of the Company and Boeing for breaches of representations and warranties, post-Closing covenants and certain other specified matters, subject to certain limitations, including caps on their indemnification obligations.. The parties have obtained a buy-side representation and warranty insurance policy, bound prior to, or contemporaneously with, the date of the Purchase Agreement (the “RWI Policy”). In connection with claims by the Company for indemnification under the Purchase Agreement for breach of representations and warranties, the Company must seek payment from the RWI Policy for so long as and to the extent that the RWI Policy is available. Termination Rights The Purchase Agreement may be terminated: (i) by mutual written consent; (ii) by either the Company or Boeing if the Closing has not occurred on or before May 9, 2027 (the “Termination Date”) (provided that the Company and Boeing each have a right to extend the Termination Date by three months if all conditions other than the Regulatory Condition have been satisfied or are capable of being satisfied at the Closing); (iii) by either the Company or Boeing following certain uncured material breaches; (iv) by Boeing if the Company fails to close after all of the mutual closing conditions and conditions to the Company’s obligation to close have been and continue to be satisfied or are capable of being satisfied; (v) by either the Company or Boeing if a final and non-appealable order permanently prohibits the Transactions; and (vi) by Boeing if the Company’s enterprise value (as calculated in accordance with the Purchase Agreement) is below a minimum level for a specified period; provided that such termination right will not be available to Boeing following the date that is five business days after the date on which the Company notifies Boeing in writing that such event has occurred. The Purchase Agreement is filed as Exhibit 2.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference. The above description of the terms of the Purchase Agreement is qualified in its entirety by reference to such exhibit.
