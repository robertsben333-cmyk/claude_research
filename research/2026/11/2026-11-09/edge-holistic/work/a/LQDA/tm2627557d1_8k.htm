Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

 

Settlement Agreement

 

On October 13, 2026 (the “Effective Date”), Liquidia Technologies, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the “Company”), a wholly-owned subsidiary of Liquidia Corporation, and United Therapeutics Corporation (“UTC”) entered into a settlement agreement (the “Settlement Agreement”) regarding all ongoing litigation between the Company and UTC, including, among other matters, the resolution of pending patent infringement litigations relating to U.S. Patent No. 11,824,327, U.S. Patent No. 11,357,782 and U.S. Patent No. 10,989,494 and the trade secret and breach of contract litigation pending in the North Carolina Business Court (collectively, the “Litigations”). 

 

Under the terms of the Settlement Agreement, the Company will continue to market, sell and promote YUTREPIA® (treprostinil) inhalation powder for the treatment of pulmonary arterial hypertension (“PAH”). In exchange, the Company will (a) file with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (“FDA”) to amend the current product label of YUTREPIA® to remove pulmonary hypertension associated with interstitial lung disease (“PH-ILD”) as an indicated treatment as well as any reference to PH-ILD, (b) immediately and permanently cease, desist, and terminate any and all activities relating to the marketing, advertising, promotion, and detailing of YUTREPIA® for the treatment of PH-ILD, and (c) (i) make a net payment to UTC of $175.0 million, due within thirty (30) business days of the Effective Date after taking into account payment by UTC to the Company to settle its ongoing litigation with Liquidia PAH, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“Liquidia PAH”) and wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, formerly known as RareGen, LLC (“RareGen”), and (ii) make a payment to UTC of an aggregate of $145.4 million, to be paid in two equal installments on or before January 31, 2027 and April 30, 2027, respectively (such payments, collectively, the “UTC Payments”).

 

The Settlement Agreement also provides that each party releases the other party and their respective affiliates from any claims arising at any time up to and including the Effective Date, including relating to the Litigations (the “Releases”). The Settlement Agreement became effective upon execution, but the Releases will become effective only upon the satisfaction of certain conditions, including (a) the Company’s payment of the UTC Payments, (b) the filing of dismissals with prejudice of the Litigations with the applicable courts, and (c) the Company’s receipt of FDA approval to remove the PH-ILD indication from YUTREPIA’s® product label. In addition, the Settlement Agreement contains customary covenants not to sue the other party in certain circumstances and provides for the dismissal of the Litigations with prejudice. Each party will continue to bear its own costs, expenses and attorneys’ fees in connection with the Litigations and the negotiation and execution of the Settlement Agreement.

 

As a requirement under the Settlement Agreement, the Company was required to secure a settlement of the antitrust and breach of contract proceedings brought by Sandoz and Liquidia PAH against UTC and Smiths Medical ASD, Inc. (the “RareGen Litigation”). Accordingly, the Company entered into agreements with each of Sandoz Inc. (“Sandoz”), Henderson SPV, LLC (“Henderson”) and PBM RG Holdings, LLC (“PBM”), who have interests in the RareGen Litigation, as described below.

 

Sandoz Agreement

 

On October 11, 2026, the Company entered into a Settlement and Termination Agreement with Sandoz (the “Sandoz Agreement”) to settle Sandoz’s entitlement to a fifty percent (50%) share of the proceeds from the RareGen Litigation pursuant to that certain Promotion Agreement, dated as of August 1, 2018 (the “Promotion Agreement”), by and between Sandoz and RareGen. Under the Sandoz Agreement, Liquidia Technologies will make a payment of $21.75 million (the “Sandoz Payment”) into escrow no later than three (3) business days after the date of the Sandoz Agreement, which amount will be released to Sandoz upon Sandoz’s delivery into escrow of the documents necessary to dismiss the RareGen Litigation with prejudice and the entry by the Company and/or its affiliates into the Settlement Agreement. Upon payment of the Sandoz Payment to Sandoz, Sandoz’s entire interest in the RareGen Litigation will be assigned to Liquidia Technologies, the Promotion Agreement will terminate, except for certain other payment obligations owed by Sandoz to Liquidia PAH with respect to activities conducted during the third quarter of 2026, and Liquidia Technologies will have the sole and exclusive right to control the RareGen Litigation for the purpose of effecting its dismissal with prejudice, with each party bearing its own attorneys’ fees, costs and expenses.

 

 

 

 

Henderson Agreement

 

Additionally, on October 8, 2026, Liquidia Technologies and Liquidia PAH entered into a Settlement and Termination Agreement with Henderson (the “Henderson Agreement”), which fixes the amount payable to Henderson in complete satisfaction of its economic rights under that certain Litigation Financing Agreement, dated as of June 4, 2020, by and between Henderson and RareGen, pursuant to which Henderson funded a portion of Liquidia PAH’s legal and litigation expenses in the RareGen Litigation in exchange for a share of certain litigation or settlement proceeds (the “Financing Agreement”). Under the Henderson Agreement, Liquidia Technologies and Liquidia PAH are jointly and severally obligated to pay Henderson $19.05 million (the “Henderson Payment”) no later than three (3) business days after the Company and/or its affiliates enter into the Settlement Agreement. The Financing Agreement, and all of Henderson’s related rights, remedies, claims, liens and priorities, remain in full force and effect until that amount is paid in full to Henderson, at which time the Financing Agreement will terminate. The Henderson Agreement will also terminate if the Settlement Agreement is not executed or does not include a settlement of the RareGen Litigation.

 

PBM Agreement

 

Also, on October 10, 2026, Liquidia Technologies and Liquidia PAH entered into a Settlement and Termination Agreement with PBM (the “PBM Agreement”), which fixes the amount payable to PBM in complete satisfaction of its economic rights under the Litigation Funding and Indemnification Agreement, dated as of November 17, 2020 (the “Indemnification Agreement”), by and between PBM and RareGen, under which PBM controls the RareGen Litigation, reimburses Liquidia PAH’s litigation expenses not reimbursed by Henderson and is entitled to the proceeds of the RareGen Litigation to which Henderson is not entitled. Under the PBM Agreement, Liquidia Technologies and Liquidia PAH are jointly and severally obligated to pay PBM $19.45 million (the “PBM Payment” and together with the UTC Payments, the Sandoz Payment and the Henderson Payment, the “Settlement Payments”) no later than three (3) business days after the Company and/or its affiliates enter into the Settlement Agreement with UTC, which payment will constitute full payment of all amounts owing to PBM under the Indemnification Agreement, and the Indemnification Agreement will terminate upon receipt of that payment, other than provisions that expressly survive termination. PBM is a related party of the Company because it is controlled by Paul B. Manning, a stockholder of the Company who beneficially owns at least five percent (5%) of the Company’s outstanding common stock and is also a member of the Company’s Board of Directors (the “Board”), and it is also owned by Roger Jeffs, the Company’s Chief Executive Officer and a member of the Board, Damian deGoa, a member of the Board, and Russell Schundler, the Company’s General Counsel. The PBM Agreement was reviewed and approved by the disinterested members of the Board.

 

Seventh Amendment to Revenue Interest Financing Agreement

 

As previously disclosed, on January 9, 2023, the Company, entered into a Revenue Interest Financing Agreement with HealthCare Royalty Partners IV, L.P. (“HCR”), as amended by that certain First Amendment to Revenue Interest Financing Agreement, dated as of April 17, 2023, as amended by that certain Second Amendment to Revenue Interest Financing Agreement, dated as of June 28, 2023, as amended by that certain Third Amendment to Revenue Interest Financing Agreement, dated as of July 27, 2023, as amended by that certain Fourth Amendment to Revenue Interest Financing Agreement, dated as of January 3, 2024, as amended by that certain Fifth Amendment to Revenue Interest Financing Agreement, dated as of September 11, 2024, and as further amended by that certain Sixth Amendment to Revenue Interest Financing Agreement, dated as of March 17, 2025 (as amended, the “Financing Agreement”).

 

On October 13, 2026, the Company and HCR entered into a Seventh Amendment to the Financing Agreement (the “Seventh Amendment”), pursuant to which HCR agreed to provide its consent for the Company to enter into a letter of credit with a third-party lender in connection with the Settlement Agreement. As consideration for such consent, the Company has agreed to increase the minimum cash covenant under the Financing Agreement from $15.0 million to $45.0 million.

 

The foregoing descriptions of certain terms of the Settlement Agreement, the Sandoz Agreement, the Henderson Agreement, the PBM Agreement and the Seventh Amendment do not purport to be complete, are intended to be summaries of the material terms of such agreements and each is qualified in its entirety by reference to the complete text of each such agreement, which are filed hereto as Exhibit 10.1, Exhibit 10.2, Exhibit 10.3, Exhibit 10.4 and Exhibit 10.5 respectively. 

 

 

 

 

Item 1.02 Termination of a Material Definitive Agreement.

 

The information set forth in Item 1.01 of this Current Report on Form 8-K regarding the Sandoz Agreement, the Henderson Agreement and the PBM Agreement is incorporated by reference into this Item 1.02. Each such agreement provides for the termination, in whole or in part, of an existing agreement to which one or more of the Company’s subsidiaries is a party. None of the terminations described below are effective upon execution of the applicable settlement and termination agreement. In each case, the termination occurs only upon payment of the applicable settlement amount in full, and in the case of the Promotion Agreement, only after the Sandoz Agreement has become effective in accordance with its terms.

 

Promotion Agreement

 

Under the Promotion Agreement, Liquidia PAH conducts promotional and related commercial activities for Sandoz’s generic treprostinil injection (“Treprostinil Injection”) in the United States in exchange for a tiered share of net profits from sales of Treprostinil Injection, and all proceeds of the RareGen Litigation are divided evenly between Sandoz and Liquidia PAH. Under the Sandoz Agreement, the Promotion Agreement will terminate with respect to the RareGen Litigation upon payment of the Sandoz Payment to Sandoz, except for certain other payment obligations owed by Sandoz to Liquidia PAH with respect to activities during the third quarter of 2026.

 

Financing Agreement

 

Under the Financing Agreement, Henderson funded $10.0 million of Liquidia PAH’s legal and litigation expenses in the RareGen Litigation in exchange for a share of certain litigation or settlement proceeds, and Henderson holds liens on, and priority rights in, those proceeds. Under the Henderson Agreement, the Financing Agreement will terminate upon payment of the Henderson Payment to Henderson, which will constitute full payment of all amounts owing to Henderson under the Financing Agreement.

 

Indemnification Agreement

 

Under the Indemnification Agreement, PBM retains the sole and exclusive right to control the RareGen Litigation, funds the counsel fees and litigation expenses of Liquidia PAH that are not funded by Henderson, is entitled to the litigation proceeds remaining after amounts payable to Henderson, and holds a security interest in the rights of Liquidia PAH in the RareGen Litigation and the proceeds thereof that is subordinated to the security interest of Henderson. Further, the Indemnification Agreement provides that Liquidia PAH and its affiliates are not entitled to any proceeds of, and do not bear any financial or other liability for, the RareGen Litigation unless PBM defaults. Under the PBM Agreement, the Indemnification Agreement will terminate upon payment of the PBM Payment to PBM, which will constitute full payment of all amounts owing to PBM under the Indemnification Agreement.

 

The foregoing descriptions of the termination provisions of the Sandoz Agreement, the Henderson Agreement and the PBM Agreement, and of the Promotion Agreement, the Financing Agreement and the Indemnification Agreement, do not purport to be complete and are qualified in their entirety by reference to the complete text of each such agreement.
