Results of Annual General Meeting

Summary by AI BETAClose X

Kainos Group plc announced that all resolutions proposed at its 2026 Annual General Meeting were carried by poll vote, with resolutions 1 to 11 passing as ordinary resolutions and resolutions 12 to 15 as special resolutions. Notably, the audited accounts and financial statements received 99.99% of votes for, and the proposed final dividend of 19.8 pence per ordinary share was approved with 99.99% of votes for. The re-election of directors and the re-appointment of KPMG as auditor also saw strong support, with votes for generally exceeding 97% of the issued share capital voted.
