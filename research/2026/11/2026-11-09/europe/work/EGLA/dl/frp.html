About EuroGroup Laminations S.p.A.

https://www.eglagroup.com/

EuroGroup Laminations designs, produces and distributes laminations, stators and rotors for electric motors, generators and transformers, made from blanked electrical steel, and operates 15 plants worldwide. In 2025 revenue was €831 million: E-mobility solutions for electric vehicles brought €514 million (about 62 percent) and Industrial & Infrastructure solutions €317 million (about 38 percent). A planned takeover by FountainVest was abandoned in February 2026, and the company remains listed.
