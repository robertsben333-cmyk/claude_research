    AGREEMENT SIGNED WITH THE TOSANO GROUP FOR THE LEASE OF 15 GS BUSINESS UNITS
    (HYPERMARKETS AND LARGE-FORMAT STORES), WITH SUBSEQUENT PURCHASE OPTIONS
•   The transaction provides for the lease to the Tosano Group, by the subsidiary GS S.p.A., of 15
    business units (hypermarkets and large-format stores), with subsequent purchase options,
    and the enhancement of 13 properties owned by GS, against lease payments of Euro 110 million
    over a five-year period (in addition to Euro 5 million as consideration for the granting of the
    options), Euro 35 million as the price for the business units, a total price for the properties of
    Euro 248.6 million, as well as use fees of Euro 54.4 million under the rent-to-buy agreement.
•   Management estimates an annual EBITDA improvement of approximately Euro 40 million in
    the first five years and approximately Euro 60.2 million in the following seven years, subject
    to full implementation of the transaction in accordance with the plan assumptions.
•   The agreement aims to structurally strengthen the profitability and cash generation of GS
    S.p.A., with expected positive effects on the consolidated economic and financial results of the
    NewPrinces Group.
•   The businesses involved currently generate annual revenues of approximately Euro 500
    million, equal to approximately 14% of the total turnover of GS S.p.A.
•   With the transaction, NewPrinces considers the first phase of the rationalisation programme
    for GS's commercial perimeter to be complete, launching a new development phase focused
    on supermarkets and the Express format.


Reggio Emilia, 5 November 2026 – NewPrinces S.p.A. ("NewPrinces" or the "Company") announces that
its subsidiary GS S.p.A. ("GS") has entered into an agreement with NOVA RETAIL s.r.l., whose sole
shareholder is Supermercati Tosano Cerea S.r.l. (the "Tosano Group"), concerning the lease by GS to the
Tosano Group of 15 business units consisting of hypermarkets and large-format stores, with provision for
subsequent purchase options, as well as the enhancement of 13 properties owned by GS included in the
contractual perimeter (the "Transaction").
The Transaction is structured in an initial five-year lease phase for the business units and, subject to the
exercise of the contractually provided options, a subsequent phase involving their acquisition and the
enhancement of the properties through a rent-to-buy programme lasting a further seven years.
The Transaction represents a significant step in the implementation of the strategic programme for the
rationalisation and enhancement of GS's commercial activities, launched by the NewPrinces Group
following the acquisition of Carrefour Italia, and is aimed at improving operating profitability,
strengthening cash generation and optimising the portfolio of commercial and real estate assets.
Based on management's current forecasts, the Transaction is expected to result in an EBITDA
improvement of approximately Euro 40 million per year in the first five years and approximately
Euro 60.2 million per year in the following seven years.


Main terms and conditions of the Transaction
The agreement provides for a two-phase contractual structure, with different execution scenarios and
related consideration.


Phase One – Lease of the business units
Phase One provides for the lease of the 15 business units to the Tosano Group for a period of five years,
against contractually agreed lease payments totalling Euro 110 million, in addition to Euro 5 million as
consideration for the granting of the contractual options.
The agreement grants the Tosano Group an early withdrawal right, exercisable by notice to be given by
30 April 2029, effective from 15 March 2031, with the progressive return of the businesses in accordance
with the contractually established procedures.
Should this right be exercised, the total consideration payable to GS would amount to Euro 75 million, of
which Euro 70 million relates to lease payments and Euro 5 million to the granting of the options.
In addition to this consideration, as a separate component, there is the price of the transferable goods
held in the stores on the respective transfer dates. Based on management's estimate, the total value of
the potential gross flows attributable to the early withdrawal scenario would amount to approximately
Euro 75 million plus the value of the goods, subject to the actual valuation of the goods by inventory and
the related payment terms.
Should the relationship continue for the entire five-year term, the total consideration payable to GS would
amount to Euro 125 million, broken down as follows:
•   Euro 110 million as lease payments for the business units;
•   Euro 5 million as consideration for the granting of the contractual options;
•   A further Euro 10 million, the classification of which will depend on the exercise of the purchase
    options.
In particular, if the options are exercised, the amount of Euro 10 million will constitute a first instalment of
the purchase price of the business units, payable on the completion date of the related acquisition.
If the options are not exercised, the same amount will instead be payable as additional consideration for
the use of GS's properties during the lease period.
The above alternative components are not cumulative.
Phase One also includes the sale of the goods held in the 15 hypermarkets and large-format stores at the
time of their respective handovers. Assuming completion of the five-year period and exercise of the
options, the Phase One consideration would therefore represent potential gross flows of Euro 125 million
plus the value of the goods, it being understood that receipts for the goods are settled separately.
The agreements also provide for additional obligations on the part of the Tosano Group, as well as the
assumption by the lessee of ordinary and extraordinary maintenance costs, in accordance with the
contractually established terms.


Phase Two – Sale of the business units to the Tosano Group and real estate enhancement
If the options are exercised by 30 June 2032, the Tosano Group will acquire the business units and a
seven-year rent-to-buy programme will be launched for the 13 GS-owned properties included in the
Transaction perimeter.
The total consideration for the acquisition of the business units is Euro 35 million, of which Euro 10 million
corresponds to the initial instalment included in the Phase One consideration and Euro 25 million to a
deferred balance.
Phase Two also provides for:
•   Euro 280.4 million as payments under the rent-to-buy real estate programme, including the
    components relating to the use of the properties and advance payments on the purchase price;
•   Euro 22.6 million as the final balance of the price of the properties, should the purchase be
    completed on the expiry date of 30 June 2039.
The total agreed price for the properties is Euro 248.6 million, from which the advance payments made
during the rent-to-buy programme will be deducted.
Therefore, assuming full execution of the agreements and exercise of the options provided for, the total
potential consideration of the Transaction amounts to Euro 453 million (in addition to the value of the
goods), of which Euro 125 million relates to Phase One and Euro 328 million to the subsequent acquisition
and real estate enhancement phase.
The agreement also provides for consideration adjustment mechanisms in the event of changes to the
Transaction perimeter and specific provisions regarding the possible early purchase of the properties.

Expected economic and financial effects: strengthening of EBITDA, earnings and cash generation
The 15 business units involved in the Transaction currently generate total annual turnover of
approximately Euro 500 million, corresponding to approximately 14% of GS's annual turnover of
approximately Euro 3.5 billion.
The Transaction will entail the transfer of the operational management of these businesses to the Tosano
Group, resulting in a reduction in consolidated revenues attributable to the perimeter concerned, against
an expected improvement in the Group's profitability profile and cash generation capacity.
Based on management's current assessments, the annual EBITDA improvement resulting from the
Transaction is estimated at approximately Euro 40 million during the initial five-year phase and
approximately Euro 60.2 million during the subsequent seven-year phase, assuming
implementation of the plan under the expected conditions.
The estimates reflect the expected economic effects of the transfer of the management of the large-
format stores and of the execution of the contractual agreements in their respective phases.
From a financial perspective, GS will benefit directly from the flows arising from the consideration
provided for under the agreements, initially through the lease payments for the business units and
subsequently, if the options are exercised, through the consideration for the acquisition of the commercial
businesses and the rent-to-buy real estate programme.
The sale to the Tosano Group of the goods held in the 15 stores at the time of their respective transfers is
also envisaged, in accordance with the contractually established eligibility and valuation criteria and on
the basis of jointly conducted inventories. The total value of the goods will represent an additional
component, separate from the potential contractual value of the Transaction of Euro 453 million. The
monetisation of inventories is expected to contribute positively to cash generation and the release of
working capital, mainly in 2027, depending on the actual handover dates and the payment terms provided
for in the agreement.
For the purposes of the economic representation of the Transaction, the estimated value of the goods is
an additional component to the contractual consideration; potential gross flows amount to Euro 75 million
(plus the value of the goods) in the specific early withdrawal scenario, Euro 125 million plus the value of
the goods for the completed Phase One with exercise of the options, and Euro 453 million plus the value
of the goods as the indicative total value in the full execution scenario. These flows are expected to
contribute positively to the subsidiary's cash generation and, consequently, to the consolidated financial
position of the NewPrinces Group. The improvement in operating profitability, together with the
enhancement of assets and the contractual consideration, is also expected to have a positive impact on
GS's future results, with effects on the Group's consolidated earnings, depending on the nature and
accounting treatment of the respective components. The combination of these factors is aimed at
achieving a structural improvement in the economic and financial profile of the retail businesses, through
greater profitability, higher cash generation capacity and more efficient capital allocation. The actual
realisation of the expected benefits will depend on the timing of execution of the Transaction,
developments in operating conditions and the accounting treatment of the individual contractual
components.


Euromercato brand
The agreements also provide for the licensing of the Euromercato brand, owned by GS.
Strategic rationale and completion of the first phase of the rationalisation plan
The Transaction forms part of NewPrinces' strategy aimed at rationalising GS's commercial portfolio,
improving profitability and strengthening the cash generation capacity of the retail businesses. The
decision to entrust the management of the 15 business units to the Tosano Group reflects the intention to
enhance the value of the large-format stores through a specialised operator with an established
distribution model in the hypermarket segment. For the Tosano Group, the Transaction represents an
opportunity to expand its distribution network and strengthen its presence nationwide. For NewPrinces,
the agreement makes it possible to optimise the perimeter of its commercial activities, improving their
economic and financial profile while enhancing the value of the real estate assets involved, consistent with
the Group's strategy of rationalisation and strengthening of profitability. With the finalisation of the
agreement, NewPrinces considers the first phase of its strategic programme for the rationalisation
and enhancement of GS's commercial perimeter to be complete.
The completion of this phase will enable the Group to progressively focus its managerial and financial
resources on developing the store formats identified as priorities for GS's future positioning.


Development prospects and relaunch of the GS brand
As part of the next phase of the business plan, NewPrinces intends to focus the development strategy of
the GS brand on supermarkets with sales areas of between 1,000 and 5,000 square metres and on the
GS Express format.
The programme provides for the progressive strengthening of the distribution network, with particular
focus on the expansion of the Express format in the convenience segment, as well as the modernisation
and refurbishment of existing stores.
The initiatives will be aimed at improving operational efficiency, commercial productivity and the shopping
experience, as well as strengthening the identity and positioning of the GS brand.
The greater focus on priority formats is aimed at supporting network growth, improving its profitability
profile and cash generation capacity over the medium to long term.

Group represents a particularly significant step in the implementation of our strategy to transform our
retail businesses and marks the completion of the first phase of the rationalisation programme for GS's
commercial perimeter.
"With this Transaction, we are entrusting a specialised industrial partner with the management of 15 large-
format stores that currently generate annual turnover of approximately Euro 500 million, launching a
commercial and real estate enhancement process aimed at structurally improving the Group's profitability
and cash generation capacity.
"The agreement has a total potential contractual value of Euro 453 million and, based on our current
estimates, is expected to result in an EBITDA improvement of approximately Euro 40 million per
year in the first five years and approximately Euro 60.2 million per year in the following seven
years, in line with the plan implementation assumptions.
"The Transaction is also aimed at strengthening GS's cash generation through a multi-year programme of
contractual consideration, with expected positive effects on the Group's economic and financial profile.
"We believe this agreement confirms our commitment to prioritising earnings quality, discipline in capital
allocation and cash generation over mere growth in size.
"I would particularly like to highlight the value of the Tosano Group, which we recognise as having
distinctive expertise in the management of large hypermarkets. Over time, Tosano has built a commercial
and operating model of extraordinary effectiveness, demonstrating in the field its management capability,
efficiency, strength of offering and customer focus precisely in the most complex formats of large-scale
retail. In our view, it is a benchmark in Italy for this type of store: it is difficult to imagine a more qualified
industrial partner to fully unlock the potential of these 15 hypermarkets. Our choice stems from the
conviction that the stores entrusted to Tosano will benefit from its experience, execution capability and
the strength of its distribution model, creating value for customers, for local communities and for both
companies.
"With the completion of the first phase of our rationalisation programme, we can now focus our resources
on the relaunch and development of the GS brand, by strengthening the supermarket network and the
Express format, alongside a programme of store expansion and modernisation.
"Our goal is to build a more efficient, competitive and profitable distribution platform, capable of
contributing increasingly to cash generation and sustainable long-term value creation."

NEWPRINCES GROUP
NewPrinces Group is a leading Italian group operating in the food & beverage and retail sectors, with an
integrated industrial and distribution platform, which generated pro forma revenues of approximately
Euro 6.5 billion in 2025.

platforms, with a network of over 1,000 stores nationwide.

operational excellence and a long-term industrial vision, positioning itself as one of the leading players in
the European food ecosystem.
