PART III — NARRATIVE

State below in reasonable detail why Forms 10-K, 20-F, 11-K, 10-Q, 10-D, N-CEN, N-CSR, or the transition report or portion thereof, could not be filed within the prescribed time period.

Vylor Inc. (“Vylor” or the “Company”) is unable to file its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarterly period ended September 30, 2026 by the prescribed due date without unreasonable effort or expense as the Company requires additional time to complete certain processes and procedures related to the required reporting resulting from the spin-off of the Company from Corteva, Inc. (“Corteva”) completed on October 1, 2026 (the “Separation”). Corteva is also unable to file its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q due to the Separation.

The Company plans to file the Form 10-Q no later than the fifth calendar day following the prescribed due date, pursuant to Rule 12b-25(b) under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended.

Vylor will be treated as the “accounting spinnor” in the Separation and therefore will be the “accounting successor” to Corteva following the spin-off. Vylor’s financial statements for the quarter ending September 30, 2026 will be the consolidated interim financial statements of Corteva. Vylor anticipates its financial results for the quarter ending September 30, 2026 will differ significantly from the same period in the prior year due to changes in its expenses. Higher expenses are expected primarily from the activities required to complete the Separation and on-going investments within research and development. As a result, the Company expects a higher seasonal loss as compared to the same period in 2025.

Vylor expects to issue financial results in advance of the scheduled earnings call on November 12, 2026.
