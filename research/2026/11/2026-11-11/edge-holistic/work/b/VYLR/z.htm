PART III — NARRATIVE

State below in reasonable detail why Forms 10-K, 20-F, 11-K, 10-Q, 10-D, N-CEN, N-CSR, or the transition report or portion thereof, could not be filed within the prescribed time period.

Corteva, Inc. and EIDP, Inc. (the “Companies”) are unable to file their Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarterly period ended September 30, 2026 by the prescribed due date without unreasonable effort or expense as the Companies require additional time to complete certain processes and procedures related to the required reporting resulting from the spin-off of the Companies’ seed operating segment completed on October 1, 2026 (the “Separation”). The Companies’ spin-off, Vylor Inc. (“Vylor”) is also unable to file its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q due to the Separation.

The Companies plan to file the Form 10-Q no later than the fifth calendar day following the prescribed due date, pursuant to Rule 12b-25(b) under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended.

The Companies anticipate their financial results for the quarter ending September 30, 2026 will differ significantly from the same period in the prior year due to changes in their expenses.
