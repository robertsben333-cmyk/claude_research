TENDER OFFER AND POST-OFFER COOPERATION AGREEMENT

This TENDER OFFER AND POST-OFFER COOPERATION AGREEMENT (this “Agreement”) is entered into as of November 9, 2026, by and between DoubleU Games Co., Ltd., a company incorporated under the laws of the Republic of Korea (“DUG”), having its registered office at 16F, GFC, 152, Teheran-ro, Gangnam-gu, Seoul, Republic of Korea (06236), and DoubleDown Interactive Co., Ltd., a company incorporated under the laws of the Republic of Korea (“DDI” and, together with DUG, the “Parties” and each a “Party”), having its registered office at 13F, GFC, 152, Teheran-ro, Gangnam-gu, Seoul, Republic of Korea (06236).

RECITALS

WHEREAS, DUG owns approximately 67.05% of the issued and outstanding common shares, par value KRW 10,000 per share, of DDI (the “Common Shares”), and the remaining Common Shares are held entirely in the form of American Depositary Shares (“ADSs”) listed on the Nasdaq Global Select Market (“Nasdaq”) under the symbol “DDI,” by holders unaffiliated with DUG;

WHEREAS, each ADS represents one-twentieth (1/20) of one Common Share (i.e., twenty (20) ADSs represent one (1) Common Share);

WHEREAS, DUG desires to acquire, pursuant to the Offer, all of the ADSs not owned by DUG or its Affiliates for cash, to acquire the remaining Common Shares (held in the form of ADSs) pursuant to the Post-Offer Transaction, and thereafter to cause DDI to (i) become a wholly owned subsidiary of DUG, (ii) cause the ADSs to be delisted from Nasdaq and deregistered under the Exchange Act and (iii) DDI’s reporting obligations under the Exchange Act to be terminated;

WHEREAS, a special committee of the board of directors of DDI (the “Board”) created by the Board pursuant to a Board resolution delegating certain powers and authority of the Board to such special committee, composed solely of independent and disinterested directors (the “Special Committee”), advised by its own independent legal counsel and financial advisor, has negotiated the terms of this Agreement and the Transactions with DUG;

WHEREAS, the Special Committee has received and provided (on a non-reliance basis and for informational purposes only) to the Board, the written opinion of Kroll, LLC, operating through its Duff & Phelps Opinions Practice, as an independent financial advisor to the Special Committee (the “Financial Advisor”), to the effect that, as of the date of such opinion and based on and subject to the assumptions, qualifications, limitations and other matters set forth therein, the Final Offer Price to be received by the Unaffiliated Holders is fair, from a financial point of view, to the Unaffiliated Holders (the “Fairness Opinion”);

WHEREAS, the Special Committee has recommended that the Board approve this Agreement, and the Board, after considering such recommendation and the Fairness Opinion and in consultation with its advisors has, following disclosure of all material facts relating to this Agreement and the Transactions and by the affirmative vote of at least two-thirds (2/3rd) of all directors in office in accordance with Article 398 of the KCA, unanimously approved this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby and resolved to recommend that Unaffiliated Holders accept the Offer and tender their ADSs, in accordance with this Agreement and the terms herein and in the Offer Documents;

WHEREAS, DUG will file with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) a Tender Offer Statement on Schedule TO, DUG and DDI will jointly file a Rule 13e-3 Transaction Statement on Schedule 13E-3, which may be combined with the Schedule TO, and DDI will file a Solicitation/Recommendation Statement on Schedule 14D-9; and

WHEREAS, following completion of the Offer, DUG intends to acquire the remaining Common Shares (including Common Shares represented by ADSs) through a second-step transaction under the Korean Commercial Act (the “KCA”) as provided in Article 6.
