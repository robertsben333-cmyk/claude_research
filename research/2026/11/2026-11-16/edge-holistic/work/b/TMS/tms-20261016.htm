Item 1.01.     Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement

On October 16, 2026 (the “Closing Date”), Teamshares Continuity Holdings LLC (the “Borrower”), an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Teamshares Inc. (the “Company”), entered into a Credit Agreement (the “Credit Agreement”) with the Company and Teamshares LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company and the direct parent of the Borrower (“Teamshares LLC”), each as a guarantor, the subsidiaries of the Borrower party thereto as subsidiary guarantors (the “Subsidiary Guarantors”), the lenders party thereto (the “Lenders”), and Sound Point Agency LLC, as administrative agent and collateral agent (the “Agent”).

The Credit Agreement established a senior secured term loan facility in an aggregate principal amount of $130.0 million (the “Term Loan”), which was funded in full on the Closing Date. The Term Loan matures on the fourth anniversary of the Closing Date. The Borrower must make quarterly principal payments on the last business day of each March, June, September and December, beginning December 31, 2026. Each required quarterly payment is (i) for the last business day of each fiscal quarter ending December 31, 2026 and on or before September 30, 2027, $6.25 million, (ii) for the last business day of each fiscal quarter ending December 31, 2027 and on or before September 30, 2028, $7.50 million, (iii) for the last business day of each fiscal quarter ending December 31, 2028 and on or before September 30, 2029, $8,75 million and (iv) for the last business day of each fiscal quarter ending December 31, 2029 and on or before September 30, 2030, $10.0 million.

The Borrower plans to use the proceeds of the Term Loan, together with cash on hand and restricted cash released in connection with the transaction, to repay in full all outstanding obligations under the Existing Credit Agreement (as defined in Item 1.02 below) and to repay additional existing indebtedness.

The Term Loan bears interest at a rate equal to (i) one-month Term SOFR (subject to a floor of 3.00%) plus (ii) 9.50% per annum, payable monthly in arrears in cash. The Borrower may voluntarily prepay the Term Loan in whole or in part on three business days’ notice, without premium or penalty. The Borrower must prepay the Term Loan with the net proceeds of certain asset sales within ten business days of receipt, subject to the right to reinvest those proceeds within 90 days (or 180 days if the Borrower or applicable Subsidiary Guarantor enters into a legally binding commitment to invest such asset sale proceeds within 90 days of receipt thereof).

The Company, Teamshares LLC and the Subsidiary Guarantors have guaranteed the Borrower’s obligations under the Credit Agreement. The Subsidiary Guarantors consist of the Borrower’s material subsidiaries. Additional material subsidiaries must join as guarantors under the Credit Agreement after each calendar quarter, subject to customary limitations. The obligations under the Credit Agreement are secured by a first-priority lien on substantially all assets of the Borrower and the Subsidiary Guarantors, and by a pledge by (i) the Company of its equity interests in Teamshares LLC and (ii) Teamshares LLC of its equity interests in the Borrower, in each case subject to customary exceptions.

The Credit Agreement contains certain customary affirmative and negative covenants limiting the ability of the Borrower and the Subsidiary Guarantors to incur additional indebtedness, grant liens, make investments, dispose of assets, merge or consolidate, and enter into transactions with affiliates, in each case subject to certain exceptions and qualifications. The Company may not pay dividends or other distributions on its equity, or repurchase its equity or rights to acquire its equity, without the Agent’s consent. The Credit Agreement also requires compliance with certain financial covenants, measured as of the end of each fiscal quarter. These financial covenants include a maximum net leverage, minimum EBITDA yield, free cash flow yield, and minimum liquidity tests.

The Credit Agreement contains customary events of default, including nonpayment of amounts due under the Credit Agreement, inaccuracy of representations and warranties, covenant breaches, cross-default to other indebtedness of the Company or its subsidiaries in excess of $5.0 million, bankruptcy and insolvency events, unsatisfied judgments in excess of $5.0 million, invalidity of the loan documents or liens, and a change of control.

The foregoing description of the Credit Agreement does not purport to be complete. It is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Credit Agreement, which is filed as Exhibit 10.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.

Item 1.02.    Termination of a Material Definitive Agreement.


On the Closing Date, in connection with entering into the Credit Agreement, the Borrower used the proceeds of the Term Loan, together with cash on hand and restricted cash released in connection with the transaction, to repay in full all outstanding obligations under, and terminate, the Credit Agreement, dated as of May 4, 2021, as amended, by and among the Borrower, the Company, the lenders party thereto, and Westmount Group LLC, an affiliate of i80 Group LLC, as administrative agent and collateral agent (the “Existing Credit Agreement”).

The Existing Credit Agreement established a delayed-draw term loan facility with aggregate commitments of $150.0 million. Availability was subject to a borrowing base calculated primarily by reference to the EBITDA of eligible acquired businesses whose equity was pledged as collateral. The draw period ended on June 5, 2025, and the outstanding term loans were scheduled to mature on December 5, 2026.

Borrowings bore interest at a rate equal to (i) Term SOFR (subject to a floor of 3.00%) plus (ii) 11.00% per annum. Cash interest was capped at 16.00% per annum, and any interest above that cap was paid in kind. The obligations under the Existing Credit Agreement were guaranteed by the Company. The Existing Credit Agreement was secured by substantially all assets of the Borrower, including its equity interests in the pledged businesses, and by a pledge of the equity interests in the Borrower.

The aggregate payoff amount was approximately $152.8 million. This consisted of $150.0 million of outstanding principal (including capitalized paid-in-kind interest) and $2.8 million of accrued and unpaid interest. Upon payment, all commitments under the Existing Credit Agreement were terminated. All guarantees, including the Company’s guaranty and its related conditional guaranty under the Existing Credit Agreement, were released, and all liens securing the obligations under the Existing Credit Agreement were released.

Item 3.03.    Material Modification to Rights of Security Holders.
Under the Credit Agreement, the Company may not, without the prior written consent of the Agent, pay any dividend or other distribution on its equity, or purchase, redeem, retire or otherwise acquire any of its equity or any option, warrant or other right to acquire its equity, subject to certain exceptions. In addition, the Borrower and the Subsidiary Guarantors may not make distributions to the Company other than as described in Item 1.01. These restrictions limit the Company’s ability to pay dividends on, or repurchase, its common stock and warrants while the Credit Agreement is outstanding.

The information set forth in Item 1.01 of this Current Report on Form 8-K is incorporated by reference into this Item 3.03.
