2026 Interim Results

Summary by AI BETAClose X

ME Group International PLC reported interim results for the six months ended 30 April 2026, with revenue marginally up by 0.3% to £154.3 million and EBITDA increasing by 7.1% to £57.0 million, though profit before tax decreased by 3.8% to £32.7 million. The company saw strong growth in its laundry operations, with Wash.ME revenue up 16.3% to £54.8 million, and secured a significant partnership with ASDA. Despite a slowdown in vending revenue in April, primarily due to reduced photobooth activity in some countries, the group expects full-year profit before tax to be between £69 million and £74 million. The interim dividend was reduced by 6.5% to 3.60 pence per share.
