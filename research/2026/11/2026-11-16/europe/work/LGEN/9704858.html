L&G Half Year Results 2026

Summary by AI BETAClose X

Legal & General Group Plc reported strong first-half 2026 results, with core operating profit up 7% to £918 million and core operating EPS increasing 11%, exceeding the company's target range. The company has completed approximately £450 million of its £1.2 billion buyback programme and confirmed a 2% interim dividend increase. Asset Management saw significant growth with fee-related earnings up 37%, while Institutional Retirement maintained strict pricing discipline, writing or being exclusive on £5.7 billion of global PRT. Retail business also performed well, with Workplace Pensions administered assets increasing 27% to £128 billion and total UK DC assets reaching £236 billion. The Solvency II coverage ratio remains strong at 201%.
