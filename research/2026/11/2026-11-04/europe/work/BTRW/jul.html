FY26 July Trading Update

Summary by AI BETAClose X

Barratt Redrow plc reported a solid performance for the 52 weeks ended 28 June 2026, completing 17,667 homes and achieving adjusted profit before tax in line with market expectations, despite challenging market conditions. The company is updating its capital allocation policy to return £400 million to shareholders in FY27, primarily through share buybacks, replacing ordinary dividends due to a significant discount in its share price relative to tangible net asset value. The integration of Redrow is progressing well, delivering synergies, and the company maintains a strong balance sheet with year-end net cash of approximately £772 million. For FY27, Barratt Redrow anticipates total home completions between 17,700 and 18,200.
