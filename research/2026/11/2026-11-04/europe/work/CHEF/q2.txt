Following the end of the second quarter, Cheffelo began rolling out a refreshed brand
identity across its Nordic markets.
The refresh brings the Company’s local brands closer together under the shared
promise, For all tables, while retaining their strong local relevance. Designed for a
digital-first, direct-to-consumer business, the updated identity introduces a bolder
visual expression and a more personal, fun and welcoming tone of voice across
customer touchpoints. The rollout will be supported by advertising campaigns and
updated mealkit boxes in Norway, Sweden and Denmark, with Finland to follow as the
Company prepares for its market entry.
