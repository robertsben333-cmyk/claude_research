Interim Results

Summary by AI BETAClose X

Dotdigital Group plc reported interim results for the six months ended 31 December 2025, showing forward-looking contracted ARR increased 13% to £75.4 million, with recurring revenue from the core CXDP up 11% to £37.3 million, representing 84% of total revenue. Total Group revenue rose 4% to £44.2 million, while adjusted EBITDA was £13.6 million and adjusted profit before tax was £8.9 million. Cash reserves stood at £36.1 million following the Social Snowball acquisition. Post-period, Dotdigital acquired Alia for up to $60 million, enhancing its data capture capabilities. The company remains confident in meeting full-year expectations on a constant currency basis.
