About NOBA Bank Group

With a diversified offering through our four brands – Nordax Bank, Bank Norwegian, Svensk
Hypotekspension and DBT – and over two million customers, we have the size, knowledge and
scalability required to enable financial health for more people. NOBA provides specialized, customer-
centric financial offerings that are sustainable for the individual, the SME, the bank and society at
large, today and in the future. The NOBA group has around 750 full-time employees and is active in
eight markets. As of 26 September 2025, the NOBA share is listed on Nasdaq Stockholm.

Read more about NOBA and our brands at www.noba.bank.
