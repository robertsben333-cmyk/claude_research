THIS IS NOBA
With a diversified offering through our four brands – Nordax Bank, Bank Norwe-
gian, Svensk Hypotekspension and DBT – and over two million customers, we have
the size, knowledge and scalability required to enable financial health for more
people. NOBA provides specialised, customer-centric financial offerings that are
sustainable for the individual, the SME, the bank and society at large, today and in
the future.
                                                                                       THE BANK FOR THE NEW NORMAL   A DIGITAL FRONTRUNNER

NOBA Bank Group AB (publ) (“NOBA”) has around 750 employees and is active in
eight markets. As of June 2026, lending amounted to SEK 143bn, and our custom-
ers had entrusted us with SEK 115bn in savings. Our business is growing organically
with a high and stable earnings capacity, providing us with ample opportunities
to be on the offense and expand further organically and potentially also through
future acquisitions. As of 26 September 2025, the NOBA share is listed on Nasdaq
Stockholm.
