Before I get into the details, I want to provide an update on our tariﬀ assumptions.

Let me unpack the moving pieces around tariﬀ rates and then I will walk you through how we are
factoring this into our gross margin outlook for the year, which remains largely unchanged.

Our prior outlook in May assumed 10% tariﬀ rate from February 24 through July 24 under Section 122

an additional $35 million of beneﬁt to the year.
