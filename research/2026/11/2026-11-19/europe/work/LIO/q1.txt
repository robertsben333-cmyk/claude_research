 Liontrust Asset Management Plc reported a significant increase in assets under management and advice (AuMA) to £24.3 billion as of June 30, 2026, up from £19.6 billion at the end of the previous quarter, largely driven by the acquisition of River Global Holdings Limited which added £3.0 billion. The company experienced net outflows of £357 million during the period, a notable improvement from £1.1 billion in the prior year. The acquisition is expected to enhance investment capabilities and broaden distribution, with eight River Global funds already ranking in the top two quartiles over three years.

 LIONTRUST ASSET MANAGEMENT PLC 
 Trading Update 
 Liontrust Asset Management Plc (" Liontrust " or the " Company " or the " Group "), the specialist independent active asset management group, today issues its trading update for the three months ended 30 June 2026 (the " Period ").   
 ·    Assets under management and advice (" AuMA ") were £24.3 billion as at 30 June 2026 1 (31 March 2026: £19.6 billion). 
 ·    AuMA as at 17 July 2026 were £24.0 billion 1 . 
 ·    Net outflows of £357 million in the Period (2026: £1.1 billion). 
 ·    The acquisition of River Global Holdings Limited (" RGH ") completed on 30 June 2026 and added a further £3.0 billion to AuMA. 
 Commenting, John Ions, Chief Executive Officer, said: 
 "The acquisition of River Global has increased AuMA by £3 billion, taking AuMA to over £24 billion, and expanded our investment capabilities and styles, while enhancing the breadth and depth of our investment professionals. River Global has also added several strongly performing funds to the Group, including eight ranked in the first or second quartile of their respective sectors over three years 2 . 
 We are now in a stronger position to meet the evolving needs of investors globally and further broaden our distribution and provide clients with bespoke investment solutions. The integration of our fixed income teams earlier this year has already enhanced the management of existing strategies and created opportunities to offer new funds and mandates. 
 The growing strength of our global distribution, client engagement, marketing reach and brand is reflected in recent mandate wins and improving net flow trends. Combined with our broader investment capabilities, this leaves Liontrust well placed to capitalise on the opportunities ahead."

 Key Fund Performance 
 Twenty-nine funds in the first or second quartile over 3 years: 
 1.    Seven Fixed Income funds - Strategic Bond, MA Monthly High Income, SF Corporate Bond, SF Monthly Income Bond, GF High Yield, GF Global Corporate Bond and GF SF European Corporate Bond funds. 
 2.    Eight Global equity funds - Global Dividend, Global Innovation, Global Technology, Balanced, Global Alpha, Japan Equity, Latin America and GF Global Alpha Long Short funds. 
 3.    Three European equity funds - European Dynamic, GF European Smaller Companies and GF European Strategic Equity funds. 
 4.    One UK equity fund - Edinburgh Investment Trust Plc. 
 5.    Two Multi-Asset funds - MA Explorer 35 and MA Explorer Income 60 funds. 
 6.    Eight River Global funds - Global Recovery IE, UK Recovery, Global Recovery, UK Opportunities, Global Income and Growth, UK Listed Smaller Companies, River UK Micro Cap Limited and UK Equity Income funds.
