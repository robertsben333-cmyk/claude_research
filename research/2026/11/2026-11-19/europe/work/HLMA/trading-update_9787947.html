Summary by AI BETAClose X

Halma plc has announced strong first-half progress and increased its full-year Adjusted EBIT margin guidance to 23.5%-24%, up from the previous expectation of around 22.7%. The company anticipates low double-digit percentage organic constant currency revenue growth for the full year, with photonics contributing approximately five percentage points. Order intake remains ahead of revenue and the prior year's comparable period. Halma has invested a record £515 million in six acquisitions year-to-date and completed three disposals realising approximately £83 million. The appreciation of Sterling against the US Dollar and Euro is expected to have a negative currency translation effect on group results.
