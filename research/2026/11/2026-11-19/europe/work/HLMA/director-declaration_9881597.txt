 Halma announced that its Senior Independent Director, Sharmila Nebhrajani OBE, will be appointed as a non-executive director of Genus plc with effect from 1 January 2027; the announcement includes no board resignations, no financial figures or guidance changes and therefore appears to be a governance appointment with limited disclosed near-term impact on Halma’s earnings, cash position or investor guidance.

 Halma PLC
 18 November 2026
 In accordance with UKLR 6.4.9(2), Halma plc (the ‘Company') announces that Sharmila Nebhrajani OBE, Senior Independent Director of the Company, will be appointed as a non-executive director of Genus plc with effect from 1 January 2027. 
 For further information please contact: 
 Mark Jenkins 
 Company Secretary, Halma plc 
 Tel: +44 (0)1494 721111
