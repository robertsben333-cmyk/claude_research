Capital Markets Day sets out growth drivers underpinning medium-term ambition

Summary by AI BETAClose X

PayPoint Plc hosted a Capital Markets Day to outline its medium-term growth strategy, targeting 5-8% net revenue growth annually, supported by a simplified operating model across four business units. Network Services aims for 5-10% net revenue growth, Digital Payments & Open Banking over 20% compound annual growth, Love2shop focuses on maximizing lifetime billings value, and Merchant Services targets a return to long-term profitable growth. The company reaffirmed its commitment to strong cash generation, dividends moving towards two times cover, and a £30 million annual share buyback program through March 2028, with current trading in line with expectations for FY27.
