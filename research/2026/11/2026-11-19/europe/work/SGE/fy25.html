Results for the year ended 30 September 2025

Summary by AI BETAClose X

The Sage Group plc reported strong financial results for the year ended 30 September 2025, with underlying total revenue increasing by 10% to £2,513 million and underlying operating profit growing by 17% to £600 million, leading to a 1.5 percentage point increase in the operating profit margin to 23.9%. Annualised Recurring Revenue (ARR) also saw an 11% increase to £2,574 million, driven by a 12% rise in subscription revenue to £2,093 million. The company proposed a final dividend of 14.4p per share, a 7% increase year-on-year, and announced a new share buyback program of up to £300 million, reflecting confidence in future prospects. Sage also highlighted strategic progress in its AI initiatives, including the expansion of Sage Copilot and the introduction of AI agents.
