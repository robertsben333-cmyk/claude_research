Half-year Financial Report

Summary by AI BETAClose X

British Land delivered a strong H1: Underlying profit £166m (+7%), underlying EPS 16.1p (+5%) and interim dividend 12.88p (+5%); like‑for‑like net rental growth +12% (Campuses +25%), portfolio occupancy 96.4% and total property return +3.9%; portfolio valuation rose 1.5% to £10,471m and EPRA NTA rose to 599p (+2%). Balance sheet metrics include LTV 39.6%, Group net debt/EBITDA 7.0x (improved), £2.9bn of financing activity including a £775m RCF, and £2.0bn of undrawn facilities and cash with no refinancing requirement until 2030. FY27 underlying EPS guidance upgraded to 31.0p (+7% YoY).
