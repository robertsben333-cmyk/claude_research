Executing our growth strategy

Summary by AI BETAClose X

Great Portland Estates PLC reported a strong year ending March 31, 2026, with EPRA EPS up 63.5% to 8.5 pence and net assets growing by 6.1% to 524 pence per share. The company achieved record leasing, with 88 new leases and renewals generating £70.9 million in annual rent, 10.3% above ERV, and saw development values increase by 22.2%. Asset sales totaled £490 million, exceeding book value by 2.3%, while acquisitions added to their Fitzrovia cluster. The portfolio valuation rose by 4.3% to £3.0 billion, with offices up 5.4% and rental values increasing by 5.8%. The company also announced a new £525 million revolving credit facility and confirmed its Baa2 issuer rating.
