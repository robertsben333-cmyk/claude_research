Derwent London reports strong Q3 operational progress: £39.8m of leasing YTD with open-market lettings 6.1% above December 2025 ERV and a further £1.7m under offer, reducing EPRA vacancy to 3.4% (H1: 4.4%); £290m of disposals completed or exchanged YTD with c.£135m more under offer toward a c.£400m 2026 disposal target; a £50m share buyback completed at an average price of 1,959p adding c.30p to NTA; net debt fell to £1.34bn (H1: £1.38bn) with EPRA LTV 28.2% and cash/undrawn facilities of £620m; four West End developments totalling 527,300 sq ft target ungeared IRRs of 10–15%.
