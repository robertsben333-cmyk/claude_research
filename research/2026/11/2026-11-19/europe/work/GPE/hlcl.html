Helical PLC reported significant progress in the period from April 1 to October 22, 2026, highlighted by a major letting at Brettenham House for 23,887 sq ft to FGS Global, establishing rental benchmarks exceeding £140 psf. The company is on track to complete developments at Delta Paddington and 10 King William Street by December 2026, with strong occupier interest. For its Southwark PBSA scheme, Gateway 2 approval in November will trigger a £10.2m profit payment and reimbursement of £5.3m in costs. Helical also returned approximately £17m to shareholders via a £12m B Share Scheme and a £5m share buyback following the £333m sale of 100 New Bridge Street, which generated a £31m profit. The company extended its £200m revolving credit facility to September 2029.
