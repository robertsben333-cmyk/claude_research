Our core markets represent 90% of our business and offer attractive opportunities, with strong occupational demand and constrained supply supporting further rental growth. Dividends Our dividend is semi-annual, and in line with our dividend policy, is calculated at 80% of Underlying EPS based on the most recently completed six-month period. Applying this policy, the Board have declared an interim dividend for the period ended 30 September 2026 of 12.88p per share. 11.22p will be payable as a Property Income Distribution and 1.66p will be payable as a non-Property Income Distribution. Payment will be made on Friday 15 January 2027 to shareholders on the register at close of business on Friday 4 December 2026. A Dividend Reinvestment Plan (DRIP) is provided by Equiniti Financial Services Limited, which enables the Company’s shareholders to elect to have their cash dividend payments used to purchase the Company’s shares. More information can be found at britishland.com/investors/shareholder-information/dividend-information.
