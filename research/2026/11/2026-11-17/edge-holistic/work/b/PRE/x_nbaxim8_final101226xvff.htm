IM8 NAMED OFFICIAL NUTRITION PARTNER
OF THE NBA IN AUSTRALIA AND SOUTHEAST ASIA

– IM8 will Host Fan Activation at an NBA House in the Region in 2027 –

HONG KONG, Oct. 12, 2026 – The National Basketball Association (NBA) and IM8 (US) LLC, a premium health and longevity brand, today announced a multiyear collaboration that makes IM8 the Official Nutrition Partner of the NBA in Australia and eight markets across Southeast Asia (Brunei, Cambodia, Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand and Vietnam).

1


The announcement was made at an exclusive IM8 event at Upper House in Hong Kong by IM8 co-founders David Beckham and Danny Yeung and NBA executives, who were joined by two-time NBA champion and Naismith Basketball Hall of Fame member Chris Bosh.

As the Official Nutrition Partner of the NBA in the region, IM8 will conduct a series of NBA-themed marketing campaigns and fan engagement initiatives across the nine markets. In 2027, IM8 will also host an activation at NBA House, one of the league’s signature events, where fans can experience IM8 firsthand.

IM8, owned by Prenetics Global Limited (NASDAQ: PRE) and co-founded by Beckham, was built on a foundation of elite sport. Its roster of world-class athlete ambassadors includes two-time Kia NBA Most Valuable Player and 2021 NBA Champion Giannis Antetokounmpo.

“A few years ago, IM8 was an idea David Beckham and I sketched out together, with one ambition: to build a global health brand backed by the credibility of the world’s greatest athletes,” said Yeung, CEO of Prenetics and Co-Founder of IM8. “Today, we’re standing alongside the NBA, the most iconic league on the planet and the most powerful sports brand in the region. Together, we’re bringing IM8 to approximately 660 million people across Australia and Southeast Asia, pairing large, basketball-loving communities with a growing demand for premium health and wellness, as IM8 continues to expand globally.”

“Basketball has given me everything, and I’ve seen first-hand how this game brings people together in every corner of the world,” said Antetokounmpo. “I grew up in Athens dreaming about the NBA, so bringing IM8 to basketball fans across Australia and Southeast Asia means a lot to me. Taking care of your body allows you to show up and be your best every single day.”

“Our collaboration with IM8 reflects the NBA’s commitment to promoting the importance of a healthy lifestyle in the communities where we operate,” said NBA Head of Southeast Asia and Asia Marketing Sheila Rasu. “We look forward to working with IM8 to curate new experiences for NBA fans and IM8 consumers in Australia and across Southeast Asia.”

Fans in Australia and Southeast Asia can follow the collaboration and upcoming NBA House details at www.IM8health.com and across the league’s localized social media accounts on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and X.

About the NBA
The National Basketball Association (NBA) is a global sports and media organization with the mission to inspire and connect people everywhere through the power of basketball.  Built around five professional sports leagues:  the NBA, WNBA, NBA G League, NBA 2K League and Basketball Africa League, the NBA has established a major international presence with games and programming available in 214 countries and territories in more than 50 languages, and merchandise for sale in more than 200 countries and territories on all seven continents.  NBA rosters at the start of the 2025-26 season featured a record 135 international players from a record-tying 43 countries.  The NBA’s digital assets include NBA TV, NBA.com, the NBA App and NBA League Pass.  The NBA has created one of the largest social media communities in the world, with more than 2.5 billion likes and followers globally across all leagues, team and player platforms.  The NBA’s Tap to Watch provides seamless access to local, national and global live game telecasts via the NBA App, NBA.com, third-party digital platforms and

About IM8
IM8 is a premium health and longevity brand co-founded by David Beckham and built with an elite team of scientists spanning medicine, academia and space science. Its flagship product, Daily Ultimate Essentials, delivers 90 ingredients in a single daily sachet – engineered to replace 16 different supplements in one drink – and is NSF Certified for Sport, non-GMO, vegan, free from common allergens and contains no artificial flavors, colors or sweeteners. Launched in December 2024, IM8 ships to 46 countries and has delivered more than 57 million servings. The brand is backed by a roster of global partners and ambassadors including Giannis Antetokounmpo, Aryna Sabalenka, Lily Collins, Jay Shetty, Ollie Bearman and Inter Miami CF. IM8 is a wholly owned subsidiary of Prenetics Global Limited (NASDAQ: PRE), a leading global health sciences company dedicated to advancing consumer health. To learn more, visit www.IM8health.com.
