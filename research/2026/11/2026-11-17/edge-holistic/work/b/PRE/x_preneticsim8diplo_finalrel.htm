Prenetics’ IM8 Announces Three-Time GRAMMY-Winning DJ and Producer Diplo as IM8 Global Ambassador and Shareholder in Multi-Year Partnership
— Diplo is IM8’s first Global Ambassador from music and takes a roster built in elite sport onto the world’s biggest stages
— Diplo brings a social following of more than 12 million across Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, and more than 40 million monthly listeners on Spotify
— IM8 becomes Diplo’s exclusive partner in the all-in-one nutrition and longevity categories and joins Diplo’s Run Club, named one of Fast Company’s Most Innovative Companies of 2026, across its North American season
— Joins an equity-aligned roster of David Beckham, Giannis Antetokounmpo, Aryna Sabalenka, Ollie Bearman, Jay Shetty, Lily Collins and Inter Miami CF
NEW YORK, N.Y. – November 16, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – Prenetics Global Limited (NASDAQ: PRE) (“Prenetics” or the “Company”), a leading consumer health company and parent of the AI-native direct-to-consumer wellness brand IM8, co-founded by Sir David Beckham, today announced a multi-year global partnership with three-time GRAMMY Award-winning artist, DJ and producer Diplo, founder of Diplo’s Run Club. Diplo becomes a Global Ambassador of IM8 and a shareholder in Prenetics, demonstrating his long-term conviction in the brand and the Company, and IM8 will serve as his exclusive partner in the all-in-one nutrition and longevity categories. He is IM8’s first ambassador from music, extending a roster rooted in elite sport into live music, global nightlife and the fast-growing world of social running.
The partnership started with IM8 Daily Ultimate Essentials, which Diplo took every day through a busy summer of global performances. What stuck was the simplicity: one daily serving in place of carrying and managing a collection of separate supplements across dozens of cities and time zones. It became a daily habit for balancing his high-performance lifestyle without a complex routine.
Diplo said: “Life on tour is constant chaos — moving between different cities and time zones multiple times a week really takes a toll. IM8 is so simple and it’s the one thing that’s helped my immune system in a real way. I didn’t get sick all summer and that’s never happened before. Bringing it to our Diplo’s Run Club events is a no-brainer, because that’s what it’s all about: showing up for yourself and taking care of your body even when it gets hard.”
Danny Yeung, Co-Founder and CEO of Prenetics and IM8, said: “Diplo is on the road most of the year — new time zones every few days, late nights, constant pressure on the body — and IM8 became the one constant in his routine. Nobody scripted that; it came from him. He is our first ambassador from music, and that was deliberate. Diplo takes IM8 to the stage and the start line, in front of a generation that is redefining what fitness looks like. Diplo’s Run Club is exactly where health is going: social, joyful, and built around showing up for yourself. And like every ambassador before him, Diplo is a shareholder, not a spokesperson. He owns a piece of what we are building.”


WHY THIS PARTNERSHIP MATTERS
Diplo is one of the most influential figures in global music. A three-time GRAMMY winner with 13 career nominations, he has shaped the sound of pop and dance music for two decades as a solo artist, as founder of the record label Mad Decent, and through projects including Major Lazer, Jack Ü, Silk City and LSD, producing and collaborating with artists including Beyoncé, Justin Bieber and Dua Lipa. In 2026 he broke into the top 100 most-streamed artists in the world on Spotify, where he draws more than 40 million monthly listeners, and he connects with more than 12 million followers across Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. This year he also returned to the main stage at Coachella with Major Lazer, performing for more than 80,000 fans.
That reach is young, global and live. Diplo’s audience skews toward the next generation of health-conscious consumers, who increasingly treat wellness as part of their social lives rather than separate from them. It gives IM8 its most direct route yet into music, nightlife and fitness culture, and adds a new dimension to an ambassador family that already spans football, basketball, tennis, Formula 1, wellness media, and film and television. Music is the broadest cultural channel IM8 has entered to date.
DIPLO’S RUN CLUB
Diplo’s Run Club is where that personal habit reaches the community. IM8 joined the series at its New York (October 24) and Los Angeles (November 8) stops and will be on site at the remaining North American stops of the 2026 season:
•San Diego: Saturday, November 21, 2026
•Phoenix: Saturday, January 23, 2027
Each event pairs a full-fledged 5K run with a mini music festival, including a DJ set from Diplo himself. More than half of participants in the series’ second season were running their first 5K, and Fast Company named Diplo’s Run Club one of its Most Innovative Companies of 2026. The series reflects a broader shift toward fitness as a social experience: Strava reported 3.5x growth in running clubs on its platform in 2025. The partnership builds on IM8’s existing work with running communities, including its community challenges on Strava and activations with local run clubs.
THE PARTNERSHIP
Diplo will appear across IM8’s global marketing, brand campaigns, content and product initiatives, and IM8 will be integrated into Diplo’s Run Club events. IM8 will serve as Diplo’s exclusive partner in the all-in-one nutrition and longevity categories. He joins a roster that includes co-founder Sir David Beckham, two-time NBA MVP Giannis Antetokounmpo, World No. 2 tennis player Aryna Sabalenka, Formula 1 driver Ollie Bearman, author and podcast host Jay Shetty, actress and producer Lily Collins, and Inter Miami CF, and, like every ambassador before him, he takes equity in Prenetics as part of the partnership.

1 Annualized Revenue Run-Rate ("ARR") is an operating metric calculated by multiplying IM8 revenue recognized in the applicable month by twelve and includes revenue from both subscription and one-time purchases. ARR is not a financial measure under IFRS, and is not necessarily indicative of revenue for any future period. ARR reflects orders from both new and existing customers and incorporates the effect of cancellations and non-renewals occurring on or before the applicable month; however, it does not adjust for anticipated future cancellations, non-renewals or pauses. ARR annualizes a single month of revenue and assumes that the revenue recognized in that month is maintained for the following twelve months. As a result, ARR is sensitive to the timing of promotional activity, product launches and billing cycles. In particular, the full order value of quarterly subscription plans is recognized upon shipment of the three-month supply, which increases revenue recognized in months when quarterly billings are concentrated. It may therefore differ significantly from actual future revenue.
