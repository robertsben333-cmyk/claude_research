Prenetics’ IM8 and Authentic Brands Group Announce 10-Year Exclusive Global Partnership to Create Care Bears by IM8; Authentic to Become Strategic Shareholder in Prenetics

•Care Bears by IM8 to launch, with great-tasting, science-backed gummy multivitamins for kids and adults in 1H 2027
•Enters a global gummy supplements market projected to nearly double to ~US$47.8 billion by 2030
•IM8 to serve as exclusive operating partner, with Prenetics recording all Care Bears by IM8 revenue
•To get early access, visit www.carebearsbyIM8.com

NEW YORK, N.Y. – November 5, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — Prenetics Global Limited (NASDAQ: PRE) (“Prenetics” or the “Company”), a leading consumer health company and parent of the AI-native direct-to-consumer wellness brand IM8, co-founded by David Beckham, today announced that IM8 has entered a 10-year, exclusive, worldwide partnership with Authentic Brands Group (“Authentic”) to build Care Bears by IM8, a family health and wellness brand. IM8 will serve as the brand’s exclusive operating partner, and Prenetics will record all revenue from Care Bears by IM8 and pay a royalty to Authentic.

Care Bears by IM8 will debut with great-tasting, science-backed gummy multivitamins for both kids and adults, with a global launch targeted in the first half of 2027 as Care Bears celebrates its 45th anniversary. IM8 plans to expand the brand into better-for-you confectionery and wholesome kids’ snacks and foods. It is the first wellness brand built on Care Bears, which Authentic acquired in August 2026 as its first character IP.

As part of the agreement, Authentic will also become a strategic shareholder in Prenetics, further aligning the two companies as they build Care Bears by IM8 into a global family health and wellness brand.

Care Bears, which turns 45 in 2027, brings more than four decades of emotional connection, over $12 billion in lifetime retail sales, more than 100 characters and reach across 190+ countries in 26 languages. IM8 brings the formulation science, quality standards, digital-first growth engine and global distribution it has built since launch.

Built on one of the fastest growth stories in consumer health. IM8 reached $120 million in annualized revenue run-rate1 within 12 months of launch, one of the fastest trajectories ever recorded in the global supplement industry. In the second quarter of 2026, IM8 generated $45 million in revenue, up approximately 359% year over year and its sixth consecutive record quarter. 1 Annualized Revenue Run-Rate ("ARR") is an operating metric calculated by multiplying IM8 revenue recognized in the applicable month by twelve and includes revenue from both subscription and one-time purchases. ARR is not a financial measure under IFRS, and is not necessarily indicative of revenue for any future period. ARR reflects orders from both new and existing customers and incorporates the effect of cancellations and non-renewals occurring on or before the applicable month; however, it does not adjust for anticipated future cancellations, non-renewals or pauses. ARR annualizes a single month of revenue and assumes that the revenue recognized in that month is maintained for the following twelve months. As a result, ARR is sensitive to the timing of promotional activity, product launches and billing cycles. In particular, the full order value of quarterly subscription plans is recognized upon shipment of the three-month supply, which increases revenue recognized in months when quarterly billings are concentrated. One family health and wellness brand, built for every age. Care Bears by IM8 is not a single licensed product but a family health and wellness brand designed to grow with a family. It brings the same science-first approach behind IM8’s core range to a new audience, opening with gummy multivitamins for kids and for adults that pair great taste with a clean formulation. The gummies will be vegan and contain zero sugar, and will be made without gelatin or artificial colors and flavors. Every product will be third-party tested and developed with guidance from IM8’s advisory board of leading academic and medical experts, giving one brand a place in both the children’s and the grown-ups’ cabinet.

Over time, IM8 plans to extend the brand into further family wellness categories, including better-for-you confectionery and wholesome kids’ snacks and foods.

A large and fast-growing market. Care Bears by IM8 enters one of the fastest-growing formats in consumer health. The global gummy supplements market was valued at approximately US$24.4 billion in 2025 and is projected to reach about US$47.8 billion by 2030, a 14.4% compound annual growth rate, according to MarketsandMarkets. Children’s dietary supplements alone represented an estimated US$26.0 billion in 2025, according to Mordor Intelligence, within a global vitamins and supplements market of roughly US$164 billion, according to Fortune Business Insights. Better-for-you confectionery and kids’ snacks and foods open further addressable categories as the brand grows.

The IM8 playbook, applied to a character. Care Bears by IM8 is built on the same model as IM8 itself: science-first formulation, an expert advisory board, rigorous third-party testing, and direct-to-consumer subscriptions. Authentic Studios and Authentic Live will extend the franchise into content and fan experiences, giving the brand storytelling reach that a typical wellness launch cannot buy.

A prime global retail opportunity. Where IM8 was built direct-to-consumer, Care Bears by IM8 is designed for the shelf as well as the subscription. Care Bears is already sold in more than 190 countries through a network of more than 500 licensing partners, and its characters are instantly recognizable in the aisles where families shop. That gives the franchise a strong position to secure placement with major pharmacy, grocery, mass and club retailers worldwide, alongside IM8’s direct-to-consumer and e-commerce channels.

Launch timing. IM8 is targeting the global launch of Care Bears by IM8 kids’ and adult gummy multivitamins in the first half of 2027. Additional categories will follow, with timing to be announced.

Strategic Highlights
•A brand, not a product. Care Bears by IM8 starts in vitamins and supplements and is designed to extend into adjacent family categories, creating multiple revenue streams under one brand, each with its own retail aisle and growth curve.
•Upside not in current guidance. Prenetics’ 2027 revenue guidance excludes Care Bears by IM8, so the brand represents significant incremental growth beyond current projections.
•Staged rollout de-risks execution. Launching first with gummy multivitamins, building on IM8’s core competency in supplements, proves the brand before expanding into adjacent categories.
•Lifetime household value. The franchise can acquire a family through a child’s vitamins and snacks and retain it with the parents’ own adult multivitamin.
•Pre-built awareness lowers customer acquisition cost. Care Bears enters wellness with roughly 85% global brand awareness, so marketing spend goes toward conversion rather than introduction.
•Global retail expansion. Care Bears gives IM8 a natural entry into mass retail worldwide, adding pharmacy, grocery and big-box distribution to IM8’s direct-to-consumer engine.
•Incremental to the flagship, not cannibalistic. IM8’s flagship, Daily Ultimate Essentials, is a premium all-in-one powder sold mainly by direct-to-consumer subscription to health-optimizing adults. Care Bears by IM8 is a gummy for kids, parents and nostalgic millennials, built for retail shelves as well as online. With a different delivery format, customer profile and channel, Prenetics expects the franchise to add new customers rather than draw from the flagship, while giving the many parents among IM8’s current customers the kids’ product they have been asking for.
•Capital-efficient structure. Under the 10-year, exclusive, worldwide agreement, IM8 is the exclusive operating partner of Care Bears by IM8, and Prenetics will record all revenue generated by the brand and pay a royalty to Authentic. The license gives IM8 long-term, protected access to a global IP without acquiring it, with no competing Care Bears wellness brand in any market.
•Strategic alignment with Authentic. Authentic generates more than $36 billion in annual systemwide retail sales.
