Imperial Brands PLC is on track to meet its full-year guidance for FY26 across all metrics, expecting low-single-digit tobacco net revenue growth and double-digit NGP net revenue growth with market share gains. The company anticipates group adjusted operating profit growth within the 3% to 5% range and high-single-digit earnings per share growth, alongside over £2.2 billion in free cash flow. Furthermore, Imperial Brands has completed a £1.45 billion share buyback and announced a new £1.5 billion share buyback program for FY27, demonstrating confidence in its future performance and commitment to shareholder returns. The company is also progressing with its transformation initiatives, aiming for at least £320 million in savings by 2030.
