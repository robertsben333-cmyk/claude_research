Trifast PLC expects its full-year 2026 underlying EBIT to be approximately £16.0 million, in line with market expectations, despite a 7.0% year-on-year revenue decline to £207.0 million due to lower volumes and exiting low-margin customers. The company anticipates improved EBIT margins of around 7.8% from strategic self-help initiatives, including operational efficiencies and inventory discipline, and maintains leverage below 1.0x. Trifast is closing its Malaysian manufacturing operations to reduce costs and simplify its footprint, while retaining a sales and distribution hub, and expects this closure, along with Middle East conflicts, to reduce FY27 revenue by £8.0 million. The company reports its strongest commercial pipeline in two years, indicating confidence in medium-term prospects and a target of over 10% EBIT margins.
