Zegona Communications plc Transfer to ESCC Listing and eligibility for FTSE UK Indices Zegona Communications plc (" Zegona ", and together with its subsidiaries, the " Group ") announces that it is proposing to transfer the listing category of all of its ordinary shares of £0.01 each (the " Ordinary Shares ") from the equity shares (transition) category of the Official List maintained by the Financial Conduct Authority (the " FCA ") (the " Official List ") to the equity shares (commercial companies) (" ESCC ") category of the Official List, in accordance with UKLR 21.5R and UKLR TP 2 (the " Transfer "). 1 Background to and reasons for the Transfer Zegona is publicly listed on the Main Market of the London Stock Exchange. It was established in 2015 with the objective of investing in businesses in the European Telecommunications, Media and Technology sector and improving their performance to deliver attractive shareholder returns. Zegona was founded by former Virgin Media executives Eamonn O'Hare and Robert Samuelson. In 2024, Zegona completed the acquisition of Vodafone Spain. Zegona currently trades with an enterprise value of c.€8 billion and has returned €1.6 billion to shareholders since the acquisition of Vodafone Spain 2 . As the new UK Listing Rules (the " UKLRs ") create a new, simplified and more competitive listing regime in the UK, the Board has concluded that it would be in the best interests of Zegona and its shareholders as a whole to effect the Transfer 3 . Zegona's board of directors (the " Board ") believes that the Transfer will bring a number of benefits to Zegona and its shareholders. In particular, the Board believes the Transfer will: enable the Ordinary Shares to be considered for inclusion in the FTSE UK Index Series which is expected to improve passive investment flows and liquidity; support execution of the Group's overall strategy, through raising its visibility; and bring Zegona within the additional requirements applicable to companies in the ESCC category, including those relating to significant transactions and related party transactions. Zegona has therefore made the required application to the FCA to approve the Transfer with effect from 8:00 a.m. on 25 November 2026. As at 27 October 2026, Zegona had 224,151,802 Ordinary Shares in issue. Effect of the Transfer Following the Transfer, certain additional provisions of the UKLRs (UKLR 4-10) will apply to Zegona. 4 In particular, Zegona will be: required to appoint a sponsor or obtain a sponsor's guidance in certain circumstances; subject to certain additional eligibility requirements; required to comply with additional continuing obligations; required to make announcements with respect to certain transactions; subject to additional requirements with respect to related party transactions; subject to certain restrictions in respect of further issuances of shares and dealing in its own securities and treasury shares; and subject to additional content requirements with respect to any issued circulars. New eligibility requirements Zegona confirms that it will be able to meet the new eligibility requirements in UKLR 5.2, 5.3 and 5.4 which will apply to it on the Transfer. Zegona confirms that it is not an externally managed company, it does not have a controlling shareholder and it has in place a constitution that allows it to comply with the UKLRs. FTSE eligibility and qualification It is anticipated that, subject to the Transfer becoming effective and other conditions being met, Zegona will be eligible to be considered for inclusion into the FTSE UK Index Series, with the effective date for the rebalance on 22 March 2027. 5 Corporate Governance The Board will be required to continue to report against the provisions of the UK Corporate Governance Code following the Transfer. 6 UK Takeover Code As Zegona has its registered office in the UK and its Ordinary Shares are admitted to trading on the Main Market of the London Stock Exchange, Zegona is currently, and, following the Transfer will remain, subject to the UK Takeover Code. Appointment of Sponsor Zegona has appointed Barclays Bank PLC (" Barclays ") to act as its FCA approved sponsor in relation to the Transfer. Barclays has given and has not withdrawn its written consent to the inclusion of the reference to its name in the form and context in which it is included in this announcement.
