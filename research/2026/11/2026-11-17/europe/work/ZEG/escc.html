ESCC listing and eligibility for FTSE UK Indices

Summary by AI BETAClose X

Zegona Communications plc is proposing to transfer its listing category from the equity shares (transition) to the equity shares (commercial companies) category of the Official List, effective November 25, 2026, with the aim of becoming eligible for inclusion in the FTSE UK Index Series by March 2027. This move is expected to enhance passive investment flows and liquidity, support the company's strategy, and align with new UK Listing Rules. Zegona, which acquired Vodafone Spain for €5 billion and has returned €1.6 billion to shareholders since then, currently has an enterprise value of approximately €8 billion and 224,151,802 ordinary shares in issue as of October 27, 2026. The company confirms it meets the new eligibility requirements and has appointed Barclays Bank PLC as its sponsor for the transfer.
