 FirstGroup plc has announced the completion of the second tranche of its £100 million share buyback programme, having purchased 27,522,679 ordinary shares for £49,806,894, which are now held in treasury. The company also confirmed the commencement of the third £25 million tranche of the buyback programme, with RBC Europe Limited undertaking this portion. These actions are in line with the original buyback programme announced on 18 June 2026.
 Disclaimer* 
 12 October 2026 
 FirstGroup plc 
 On-market Share Buyback Programme of   £100m 
 Completion of Second Tranche and Commencement of Third Tranche 
 FirstGroup plc (" FirstGroup ")   announces that, in accordance with the terms of its £100m on-market share buyback programme announced on   18 June 2026 (the “ Buyback ”), Panmure Liberum Limited completed the second tranche on 9 October 2026.   In the first two tranches 27,522,679 ordinary shares have been purchased fo r £49,806,894 .   The shares are currently being held in Treasury. 
 RBC Europe Limited will now commence the third £25m   tranche of the Buyback of with effect from today.   The third and fourth tranches of the Buyback are expected to proceed as set out in our announcement of 18 June 2026.
