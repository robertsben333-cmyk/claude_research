SUMMARY Takeda Pharmaceutical Company Limited ( TAK +4.42% ) reported a 1% decrease in core revenue at constant exchange rates for the second quarter of fiscal year 2026, as growth from new product launches and core in-line brands was offset by headwinds in the mature and off-patent portfolio. Management reported that the company is on track to meet its fiscal year priorities, supported by the launch of three new medicines: ORZEYFUL, MIMRYLO, and ELAHERE. Takeda decided to share zasocitinib ulcerative colitis data ahead of the Crohn's disease results due to a multi-month delay between the two data sets, reporting that the 50-milligram dose showed clinical remission rates on par with other investigational oral products. INDUSTRY GLOSSARY NT1 : Narcolepsy type 1, a lifelong neurological disorder caused by orexin deficiency. CER : Constant exchange rate, a method of comparing financial results without the effects of currency fluctuations. PASI 100 : Psoriasis Area and Severity Index 100, indicating a 100% improvement or complete skin clearance. TYK2 : Tyrosine kinase 2, an enzyme involved in signaling pathways associated with inflammatory diseases. IBD : Inflammatory bowel disease, including conditions like ulcerative colitis and Crohn's disease. PDT : Plasma-derived therapies, treatments derived from human blood plasma. ADC : Antibody-drug conjugate, a class of biopharmaceutical drugs designed as a targeted therapy for cancer. NCCN : National Comprehensive Cancer Network, an alliance of cancer centers that publishes clinical practice guidelines. DEA : Drug Enforcement Administration, the U.S. federal agency responsible for drug scheduling and enforcement. TOPO1 : Topoisomerase I inhibitor, a type of chemotherapy payload used in antibody-drug conjugates.
