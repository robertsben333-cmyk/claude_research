SUMMARY

Takeda Pharmaceutical Company Limited (TAK +4.42%) reported a 1% decrease in core revenue at constant exchange rates for the second quarter of fiscal year 2026, as growth from new product launches and core in-line brands was offset by headwinds in the mature and off-patent portfolio. Management reported that the company is on track to meet its fiscal year priorities, supported by the launch of three new medicines: ORZEYFUL, MIMRYLO, and ELAHERE. The company raised its full-year absolute forecasts for revenue and operating profit, primarily citing favorable foreign exchange rate assumptions and better-than-expected performance in mature brands like VYVANSE. Takeda continues to reinvest savings from its organizational transformation program into its late-stage pipeline and upcoming product launches to drive future growth acceleration.

  • Julie Kim noted that ORZEYFUL was recently selected as one of TIME Magazine's best inventions of 2026 in the scientific innovation category.
  • Management expects to begin the U.S. launch of ORZEYFUL in early November 2026 following the anticipated completion of DEA scheduling.
  • Andrew Plump reported that zasocitinib demonstrated a 36.5% rate of complete skin clearance at week 16, which represents a potential best-in-class profile for oral psoriasis treatments.
  • Takeda decided to share zasocitinib ulcerative colitis data ahead of the Crohn's disease results due to a multi-month delay between the two data sets, reporting that the 50-milligram dose showed clinical remission rates on par with other investigational oral products.
  • Teresa Bitetti indicated that MIMRYLO is the only polycythemia vera treatment listed as a category 1 recommendation in the NCCN guidelines for both high-risk and low-risk patients.
  • The company initiated a Phase III study for elritercept in myelofibrosis and expects to submit regulatory filings for TAK-881 in Europe and Japan by the end of the fiscal year.
  • Management reduced its forecast for fiscal year 2026 restructuring costs by JPY 10 billion to a total of JPY 160 billion.

INDUSTRY GLOSSARY

  • NT1: Narcolepsy type 1, a lifelong neurological disorder caused by orexin deficiency.
  • CER: Constant exchange rate, a method of comparing financial results without the effects of currency fluctuations.
  • PASI 100: Psoriasis Area and Severity Index 100, indicating a 100% improvement or complete skin clearance.
  • TYK2: Tyrosine kinase 2, an enzyme involved in signaling pathways associated with inflammatory diseases.
  • IBD: Inflammatory bowel disease, including conditions like ulcerative colitis and Crohn's disease.
  • PDT: Plasma-derived therapies, treatments derived from human blood plasma.
  • ADC: Antibody-drug conjugate, a class of biopharmaceutical drugs designed as a targeted therapy for cancer.
  • NCCN: National Comprehensive Cancer Network, an alliance of cancer centers that publishes clinical practice guidelines.
  • DEA: Drug Enforcement Administration, the U.S. federal agency responsible for drug scheduling and enforcement.
  • TOPO1: Topoisomerase I inhibitor, a type of chemotherapy payload used in antibody-drug conjugates.
