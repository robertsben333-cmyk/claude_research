Hays plc

First Quarter Trading Statement

 

12 October 2026

Hays plc ('Hays' or the 'Company') today announces its quarterly update for the three months ended 30 September 2026 (‘First Quarter Trading Statement’), which is available at: http://www.rns-pdf.londonstockexchange.com/rns/4383Y_1-2026-10-9.pdf

The First Quarter Trading Statement will also be available on the Hays website www.haysplc.com/investors and has been submitted in full unedited text to the Financial Conduct Authority's National Storage Mechanism and will shortly be available for inspection at https://data.fca.org.uk/#/nsm/nationalstoragemechanism.

 

Conference call

James Hilton and Kean Marden will conduct a conference call for analysts and investors at 9:00am UK time on 12 October 2026. Participants are invited to register via the URL link below:

https://register-conf.media-server.com/register/BIef72d95afb8f4829aa678fe9986dfd21

 

Once registered, you will receive a confirmation email, with the details of the call and a personal login link and PIN which will place you directly into the call, without the need to speak to an operator. The call will be recorded and will also be available for playback via the results centre on our investor website.
