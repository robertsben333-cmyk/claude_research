Arch profits fall as Iran war adds to catastrophe losses

  • by Gavin Souter
  • Financial Results, P/C Insurers
  • Oct 28, 2026

Arch Capital Group reported lower third-quarter profits Tuesday as catastrophe losses, including claims related to the Iran war, weighed on underwriting results.

The Bermuda-based insurer and reinsurer reported net income of $1.03 billion, down 23.5% from $1.34 billion in the year-earlier period. Net premiums written fell 3% to $3.85 billion.

Arch recorded $234 million in pretax catastrophe losses, net of reinsurance and reinstatement premiums, compared with $72 million in the year-earlier period. The company did not disclose the amount attributable to the Iran conflict.


The war also generated additional business, particularly marine war risk coverage, executives said Wednesday on a conference call with analysts.

“I think we see it more as an opportunity than anything else,” CEO Nicolas Papadopoulo said, citing demand for marine war and land-based war coverage.

Arch’s combined ratio deteriorated to 82.5% from 79.8% a year earlier.

Gross premiums written in its insurance segment increased 7.8% to $2.77 billion, while reinsurance premiums declined 8% to $2.31 billion.

Net investment income increased 10.5% to $451 million, but Arch recorded $99 million in net realized investment losses, compared with $210 million in gains a year earlier.

Mr. Papadopoulo said abundant capacity and declining property reinsurance rates were limiting growth opportunities, while casualty insurance pricing remained attractive.
