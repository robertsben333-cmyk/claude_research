Proposed capital raise

Summary by AI BETAClose X

WH Smith PLC is proposing a capital raise of up to approximately 26 million new ordinary shares, representing about 20% of its existing share capital, through a placing, subscription by directors and management, and a retail offer. This move aims to strengthen the balance sheet, support growth initiatives, and reduce leverage to around 2x by the end of the 2026 financial year, following a downturn in trading conditions impacted by geopolitical events and weaker consumer confidence. Directors and management intend to contribute approximately £1.725 million to the subscription.
