Synsam AB - Investor News

Synsam invites you to a video presentation of the Q3 report 2026 and a live Q&A session

Synsam AB (publ) will release its interim report for the third quarter of 2026 on Thursday, November 12 at 07.30 CET. At the same time, the company will publish President and CEO Håkan Lundstedt’s comment, together with a presentation of the results with CFO Per Hedblom. At 10.30 CET the same day, you will have the opportunity to participate in a live Q&A session, in English.

The CEO comment and presentation of the results are video materials created to complement the interim report and provide further insight and better understanding of the company's operations for the stock market. The presentation material will be available on the company's website at 07.30 CET on November 12, 2026, at www.synsamgroup.com/investor-relations. 

Investors, analysts, and media are welcome to watch the CEO comment and presentation of the results on the reporting day, as well as participate in the live Q&A session in Swedish at 09.30 CET or in English at 10.30 CET. No pre-registration is required.

New for this quarter, the Swedish Q&A session will also be available with AI-generated English translation, providing greater accessibility for international investors and other stakeholders.

Live Q&A in Swedish, with possibility to ask questions

  • Date and time: November 12, 2026, at 09.30 CET
  • Place: Webcast
  • Speakers: President and CEO Håkan Lundstedt and CFO Per Hedblom
  • Language: Swedish, with AI-generated English translation available
  • Link to participate in the Q&A session: Synsam Group Q3

Please note that the AI-generated translation may contain inaccuracies or errors. In the event of any discrepancies, the statements made in Swedish during the live webcast shall prevail.

Live Q&A in English, with possibility to ask questions

Synsam Group is a leading optician group in the Nordic region, with a differentiated and affordable subscription offering. Synsam Group offers a wide range of products and services for eye health and eye fashion, catering to the customer’s different lifestyles and needs. The group has approximately 5,600 employees, net sales of approximately SEK 7.3 billion (rolling twelve months until June 2026) and approximately 600 stores in the Nordics, operating under the brands Synsam, Synsam Outlet and Profil Optik in Denmark. Through digitalization, subscription services and other innovative concepts, Synsam Group is at the forefront of innovation in the Nordic optical retail market across multiple dimensions including customer journey, product offering and ESG. Synsam Group’s share is traded on Nasdaq Stockholm (SYNSAM).www.synsamgroup.com
