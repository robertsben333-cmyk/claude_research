On September 10, Truecaller completed its acquisition of US-based text+. Text+ is a US software
company that offers a flexible and cost-effective alternative to traditional mobile operators. The
acquisition strengthens Truecaller’s presence in the United States and brings the company closer to
becoming a comprehensive communications platform by expanding its offering with complementary
services such as second phone numbers and Voice over Internet Protocol (VoIP). The purchase price
amounted to USD 15.7 million (corresponding to SEK 151.6 million) on a cash-and-debt-free basis and was
financed using the company’s own funds.
Continued buybacks. During the quarter, Truecaller repurchased 19.5 million Class B shares. At the end
of the period Truecaller held approximately 7.2 million B-shares and 2.1 million C-shares.
Cancellation of shares On September 10 an extraordinary general meeting resolved to cancel just under
24.2 million repurchased Class B shares, corresponding to just over 7 percent of the company’s
outstanding share capital.

CEO WORD Q3 2026

Return to growth following a successful strategic reset
Following a challenging year, I am very pleased to report that the third quarter marks a
strong sequential recovery across all our revenue streams, signaling a return to growth.
Our consistent focus on shifting our revenue mix towards recurring revenues is delivering
clear results, while our advertising business has improved significantly on the back of our
successful reset of the business.
Net sales for the quarter amounted to SEK 464.0 million, representing an 18 percent
sequential growth compared to Q2, and an increase of 3 percent in constant currencies
year-on-year. Supported by disciplined cost control, our underlying profitability returned
to a healthy level, yielding an EBITDA excluding non-recurring items of SEK 148.5 million,
equivalent to an EBITDA margin of 32.0 percent. While these results are highly
encouraging, we firmly believe there is still more to harvest from the structural
improvements we have made during the year.

Revenues
Total revenues during the quarter decreased by 2 percent
compared to the corresponding quarter the previous year,
and amounted to SEK 469.3 (476.7) million. Currency effects
had a negative effect on revenues during the quarter. In
constant currencies revenues is estimated to have
increased by 2 percent, see Currency exposure below.
Other income amounted to SEK 5.3 (8.8) million.

Direct advertising sales continued to develop positively
compared with the corresponding period last year.
Premium revenues increased by 32 percent to SEK 127.1
(96.4) million. In constant currencies, subscription revenue
growth amounted to 32 percent. Premium revenues
continue to grow steadily, driven by an increasingly
compelling product offering with more premium features
combined with more targeted marketing of the
subscription service. The average number of paying
subscribers increased by 23 percent year-over-year to 4.06
(3.30) million. The conversion rate to paying users
continued to improve, reaching an average of 0.80%
(0.69%) during the quarter. Average revenue per paying
user (ARPU) increased and amounted to SEK 10.41 (10.04).
Revenues from iOS accounted for approximately 47 percent
(47%) of total Premium revenues. The average number of
iOS subscribers increased by 26 percent year-over-year,
while Android subscribers increased by 22 percent. The
conversion rate on iOS reached 4.30 percent (3.36%).
Premium revenues continued to grow across all regions,
with the strongest growth recorded in the Middle East &
Africa (MEA) and rest of the world (RoW).

estimated to have grown by 3 percent in constant

Gross profit
Gross profit decreased by 7 percent to SEK 331.0 (354.3)
million compared with the corresponding period last year.
In constant currencies, the decrease is estimated at 4
percent. The gross margin amounted to 71.3 percent
(75.7%). The lower gross margin compared with the
corresponding period of the previous year was primarily
due to increased server costs following the implementation
of Truecaller’s new advertising infrastructure, as well as
slightly higher verification costs for new users. Gross margin
was also somewhat negatively affected by the fact that,
compared with the third quarter of 2025, Truecaller is now
able to quantify commissions payable to a larger number
of smaller advertising partners and Truecaller for Business
partners. As a result, the related revenues are recognised
on a gross basis, with partner commissions recognised as
cost of sales. This increases both revenues and cost of
sales, thereby reducing the gross margin, while having no
impact on operating profit.

The expenses related to the incentive programmes consist
of provisions for estimated social security contributions
payable when employee stock options or restricted share
units (RSUs) granted to Swedish employees are exercised,
as well as a non-cash accounting expense related to the
potential dilution arising from employee stock options and
RSUs. Only the social security contributions affect cash flow,
and only when stock options or RSUs are exercised. Sharebased compensation is measured at fair value and
recognised over the vesting period of the programme. The
social security contribution expense is remeasured based
on the Company's share price at each reporting date and
may therefore increase if the share price appreciates,
creating volatility in the income statement. For further
information, see Note 5.

period the previous year.

expects any final claim to ultimately be settled through the
bilateral MAP process, and anticipates that the ultimate
settled amount, if any, will be substantially lower than the
initial tax authority assertions.

now paying off.

are increasingly carrying live traffic.

Note 9. Write-down of shares
in subsidiaries
The Parent Company holds shares in True Software
Scandinavia AB (TSSAB), which constitute the Parent
Company’s primary asset. Truecaller AB’s market
capitalization was lower than the Parent Company’s
recognized assets; consequently, an impairment test was
performed as of June 30, 2026. The test showed that the
recoverable amount was lower than the carrying amount
subject to testing; the impairment requirement amounted
to SEK 2,600 million and has been recognized as an
impairment of shares in subsidiaries in the Parent
Company’s income statement for the period. The
impairment is not tax-deductible, as the shares constitute
business-related holdings, and it does not affect the
Group’s reported earnings or financial position. After the
impairment, the carrying amount of shares In True Software
Scandinavia AB amounted to 7,579 million.

Alternative performance measurements
In accordance with ESMA (European Securities and Markets Authority) Guidelines on Alternative
Performance Measures, the definition and reconciliation of alternative performance measures used by
Truecaller are presented here. The guidelines entail additional disclosures regarding financial measures
not defined under IFRS. The performance measures shown below are presented in the interim report.
They are used for the purposes of internal control and monitoring. As all companies do not calculate
financial measures in the same way, these measures are not always comparable to measures used by
other companies. The following measures are measures used by Truecaller to clarify the company’s
performance and simplify evaluation for users of the company’s financial reports.
Key performance
measurements

Definition

Purpose

Gross profit

Net sales minus brokerage costs.

Gross profit is used to analyze profit minus direct costs
(costs related directly to brokerage of ad space and
the costs to onboard new premium users).

Gross margin

Gross profit as a percentage of net sales.

Gross margin is a measure of profitability minus direct
costs.

EBITDA

EBIT before interest, taxes, depreciation
and amortization.

EBITDA is a measurement Truecaller uses to show how
current operations develop over time.

EBITDA margin

EBITDA as a percentage of net sales.

EBITDA margin is used to illustrate the profitability of
current operations excluding items affecting
comparability and before amortization.

EBIT (operating profit)

Operating profit (earnings) before
interest and taxes

EBIT is used to analyze the profit generated by the
operating entity.

EBIT margin

EBIT as a percentage of net sales.

The EBIT margin is used to illustrate the profitability of
current operations.

Equity to assets ratio

Equity divided by total assets.

A measure to illustrate financial risk, expressed as the
percentage of total assets financed by shareholders’
equity.

(MAU)

The number of users that have a

The number of users that have a

Used to illustrate the volume of active users of
Truecaller’s services.

Cost per thousand

CPM illustrates the cost of displaying one
ad one thousand times.

Used to illustrate the effectiveness of the ad platform.

The average revenue for one recurring

Used to illustrate how revenues per user develop over
time.
