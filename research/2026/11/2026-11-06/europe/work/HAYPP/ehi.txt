Nicotine and tobacco products continue to grow
faster than e-commerce overall
The Swedish Retail Federation’s (Svensk Handel) E-commerce Indicator for September 2026
shows that nicotine and tobacco products are growing faster than e-commerce overall so far
this year, and that the category is one of few where the share of consumers shopping online is
increasing.

Total e-commerce turnover in Sweden reached SEK 13.4 billion in September 2026, a decrease of
7 per cent compared to the corresponding month last year. In the third quarter, total e-commerce
turnover decreased by 3 per cent compared to the same quarter last year. Cumulative turnover
growth so far this year stands at 2 per cent.

During the period January–September 2026, turnover in the nicotine and tobacco products
category increased by 6 per cent compared to the same period last year, against 2 per cent for e-
commerce overall. Among the categories measured in the report, only two categories have shown
stronger growth so far this year.

In September, 6 per cent of consumers purchased nicotine and tobacco products online,
compared with 5 per cent in the same month last year. The category is thereby one of only two in
the report where the share of consumers shopping online increased compared to September 2025.

In the third quarter, turnover in the category decreased by 9 per cent compared to the
corresponding quarter in 2025. The average purchase amount per consumer was SEK 740 in the
third quarter of 2026, compared with SEK 810 in the corresponding quarter in 2025.

“The third quarter was weaker for e-commerce as a whole. But looking at the year so far, nicotine and
tobacco products continue to grow faster than the market, and more consumers are choosing to buy
them online than last year. This growth in turnover is also confirmed by the performance we have seen
at Haypp during the first and second quarters,” says Markus Lindblad, Head of Legal & External
Affairs.

Haypp in brief


Haypp Group is the leading e-commerce retailer selling reduced-risk nicotine products, primarily
nicotine pouches. The company is at the intersection of two trends, the rapid transition toward
lower risk nicotine and the shift to online purchases. With roots in the pioneering smoke-free
alternative markets of Scandinavia, Haypp uses its regulatory expertise and e-commerce
leadership to bring compelling value to over 1.1 million consumers. Operating through eleven
distinct e-commerce brands, the Group is active in five countries in Europe, the USA and Saudi
Arabia. Haypp Group's e-commerce sites include: Snus.com and Nicokick.com.
