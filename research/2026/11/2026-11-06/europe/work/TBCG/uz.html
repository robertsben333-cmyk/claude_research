TBC Bank Posts 69.7 Billion Soum Loss in Nine Months of 2026

Anvar Umarov · 22:50, 30.10.2026 · Updated 23:09 · 329 views

Tashkent, Uzbekistan (UzDaily.uz) — TBC Bank reported a net loss of 69.7 billion soums for January–September 2026, compared with a net profit of 30.3 billion soums in the same period a year earlier, according to its published financial statements.

The bank's total income increased by 6.4% year on year to 4.2 trillion soums during the nine-month period.

However, expenses related to provisions for potential credit losses rose by 42.3% to 1.2 trillion soums.

In the third quarter of 2026, TBC Bank recorded a net loss of 357.7 billion soums, compared with a loss of 70.7 billion soums in July–September 2025.

As of 1 October 2026, the bank's loan portfolio stood at 7.7 trillion soums, representing a decline of 28.9% compared with the same date a year earlier.

The share of non-performing loans (NPLs) increased from 4.5% to 13.4%.

TBC Bank's total assets amounted to 13.4 trillion soums at the end of September, down 5.2% year on year.

Meanwhile, the bank's capital increased by 19% to 3 trillion soums.
