Your rights, and who to ask

This website is for information only. It has no contact forms, no visitor accounts and no public login, so we do not collect your name, your address, or anything else you might type in. The only personal data in play here is what these cookies make possible — which is why this panel is the whole of it.

You can withdraw your consent at any time, and it is meant to be exactly as easy as giving it was: the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page reopens this panel. Withdrawing takes effect at once, and the page reloads so that anything already running stops.

Questions, or a complaint about how your data has been handled, go to contactus@orklaindia.com. You may ask what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be erased.

If we do not resolve it, you have the right to complain to the Data Protection Board of India. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may instead complain to your national data protection authority.
