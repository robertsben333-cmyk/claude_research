Schouw & Co. hosts Capital Markets Day and announces long-term EBITDA ambitions

Today, Schouw & Co. hosts a Capital Markets Day in Copenhagen, Denmark. The Capital Markets Day will focus, among other things, on the continued strength of the conglomerate model following the successful IPO of BioMar in May 2026, as well as the group's views on capital allocation and priorities in the coming years.

At the Capital Markets Day, Schouw & Co. will disclose selected elements of its “Expand 2030” strategy plan, including an ambition to deliver continued organic growth and consolidated EBITDA of DKK 4.5 billion in 2030.

In this context, the current dividend policy of stable or increasing dividends is reiterated, and the group’s leverage target of NIBD/EBITDA in the range of 1.0–2.5x is confirmed.

During the Capital Markets Day, the management teams of GPV, HydraSpecma, and Schouw & Co.'s newly acquired portfolio business Spectre will provide further details on how the businesses will contribute to the group's ambitions and provide insights into selected growth segments and M&A objectives.

The presentation material is available on www.schouw.com.
