Half-year Financial Report

AI SummaryClose X

Trifast reported HY2027 revenue of £100.7m, down 4.8% CER, while underlying EBIT increased to £7.4m (up 5.7%) and underlying EBIT margin improved to 7.3% (+70bps); underlying profit before tax was £5.4m (up 10.2%) but underlying diluted EPS declined to 1.70p (down 35.8%). Statutory loss before tax was £3.0m after £8.4m of separately disclosed transformation costs (including £4.2m for Project Ignite). Adjusted net debt was £15.8m with leverage below 1.0x and c.£77.0m of banking facilities undrawn. The Board revised FY27 guidance to revenue £200.0–205.0m and underlying EBIT £14.0–14.5m and declared an interim dividend of 0.60p.
