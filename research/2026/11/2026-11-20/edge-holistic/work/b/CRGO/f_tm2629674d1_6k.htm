Clarification Regarding Record Date for 2026 Annual General Meeting

 

Freightos Limited (the “Company”) is furnishing this Report of Foreign Private Issuer on Form 6-K to clarify a typographical error contained in Exhibit 99.2 (Form of Proxy Card) to the Company's Report of Foreign Private Issuer on Form 6-K furnished to the Securities and Exchange Commission on November 4, 2026.

 

The preamble to the proxy card inadvertently referenced a record date of November 9, 2026 in connection with the Company’s upcoming annual general meeting to be held on December 16, 2026 (the “Meeting”). As correctly set forth in the Notice of 2026 Annual General Meeting and Proxy Statement (Exhibit 99.1) and in the actual proxy cards distributed to record shareholders for use in connection with the Meeting, the actual record date for determining shareholders entitled to receive notice of, and to vote at, the Meeting is the close of business on Thursday, November 5, 2026.

 

For the avoidance of doubt, the Company and its transfer agent, Continental Stock Transfer & Trust Company, will tabulate all proxies and ballots based strictly on the record date of November 5, 2026, and the typographical reference to November 9, 2026 on the form of proxy card furnished to the SEC has no legal significance and will be disregarded. No action is required by any shareholder who has already submitted or will submit a proxy card.
