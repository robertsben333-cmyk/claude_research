## 2. What the market cares about

The size of the FY27 revenue guidance raise above the current $1.130–1.140bn range —
specifically whether it clears roughly $1.15bn — read together with subscription revenue
growth holding at or above the 24% just printed, and whether Titan Intelligence ("Max")
locations double again as management pre-committed on the Q1 call. The persona panel
converged on the same read independently: the fundamental analyst named "the FY27 raise
and GTV growth, not headline EPS" as the trading metric; the macro/peers analyst pointed to
the same margin-guide-vs-headline-beat dynamic that hit Klaviyo and Figma this season.

deviation reference, not a cap or a target — TTAN has cleared it once in six tries (+13.64%,
Dec 2025).

## 5. Thesis

**Bull case.** The beat itself looks close to mechanical: six of six non-GAAP EPS beats
since IPO, and a guide-beat track record of +1.3% to +5.3% on revenue against the high end
of guidance. Subscription revenue grew 24.2% y/y last quarter and Max adoption "more than
doubled" — a self-set, checkable bar. Positioning is de-risked into the print (short
interest down ~27% in 30 days, the stock down ~12% over five sessions) with an options book
that is one-sidedly call-skewed, so a clean beat-and-raise has room to run against thin
opposition. This season's closest peers (Samsara, Procore) were both rewarded double digits
for the same beat-and-raise shape.

**Bear case.** The current FY27 guide midpoint, on the panel's own sourced arithmetic,
implies H2 FY27 revenue growth decelerating to roughly 15% y/y versus the 25% just printed
in Q1 — so unless the raise itself exceeds simple Q2 flow-through, management is
mechanically confirming a deceleration story with its own numbers. The Q2 non-GAAP
operating-income guide ($38–39m) sits below Q1's actual ($40.8m), signalling a margin
step-down as Max/AI R&D spend ramps (R&D +27% y/y last quarter). The stock is still
GAAP-loss-making, trades near 62x forward earnings, and sits inside a 2026 software
de-rating that has sold good prints outright at Salesforce, Workday, Klaviyo and Figma.

**Base case.** A modest beat-and-raise that neither clearly breaks nor clearly confirms the
deceleration framing — guide raised, but not decisively past ~$1.15bn — producing a realised
move in the 8–11% range (below the rich 13.1% implied), with direction only weakly positive.
That is what the panel actually voted for.

## 6. What flips it

Up: an FY27 raise that clears roughly $1.15bn with subscription growth held at or above
24% and the "NDR above 110%" language retained validates the bull case cleanly and points
the stock at the call-heavy open-interest strikes 14–25% above spot ($100–$110). Down: a
token raise that leaves the range near $1.14–1.145bn, subscription growth slipping toward
21%, or a further step-down in the operating-margin guide reproduces the exact setup behind
both of TTAN's negative prints this year (March 2026's guide-driven −6.4%, and the muted
+4.1% close on June's after-hours +14% pop). Reversal risk sits at 56/100 (Med); the
red-team persona itself scored only +8 — the panel's lowest — muted mainly by this
regime-risk case rather than by disagreement on direction, and no other persona rebutted the
H2-deceleration arithmetic.

## 7. Positioning & sentiment

- **Options/skew:** front-month is call-skewed against the falling tape — ~25-delta calls
  trade roughly 8.5 vol points over ~25-delta puts, put/call open-interest ratio 0.13, and
  traded put/call 0.35 versus a 0.55 20-day average (~1.4σ low). One-sided upside
  positioning, not hedging demand.
- **Short interest/crowding:** 8.7% of float, down ~27% over 30 days; borrow under 0.5%
  with ample availability. De-risked, not crowded short — there is little fuel for a squeeze
  but also little overhang to unwind.
- **Run-up/momentum:** down ~12% over the five sessions into the print (vs. IGV −4.5%),
  ~23% lower year-over-year, ~29% below the 52-week high of $119.99. The de-rating looks
  thematic (sector-wide AI-disruption fear) rather than idiosyncratic to tonight's print.
- **Social/retail tone:** thin, not hyped — StockTwits shows only ~77 bulls voting on an
  ~$8B name, no meaningful Reddit/X thread volume surfaced, and MarketBeat news sentiment
  (0.33) sits well below the tech-sector average (0.83). This reads as institutional
  de-rating rather than a retail-driven setup, so crowded-long or meme risk is low.

## 8. Insider / communication / alt-data findings

Nothing unusual, and that absence is itself informative. All insider sales traced were
routine — the CFO's under a 10b5-1 plan adopted 2026-01-15, VC-sponsor (Bessemer, ICONIQ)
distributions that are price-insensitive and sold into weakness well below today's spot.
Zero open-market insider purchases were found, but for a late-2024 IPO with founders already
holding large stakes, that is the base case rather than a warning sign. No negative
communication signal turned up: guidance was raised, not trimmed, at the last print; the
"NDR above 110%" disclosure was retained verbatim rather than softened or dropped; and no
8-K, restatement or executive change has been filed since 2026-06-18. Form-4 activity went
quiet after 2026-08-17, consistent with a routine pre-earnings blackout rather than anything
unusual.
