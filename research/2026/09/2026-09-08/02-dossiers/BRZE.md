# BRZE — Braze, Inc.

**What this print is about.** Braze reports fiscal Q2 2027 (quarter ended 31 Jul 2026) after the close on 8 Sep 2026. The company has just delivered four consecutive quarters of *accelerating* organic revenue growth (culminating in +30.2% reported / +27% organic in Q1 FY27), dollar-based net retention has inflected up three quarters in a row (108% → 109% → 110%), and management has pre-committed to +400bps of non-GAAP operating margin expansion in FY27 [1][2][15]. The problem is not the business — it is the setup. Consensus revenue sits at roughly the *top* of company guidance, the stock has run 12–32% in a month depending on window, put/call is at 0.16 (near a 52-week low), analysts have raised targets six times in twelve sessions, and an interim CFO delivers his first guide. Three months ago Braze raised revenue guidance but held the profit range and the stock fell; four weeks ago its closest public comp, Klaviyo, did exactly the same and fell 11.5% after hours [11][19]. This print is a referendum on whether Braze can raise **both** lines, or whether it repeats the "revenue-only raise" that has been punished twice in this tape.

## 3. The one metric that matters

**Whether the FY27 non-GAAP operating income guidance moves up alongside the revenue raise — i.e. does the $70–74M range go higher, or does Braze again raise revenue and hold profit?**

Why this and not headline EPS or even revenue: on 27 May 2026 Braze beat revenue by 2.9%, raised FY27 revenue by ~$10.5M to $895–899M, and left FY27 non-GAAP net income at $70–74M. The stock fell 16.1% after hours and closed lower the next day [11][12]. StockStory's contemporaneous read was explicit: "the company is targeting the same profit range even though its sales target has increased… with signs that margins are softening, some investors are opting to move out of the stock" [11]. The identical pattern at the closest public comp — Klaviyo on 5 Aug 2026, revenue beat, FY revenue raised, non-GAAP operating income *cut* to $212–218M — produced an 11.55% after-hours decline [19]. The market in this sub-sector is currently paying for margin, not growth.

**Expectation.** Management said on the Q1 call it remains "on track to achieve the 400 basis points operating margin expansion promised for the fiscal year" [15]. The Q2 guide of $17.5M on $220M is a 7.95% margin versus 5.0% actual in Q1 — a very large sequential step-up already embedded. So the bar is: beat the $17–18M and lift the full-year $70–74M. If Braze prints Q2 non-GAAP operating income above ~$19M and takes FY27 to ~$76M+, the revenue-only-raise objection is neutralised. If it prints ~$18M and reaffirms $70–74M, the May tape is the template.

**Co-primary metric to watch: dollar-based net retention.** 108% (TTM to 31 Oct 2025) → 109% (31 Jan 2026) → 110% (30 Apr 2026); large-customer DBNRR 110% → 110% → 111% [1][2][14][25]. A fourth consecutive increase (111%) confirms the expansion thesis; a flat 110% or a step back is the single cleanest way for the AI-driven re-rating narrative to break.

## 4. Fundamentals — what changed, what is at stake

**Scale and mix (Q1 FY27, quarter ended 30 Apr 2026)** [1][2]:
- Revenue $211.0M, +30.2% reported, **+27% organic** — fourth consecutive quarter of organic acceleration [15].
- Subscription $195.2M (92.5%); professional services and other $15.8M (7.5%).
- Geography: US $115.5M (54.6%), international $95.5M (45.4%) — **45% of revenue is non-US, so FX is a live translation variable** [2].
- GAAP gross margin 65.6–65.7%; non-GAAP gross margin 67.4% [1][2]. Note gross margin has been drifting down (Q3 FY26 GAAP 67.2% / non-GAAP 69.1%, versus 69.8% / 70.5% a year earlier, attributed to higher messaging volumes and hosting expense) [14]. **Messaging-volume mix is a structural gross-margin headwind.**

**Customers and retention** [1][2][25]:
- Total customers 2,713 at 30 Apr 2026, up from 2,342 y/y; +104 sequentially [17].
- Customers ≥$500k ARR: 349 versus 262 y/y (+33%); 333 at 31 Jan 2026.
- ARR crossed **$800M** early in Q1 FY27 [29].
- DBNRR 110% all / 111% ≥$500k, both up 100bps sequentially [1][15].
- No customer >10% of revenue; one customer was ~12% of accounts receivable at 30 Apr 2026 (none >10% at 31 Jan 2026) — **concentration is low; the AR item is worth a footnote check but is not a red flag on its own** [2].

**Backlog** [2][15]: total RPO $1,079.2M, +30% y/y, +4% sequentially; current RPO $670.3M, +28% y/y. RPO growth roughly tracks revenue growth, so the backlog is not flattering the reported number.

**Margins and cash** [1][2][15]:
- Non-GAAP operating income $10.5M (5.0% margin) versus $2.8M a year earlier — +300bps y/y.
- GAAP operating loss $(27.5)M; GAAP net loss $(26.6)M; GAAP EPS $(0.24).
- SBC $34.2M in the quarter — **16.2% of revenue** (my calculation), with $265.1M of unrecognised RSU/PSU cost over a 2.62-year weighted average [2]. This is the gap between the GAAP and non-GAAP stories and the main dilution driver.
- Opex growth: S&M +20.4%, R&D +25.3%, G&A −23.7% y/y [2]. Sales and marketing is growing slower than revenue — the source of the margin expansion.
- Record free cash flow $26.8M (12.7% margin), versus $22.9M a year earlier; operating cash flow $28.1M [1][2].
- Deferred revenue $342.5M at 30 Apr 2026 versus $304.6M at 31 Jan 2026 [2].

**Capital returns and dilution** [2][25]:
- $100M repurchase authorisation approved March 2026; $50M ASR executed, 1,716,002 shares delivered and retired in Q1 FY27; **$50M remains authorised**.
- Class A outstanding fell to 111.8M at 30 Apr 2026 from 112.8M at 31 Jan 2026 — buyback is currently more than offsetting dilution, but guidance assumes ~114.0M diluted shares for FY27 [1].

**AI product line** [15]: Braze AI Decisioning Studio (the OfferFit asset, acquired for $325M announced 27 Mar 2025 [30]) contributed **$5.7M of revenue in Q1 FY27** and management guided it to grow **15–20% sequentially** in Q2 — implying roughly $6.6–6.8M. Management disclosed a real operational constraint: implementation start dates had been pushed out "in excess of 4 months in some cases" in Q4 for lack of forward-deployed delivery personnel, and by the end of Q1 they had "cut that about in half." **This is a services-capacity bottleneck on the highest-narrative-value product line, and it is quantifiable — a Q2 Decisioning Studio number materially below ~$6.6M would be a concrete negative.**

**What changed since the last print:** essentially nothing at the company level that is public. No 8-K since 2 Jul 2026 [22]. The changes are all external — a strong July-quarter software tape, six analyst target raises, and a ~21% one-month share price move.

## 7. Forensics

**The CFO seat is the story.** On 28 April 2026 Braze disclosed (8-K, items 5.02/7.01) that CFO **Isabelle Winkles would step down** to join a privately held company, serving as CFO through 29 May 2026 — deliberately staying to close the quarter and present Q1 results on 27 May — then advising through 17 Aug 2026 [22][38][39]. **Pankaj Malik**, Chief Accounting Officer since 2021, was appointed **Interim CFO** effective 29 May 2026 (8-K 27 May 2026, item 5.02) [22][38]. A formal search for a permanent CFO was opened; **no permanent CFO appointment has been announced as of 8 Sep 2026** [38][39]. The 28 April 8-K explicitly reaffirmed Q1 and FY27 guidance at the same time as the departure — a deliberate decoupling of the personnel news from the numbers [40].

**Inference (mine):** this is the interim CFO's first guide. That cuts one way on process risk and another on behaviour: an interim CFO with no permanent mandate has strong incentives to guide conservatively and to avoid raising the profit commitment. That is a real, if unquantifiable, tilt against the "raise both lines" outcome that the stock needs.

**Other executive turnover.** 8-K of 7 April 2026 (item 5.02): General Counsel and Secretary **Susan Wiseman** announced her intention to retire on or before 30 June 2026 [22][41]. A new CIO was also disclosed alongside the CFO transition [40]. **Inference:** three C-suite/officer changes (CFO, GC, CIO) inside roughly two months is above a normal cadence for a company this size, though none carries an accounting flavour.

**Auditor / restatement.** Ernst & Young LLP ratified at the 30 June 2026 annual meeting with 93.3M votes for — no dissent of note, no auditor change, no restatement, no going-concern language in the Q1 FY27 10-Q [2][23].

**Governance signals from the 30 June 2026 annual meeting** [23]: say-on-pay passed 50.3M for / 17.9M against — **~26% against**. Director Neeraj Agrawal (Battery Ventures) drew 14.6M withheld against 53.7M for — **~21% withheld**. **Read-through (mine).** Two things fall out cleanly. Second, and cutting the other way, the one company in the set whose business, size, growth rate and NRR most closely resemble Braze's — Klaviyo, 26% growth, NRR 109% versus Braze's 110% — was punished 11.5% for beating revenue and lowering the profit line. Salesforce's Agentforce number also raises the bar on what an "AI monetisation" disclosure has to look like to move a stock in September 2026: $5.7M of Decisioning Studio revenue growing 15–20% sequentially is a real but small number against that comparison.

**Customer/supplier read-through.** Braze is a consumption-influenced SaaS whose revenue rides consumer-brand messaging volume; there is no single-customer dependency (no customer >10% of revenue) [2]. ## 9. Bull case / bear case / base case

**Bull case.** The business is accelerating and the tape is paying for beats. Four consecutive quarters of organic revenue acceleration to +27% organic, DBNRR up three straight quarters to 110%/111%, RPO +30% and cRPO +28% y/y, ARR through $800M, record FCF of $26.8M, and +300bps of y/y margin expansion in Q1 [1][2][15][29]. Braze has beaten its own revenue guide by 2.9–5.0% in every quarter I could check [9][10][13][25]. The July-quarter comp set — Salesforce +14%, Twilio +16.7%, Sprout Social +26.3%, PubMatic +31.9% — says beats are being rewarded, hard [17][49][51]. Seven target raises in twelve sessions from TD Cowen, Oppenheimer, BTIG, JPMorgan, Goldman, Barclays and DA Davidson, with the top of the range now $40, means the sell side is positioned to chase further [16][21][31][32]. 14.9% of the float is short with 6.5 days to cover [20]. And the stock gave back 7.4% in the final week into the print [21], meaning the run-in was partly unwound. Beat revenue, raise FY27 to ~$908M, take FY27 non-GAAP operating income above $74M, and print DBNRR at 111%, and the ±14% implied move is easily exceeded to the upside — as it was in three of the last four prints [6].

**Bear case.** The bar is set at the top of guidance and the profit line is pre-committed. Consensus revenue growth of +22.4% implies ~$220.4M against a guided range topping out at $220.5M [17] — the Street has already taken the guide, so a normal-sized beat produces an in-line-feeling headline. Management framed FY27's +400bps of margin expansion as a promise to be *achieved*, not exceeded [15], which makes a profit-guidance raise structurally unlikely; the same configuration produced a −16.1% after-hours reaction on 27 May 2026 and an explicit market complaint that "the company is targeting the same profit range even though its sales target has increased" [11][12]. The closest comparable business in the market, Klaviyo, was taken down 11.55% four weeks ago for precisely this [19]. Gross margin is structurally drifting on messaging and hosting mix [14]. The AI product that is carrying the narrative did $5.7M in a $211M quarter and is capacity-constrained on delivery headcount [15], against Salesforce's $1.5B Agentforce run-rate [49]. Positioning is the accelerant: put/call at 0.16, more than 1.3 standard deviations below the 20-day average and near a 52-week low, means the long book is unhedged [21], and the mean target of $36.25 is only ~13% above spot after a 76% six-month move [16][21]. An interim CFO with no permanent mandate delivers the guide [38]. And the historical pattern is uncomfortable: Braze's three large positive earnings reactions (Sep 2025, Dec 2025, Mar 2026) all came when it entered the print depressed, while its two large negative ones (Sep 2024, Jun 2025) and the May 2026 decline came after run-ups — **this is my inference from the price context in [7][10][11][13][14], not a sourced study.**

**Base case (mine).** Braze beats Q2 revenue by roughly 2.5–3.5% (≈$225–228M, +25–27% y/y), delivers non-GAAP EPS at or slightly above $0.16, prints DBNRR of 110–111%, reports AI Decisioning Studio revenue near $6.6–6.8M, and raises FY27 revenue guidance to roughly $903–910M while reaffirming or only nominally lifting the $70–74M non-GAAP operating income range. Under that outcome the stock is a coin-flip that leans slightly negative: the revenue number will be fine and the reaction will hinge entirely on the profit line and on management's tone about the second half. Given a ±14% implied move, an unhedged long book, a mean target only 13% above spot, and a peer set that just demonstrated it will pay for margin and punish its absence, I expect the distribution to be wide and modestly skewed down — while acknowledging that Braze has produced three double-digit *upside* reactions in the last four prints, which is exactly why my conviction here is medium and not high.

## 10. What would flip the consensus view

The most credible reversal is **an FY27 non-GAAP operating income guidance raise above $74M combined with FY27 revenue guidance above ~$905M and DBNRR at 111% or better.** That single combination retires the only bear argument that has actually moved this stock in 2026 — that Braze converts revenue upside into spending rather than profit — and it would do so in a tape that has just paid +14% for Salesforce and +16.7% for Twilio for exactly that behaviour [49][51]. Concretely: Q2 non-GAAP operating income of ~$19–20M (versus $17–18M guided), FY27 non-GAAP operating income taken to $76–80M, and a Decisioning Studio revenue figure of $7M+ with commentary that the delivery-staffing bottleneck is closed. With 14.9% of the float short [20] and put/call at 0.16 [21], that outcome would likely produce a move well beyond the implied ±14% to the upside and would force the $36.25 mean target [21] toward the $40 top of the range within days.

The mirror image — the flip against the bulls — is far simpler and needs no drama: revenue of ~$222–224M (a sub-2% beat, breaking the beat-size trend from 5.0% → 3.6% → 2.9%), FY27 revenue taken only to $900–902M, the $70–74M profit range untouched, and DBNRR flat at 110%. That is the May 2026 print repeated with a smaller cushion and a higher starting price.
