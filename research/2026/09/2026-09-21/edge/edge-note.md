## What this note is required to say

**Separate the order from the sign.** The table above ranks every name. Only HERE clears
the conviction floor. **Below the floor the sign is a coin flip on the evidence so far —
53% over 38 de-duplicated events** — so a reader who treats MLKN's +2.10 as a bullish view
or THO's −0.50 as a bearish one is reading the table wrong. Above the floor, the rank of
conviction predicted sign-correctness at ρ=+0.514 (permutation p=0.0015) on the fitted
sample; note that the forward regime is one hunter per name, where the same figure is
**+0.361**, and that is the number to expect. **Four of today's five rows sit below the
floor, so on four of five names this table states an order and not a direction.**

**`impact_sum` is not a forecast of the move.** It ranks; it does not size. The same fact
often reaches two findings from two sources and adding both double-counts it — that is
what the cluster-max was built to stop, and the measurement demoted the cluster-max. The
hunters' own expected moves differ from their finding sums in four of five names here, in
both directions (MLKN +3.5 vs +2.1; HERE −4.0 vs −3.7; THO +1.0 vs −0.5; AZO +1.2 vs +1.9).

**The control, and it is close.** `−run_up_20d_pct` ranks the day: THO +12.53, MLKN +12.38,
ABVX +10.33, AZO +4.50, HERE +1.62. **The hunt's order differs from it substantially —
Spearman ρ ≈ 0.30 between the two on these five names** — and the disagreement is
concentrated in one name: THO is the control's *top* pick and the hunt's *fourth*. HERE is
last on both. On the six resolved runs the free control ranked at ρ=0.335 against the
hunt's raw 0.407, a gap whose CI spans zero; on the larger 105-event sample the control
returns −0.42% per trade and is not stable either. **The hunt has still not been shown to
beat one number available off the sealed baseline before any subagent is spawned.**

**Sign balance: 3 positive, 2 negative** (MLKN, AZO, ABVX positive; THO, HERE negative).
That is the *opposite* of this stage's usual tilt — six of eight leaned negative on
2026-08-31, and the standing worry is that asking hunters to find what the market has
missed into a print generates pessimism. Today it did not. One day does not settle it; the
count is recorded so the pattern is visible across days.

**Nothing checked the findings.** There is no adversary pass and no second hunter
(`adversary_judged` is 0 of N on every row). A factually wrong finding entered the key at
full size and nothing in this run would have caught it. That is the accepted cost of
one hunter per name. The adversary was the only thing that ever caught a misread covenant
date or a claim contradicted by its own source.

**The key is not reproducible to better than its own size.** When the stage still
double-hunted, twelve paired names came back with a median gap of 2.40 points and **four of
the twelve had opposite signs**, on a key whose typical magnitude is about 5. Nothing
re-measures that now. On today's table that caveat is larger than the gap between ranks 1
and 4: **MLKN +2.10, AZO +1.90, ABVX +0.20 and THO −0.50 span 2.6 points, which is inside
the reproducibility noise.** Only HERE sits clearly outside it.

**How much of the baseline was measured rather than inferred.** Four of five names carry a
live option chain (AZO, ABVX, THO, MLKN) and HERE carries none — nominally the
best-instrumented day this stage has had. **But two of those four chains are not usable as
a statement of what the market priced:** MLKN has 75 contracts of total open interest with
an ATM spread 80% of mid, and ABVX has 9,137 contracts at 50% of mid. So the honest count
is **two names with a usable anchor (AZO 2,868 contracts at 10.1%, THO 1,362 at 11.8%), two
with a nominal one, and one with none.** Where there is no chain, `priced_lean_pct` falls
back to −0.05 × the 20-day run-up and the "expected move" is a historical median rather
than a priced expectation — HERE's 3.78% is exactly that, and its hunter's whole first
finding is that the median is wrong. `edge_resolve.py` still normalises by expected move,
so a normalised correlation on this day is not an implied-move measure.

**What the day would have cost to trade.** Four of five names are reachable: AZO $890m/day,
ABVX $110.8m, THO $45.8m, MLKN $12.6m — all above the $1m `thin` threshold, the most liquid
set this stage has ranked. **The exception is the one name that matters:** HERE, the single
floor-clearer and the largest conviction in the run, trades $0.04m a day and is
untradeable. So **the extreme of today's ranking is the unreachable end of it**, which is
the 2026-09-02 DLTH shape again (+23.20% on $170k a day). A reader should treat today's
ranking as a research result and not as a signal, because the only row the rule would have
selected could not have been bought or sold.

## Caveats specific to this run

- **ABVX's session is not company-confirmed.** The `amc` label is inferred from the
  comparable H1 2025 release, datelined 22:00 CEST = 16:00 ET — within minutes of the US
  close. Part of any reaction can land in the 2026-09-21 regular session rather than
  cleanly on 09-22. `edge_resolve.py` measures over the session recorded, so this row's
  measured move carries that risk. Abivax hosts no call for financial prints, so there was
  no call time to settle it with, and `ir.abivax.com` returned HTTP 503 on nine attempts
  across the sweep and the hunt.
- **MLKN's baseline has the wrong session mapping.** All seven of its prior prints are
  tagged `amc` in the sealed baseline and this one is company-confirmed `bmo` (release
  before the open, call 08:30 ET). The reaction *magnitudes* are sound — 8-K item 2.02
  acceptance times, exact — but a hunter inheriting the mapping would size an overnight gap
  instead of a regular session. The hunter was told explicitly and sized against the
  session.
- **Two baselines carry `baseline_history_trustworthy: false`** (ABVX, HERE), both flagged
  by the sweep and both passed to the hunter. ABVX's −44.1% "print" is the ABTECT Phase 3
  maintenance readout, not an earnings reaction; its genuine financial releases moved
  −4.55% and +3.62%. HERE's three baseline prints came from 6-K text matching on a 61-day
  inferred cadence. Both hunters rebuilt a base rate and said what they used.
- **`researcher_us/LESSONS.md` moved 4 of 5 names**, and it shrank three of them: MLKN
  2.5→2.1, AZO 3.2→1.9, ABVX 0.4→0.2, HERE −3.5→−3.7. THO's *sum* is unchanged at −0.5,
  but that is a coincidence and not a null: all four of its finding sizes moved and the
  file cut its negatives and its positives by 1.0 point each. The lessons
  control is live and `edge_resolve.py` will rank `spearman_pre_lessons` against the same
  realised move. Note that **AZO would have cleared the floor on its pre-lessons sum of
  +3.2 and does not on +1.9** — the guidance file changed the composition of today's book,
  which is precisely the kind of effect the control exists to catch.
- **All five hunts returned the current contract** — `print_vs_bar_pct`, `expected_move_pct`,
  `bar`, `positioning_check`, `pre_lessons`, `lands_on` and `resolves_by`. No hunt ran under
  the old contract.
- **Reachability:** `nasdaq.com` returned HTTP 503 on every WebFetch of a press-release
  page, so the AZO and THO date confirmations are `snippet_only` (both corroborated
  elsewhere); `ir.abivax.com`, `ir.heregroup.com`, NAII's IR host and `seekingalpha.com`
  (403) were also unreachable.

## One day is an anecdote

Five names cannot produce a meaningful rank correlation, and nothing in this note should be
read as one. The pooled figure across many days is the result. On the current pooled
sample the stage's own dashboard prices 102 ranked names over 11 days at ρ = −0.145 against
−0.137 for the free control, and every day from 2026-09-08 onward sits at or below zero —
so the encouraging numbers quoted above were measured on the first six days and are not
what the ledger currently reads.
