## 5. Thesis
**Bull case:** the FY27 bar is already reset to the floor (Q1 guide $50-54M is -26% y/y but easy to
clear), there was no preliminary-results warning this cycle (unlike the prior two quarters, both of
which preannounced misses), and 34.65% of float is short against only 9.65 days-to-cover — an in-line
print with a reaffirmed FY27 range is squeeze fuel.
**Bear case:** the underlying business keeps shrinking (FY26 revenue -35.7%, RPO -13.6% y/y), the
company quietly dropped the operating KPIs (partner deals, pipeline growth) it used to lead with, and
named-customer PR cadence has collapsed to roughly a fifth of the year-ago rate — forensic signs of a
business still getting worse even if the headline print is in-line.
**Base case:** an in-line-to-modest-beat quarter with the FY27 range reaffirmed rather than raised or
cut, producing a move inside the historical range but without enough conviction either way to be worth
a directional bet — consistent with the panel's own Neutral call.

the absolute numbers. The case that would confirm the bearish lean is a *cut* to the FY27 range, or
