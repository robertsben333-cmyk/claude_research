**This is the first directional call scored in this ledger.** Every panelled call before
it — fifteen in a row across seven prior run days — was `Neutral / No Edge`. LEN broke
that streak with a real signed call, `Lean Down` at 59.5% probability down, `High`
certainty (disparity 12.5, panel aligned 7-0 bearish). It missed.

**What actually happened:** GAAP EPS $1.19 against a ~$1.29-1.30 consensus and a guided
range of $1.20-1.40 — below even the low end of Lennar's own guide. Revenue $8.05B
against ~$8.31-8.32B consensus, a miss. FY26 delivery guidance cut to 80,000-81,000 homes
from the prior 82,000-83,000. Every one of those is the bear case the panel built,
printing almost exactly as feared. Home-sales gross margin came in at 15.8%, clearing
Q2's 15.6% — the one specific number the red-team's reversal case needed — but the
compound condition that case actually stated (margin beat *and* FY guide intact)
did not hold, because the delivery guide was cut. The FOMC decision 90 minutes before the
release, which three of seven personas flagged as the largest swing risk, delivered a
25bp hike, 12-0, with 16 of 18 officials projecting another hike this year — the hawkish,
fully-priced outcome the panel's bear case assumed, not a dovish surprise.

And the stock closed up 1.71% anyway. Every headline number the panel leaned on for its
bearish read was negative or in line with the bear case — EPS miss, revenue miss,
guidance cut, no dovish Fed rescue — and the direction still inverted. The shape looks
like relief off an already-washed-out setup (4-year low, ~0.9x tangible book, 8.5% short
interest, every analyst target already at or below spot, zero upgrades in the run-up)
rather than either the bull or bear mechanism the panel named. Four of seven personas
independently flagged "the bar is already shredded" as a secondary risk to their own
bearish call — the options-positioning persona explicitly warned that "any margin/orders
positive against a floored consensus has nothing standing in the way of a 7-9% gap" — and
that risk is closer to what fired than the stated reversal case.

**What the process got right and wrong.** The panel correctly identified the actual bear
inputs (EPS miss, revenue miss, guide cut) and still called the wrong direction, because
it treated "the bar is already this low" as a secondary caveat rather than the dominant
factor. This is a genuinely new failure mode for the ledger, distinct from the earlier
Neutral-call misses (TTAN, ESTC, SNOW, OKTA): those were the panel correctly staying
uncommitted on a move it then got the magnitude of wrong; this is the panel making a
directional call, getting every fundamental input right, and still landing on the wrong
sign because expectations positioning dominated the print. "The bar was already priced
for disaster" is a fixable finding — it argues for weighting positioning/expectations
inputs (the options-positioning and sentiment personas, both of whom flagged this)
higher relative to fundamentals-only reads (fundamental, base-rate) when the two
disagree on magnitude even while agreeing on sign, not a claim that the market was
irrational.

The preliminary (stage 2) read also missed, at the same sign (−22), so this single event
gives no separation between the panel and the single deep researcher — both were wrong
for the same underlying reason (both under-weighted how much bad news was already in a
4-year-low, 8.5%-short stock).
