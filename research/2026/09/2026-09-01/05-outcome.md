## DELL — miss, panel had no view on a 15.81% move

Non-GAAP EPS $7.04 vs. ~$4.88 consensus (beat by ~44%), revenue $47.0B vs. ~$45.0B
consensus (beat, +58% y/y). FY27 revenue guidance raised $25B to $192B, EPS guidance
raised to $25.50 — an emphatic beat-and-raise. Shares gapped up 8.7% and extended the
gain all session to close +15.81%, breaking the 11.5% unsigned expected move. The
Neutral call is a miss under the below-expected-move convention, though it lands inside
its wide 5.7–34.4% band. The red-team's specific reversal case — a HP-style margin
squeeze from the memory cost cycle overwhelming a headline beat — did not fire; the
$51.3B AI-server backlog carried the print instead, exactly the scenario that same
persona flagged as the top risk to its own bearish call. Stage-2 preliminary (+20,
bullish) called the direction correctly.

## CXM — miss, but the red-team's mechanism actually fired

Non-GAAP EPS $0.11 vs. $0.10 consensus (beat), revenue $213.7M vs. ~$215.6M consensus
(miss, ~−0.9%). Q3 revenue and EPS guidance both came in below consensus; FY27
subscription outlook raised only modestly with total revenue held flat. No
options-implied move existed to size against. Shares opened roughly flat, rallied to
+2.8% intraday, then reversed hard to close at the day's low, −8.55%, breaking the 6.8%
unsigned expected move. The Neutral call is a miss under the below-expected-move
convention — but this is the run's most informative reversal case: the red-team's
explicit thesis, "a beat is not enough a third year running because cRPO and guidance
stay soft," is exactly what happened (cRPO growth stayed low-single-digit, Q3 guide
disappointed, no forward inflection materialised). Stage-2 preliminary (−25, bearish)
called the direction correctly and was, notably, the only source — panel included — that
flagged the guide rather than the headline print as the risk.

## Where the process failed

**Both panelled calls missed, and both missed the same way: the panel hedged to
Neutral on names that then moved comfortably past their own expected move.** DELL and
CXM are now the fourth and fifth panelled calls in a row (after WOLF, OKTA, DLTR) to be
Neutral / No Edge — the panel has still never made a single directional call across
seven panelled names to date. DELL's miss cost nothing directionally (the move was
favourable to being long, just larger than sized); CXM's miss is more informative,
because the red-team's stated mechanism (soft guide undercutting a technical beat) is
exactly what happened, and the synthesis step still collapsed that into "no edge" rather
than leaning bearish on a persona that had both the direction and the reason right.

**Margin compression, not the headline print, drove three of the four non-panelled
misses today (MDB, CRDO, and arguably GIII's mixed picture).** MDB and CRDO both beat
every consensus line and both fell double digits on guidance/margin detail buried below
the headline — exactly the kind of signal the dossier's own margin/guidance sections are
supposed to catch. Worth checking whether the triage stage is deprioritizing margin-line
detail in favor of top-line beat/miss framing when it scores "how much could this move."

**Non-panelled preliminary reads went 2-for-4 today (GIII hit, GTLB/MDB/CRDO miss),** a
big step down from the 3-for-3 on 08-27's non-panelled names — another data point that
the preliminary read's edge is not a stable, structural advantage over the panel so much
as noisy performance that varies run to run.
