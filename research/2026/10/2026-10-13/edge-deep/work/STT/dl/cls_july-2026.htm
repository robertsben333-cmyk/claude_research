                        2.66USD trillion
                        Total daily traded volume submitted to CLS 
                        up 14.7% from July 2025

                        Lisa Danino-Lewis
                        Chief Growth Officer

                        “In July 2026, we saw average daily traded volumes of USD2.66 trillion, an increase of 14.7% compared to July 2025. Over the same period, we saw an increase in volumes across all instruments. The increase was 18% for FX forwards, 14.9% for FX swaps and 12.7% for FX spot.”
