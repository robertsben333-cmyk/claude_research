## What went wrong

- **The sweep checked 29 of 118 names** for time, confirming 23. The 7 unchecked names filled to the cap of 30 were each confirmed by their own searcher, which also settled TTMI and CRS as bmo against the sweep's "historically amc" note.
- **NP and WFRD (10-20 amc legs) were still held at 13:53 ET** on their exit date. The open exit did not happen, and step 3s sold them at market.
- **All 8 judge instances hit HTTP 429 (account session limit) at about 18:00 UTC**, before any output existed except five complete part files. After the reset, Opus 5, Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 were re-run on part 2 at 20:03 UTC. The panel was scored at about 20:10 UTC, **after the 16:00 ET close and after the amc releases had started**. The judges read only the packs, written before 16:00 ET, so their inputs are pre-print; their output is still post-close. Step 6b was skipped on the operator's instruction, so nothing was bought.
- An LVS search result listed a pre-staged IR URL that looks like tonight's release. The searcher did not open it.

## What the numbers are not

`expected_return_pct` (and `expected_edge_pct`) is what names at that percentile have earned. Its out-of-sample record is weak: correlation +0.12 with the realised return over 148 names. It is not a forecast to size on. The weights are research. Certainty (`p_up`) is recorded and decides nothing.
