  2. Reasons for Revision
  Based on steady progress in business performance through the third quarter, the lower limits of the
  forecast ranges for net sales and adjusted operating profit are expected to exceed the previous
  forecasts. Specifically, that for net sales is now expected to be 53,571 million yen (up 1.6% from the
  previously announced value) and for adjusted operating profit is expected to be 8,035 million yen (up
  17.3%).
