Investor Relations

Kongsberg Maritime ASA develops and delivers technology, integrated systems and services for more than 30,000 vessels worldwide, supporting safer, more efficient and more sustainable maritime operations. On this website you will find information for shareholders, analysts and other stakeholders, including share information, major shareholders, reports and presentations, dividend policy, general meetings, analyst coverage, financial calendar and credit rating information.

Major shareholders

Kongsberg Maritime was listed on the Oslo Stock Exchange in 2026 after being demerged from the Kongsberg Group. The Norwegian state holds a 50.004% ownership stake.
