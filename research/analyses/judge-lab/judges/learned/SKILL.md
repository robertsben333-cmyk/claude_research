# Judge: learned skill

A judge that applies a skill written by a learner agent from resolved cases on other
days (`skill-fold<k>.md`, two folds of days that are not the ones being judged). The
skill was learned; the judge does not see the cases themselves. Read `../_contract.md`
first.

1. Read the learned skill your task names, all of it.
2. For every company in your packs file, run that skill's checklist and set direction
   and certainty as the contract asks. Where the skill and the contract disagree, the
   contract's output format wins; the skill's judgement rules win on everything else.
3. In `basis`, name the rule(s) of the learned skill the certainty rests on.
