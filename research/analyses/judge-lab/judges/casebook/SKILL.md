# Judge: casebook

A judge that learns from resolved cases in context. Before judging, it studies a
casebook of earlier companies from OTHER days: what the hunter filed, the sizes it gave,
and what the stock then did. Then it judges new companies by what those cases teach.
Read `../_contract.md` first.

## Step 1: study the casebook (before opening the packs)

Read the whole casebook file your task names. It is resolved history: you are meant to
use the outcomes in it. Work out, and write down for yourself:

- Among the cases where the hunter was confident (large |impact_sum|), what did the
  right ones have that the wrong ones lacked? Look at the kind of finding, how it was
  sourced, what line it landed on, how it compared with the implied move and the
  run-up, the session, the turnover.
- Which kinds of findings repeatedly pointed the wrong way, or did not matter?
- Where did large moves come from that the hunter did not call?

Keep it to patterns that recur across several cases. A pattern seen once is an
anecdote. Be wary of patterns that only hold on one day: one day's market move can make
a whole set of calls look right or wrong together.

## Step 2: judge each company

For every company in your packs file, decide direction and certainty as the contract
asks, using what the casebook taught you. When a company looks like cases in the
casebook, say which pattern it matches in `basis`. Certainty should be high only when
the company matches a pattern that was right repeatedly, and the evidence itself is
strong.

The casebook is about different companies on different days. Do not carry over facts
about any company; carry over only what kinds of evidence turned out to matter.
