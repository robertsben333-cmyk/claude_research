# Stage E-P — the searcher and the four-model panel

Stage E-P replaced stage E-S on 2026-10-01, on the same Routine. It asks: **does a panel
of four different models, judging the hunter's evidence blind, pick the names that move
better than the hunter's own number does?** The measurement behind it is in
`research/analyses/judge-lab/` (README rounds 1 and 2, `APPROACH.md` sections 5 and 6):
on 187 resolved development names the hunter's most confident names moved LESS than the
average name, while names that three or four judges each put in their own top 20% hit
18/24 at +6.7% net.

This file is an overlay, not a copy: follow `.claude/skills/earnings-edge-hunt/SKILL.md`
step by step, and do differently only what the table and the new step 5p below say.

Read `CLAUDE.md` first, then the stage E skill in full, then come back here.

## 3u. An unfinished search is not research (operator, 2026-10-28)

A searcher whose WebSearch or WebFetch was refused for a budget, usage or rate limit has
not finished, whatever it wrote. It must never be scored, judged or traded as if it had.
On 2026-10-28 five of twenty searchers ran into the search budget shared across the
session's searchers. Their hunts went through as normal, and two of them (HRTG and CYH)
were bought.

1. **After every searcher returns**, read its hand-back for a refused or exhausted
   search, then run
   ```bash
   python3 researcher_us/scripts/hunt_status.py check --run <RUN>/edge-panel --apply
   ```
   The searcher writes `search_complete: false` itself (`config/searcher-addendum.md`
   item 6). `--apply` marks a hunt whose own text says the search was cut off. If a
   hand-back reports a limit the file does not show, mark it by hand:
   `hunt_status.py mark --run <RUN>/edge-panel --ticker <T> --reason "<what the hand-back said>"`.
   A `no flag` row is a searcher on the old contract: read its hand-back and decide.
2. **Relaunch, do not accept.** For each unfinished name run
   `hunt_status.py retry --run <RUN>/edge-panel --ticker <T>` (the old hunt moves to
   `hunts/_unfinished/`). Then launch a fresh `unpriced-searcher` on it **in a later
   turn**: end your turn and relaunch when the next notification wakes you. The budget
   is per orchestrator turn. On 2026-10-28 every searcher launched after a turn
   boundary kept WebSearch to the end, while those in the same turn as the exhausted
   ones did not. Spread launches over turns from the start: do not launch more than
   eight searchers in one turn.
3. **A name still unfinished when step 3s is due stays unfinished.** That is when the
   last searcher has returned, or 15:15 New York, whichever is first. `edge_score.py`
   marks it not rankable ("research unfinished: ..."), so it is left out of the packs,
   the panel and the book, and it never enters a judge's history. Write a run-log line
   naming every unfinished name and whether it was retried. The note lists them under
   their own heading, never in the ranked table.

## 3s. Sell what is still held, as soon as the searchers have finished

Once every searcher has returned (or been shed), and BEFORE the panel packs are built:

```bash
python3 researcher_us/scripts/alpaca_trade.py mode
python3 researcher_us/scripts/alpaca_trade.py flatten --run <RUN>/edge-panel --submit
```

`flatten` cancels every open order and closes every position at market, then waits until
the account is flat and says if anything is still held. It sells everything, not only
stage E-P's own book: this stage is the only one connected to the account. It refuses
(and sells nothing) when `execution.enabled` is false or credentials are missing; record
the reason in the run log and carry on with the research. If it reports positions still
held, say so in the run log; step 6b nets them off the budget. Write one run-log line
with what was held and what was closed, then publish.

**Budget.** One sweep, up to **twenty** searchers (`edge_panel.hunted_names`; nineteen,
stage E's cap, until 2026-10-21, thirty on 2026-10-21 only) in stage E's waves, then four
judges in one wave. Shed names by `budget.edge_degrade_order` exactly as stage E does,
cutting at 20; never shed a judge to save a searcher. In the run log's wave line say how many were confirmed,
how many hunted and which were shed, and launch the amc names first so a long day cannot
push one past its release. Relaunch as slots free: the platform runs about 8 at once.
The buy at step 6b needs an open market. A 19-name day reached it about 45 minutes after
the fire. If the last searcher returns after 15:15 New York, write that in the run log, since
step 6b refuses after 16:00.

## 5p. The panel

Run it after step 5 (`edge_score.py --run <RUN>/edge-panel`) has written `edge-scores.json`,
so the packs hold exactly the names the stage ranks.

1. **Packs.**
   ```bash
   python3 researcher_us/scripts/panel_packs.py --run <RUN>/edge-panel
   ```
   It writes `<RUN>/edge-panel/panel/packs.json`, the searchers' evidence with every size,
   verdict and final number removed, one field per line. If it reports 0 packs, there is
   nothing to judge: say so in the note and skip to 6.

   **More than 20 packs (`edge_panel.judge_pack_max`; at the current cap this does not
   happen) and it also prints `SPLIT`** and writes `packs-p1.json`, `packs-p2.json`: about
   20 KB a name, so a much larger day does not fit one judge's context. Then every judge runs once per part, on that part's file,
   writing `<member>-p1.json`, `<member>-p2.json`; `panel_score.py` merges them. Never
   hand a judge `packs.json` on a split day.

2. **Heartbeat**, then publish, before spending anything:
   `python3 scripts/run_log.py --heading "Edge hunt (panel) — panel STARTED" --line "<n> packs, four judges"`.

3. **Four judges, in ONE message, in parallel**, one per model, each with this prompt and
   nothing else (fill in the two paths):

   > Follow your definition. Your packs file is `<RUN>/edge-panel/panel/packs.json`. Write your JSON array to
   > `<RUN>/edge-panel/panel/<member>.json`. Use only Read and Write.

   | member | agent | fallback if the agent or its model is refused |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | `opus5` | `panel-judge-opus5` (`claude-opus-5`) | **none**: no alias reaches Opus 5. Run without it and record it missing |
   | `opus55` | `panel-judge-opus55` (`claude-opus-5-5`) | `general-purpose`, `model: opus`, body of `config/panel-judge.md` pasted in |
   | `sonnet55` | `panel-judge-sonnet55` (`claude-sonnet-5-5`) | `general-purpose`, `model: sonnet`, same |
   | `fable51` | `panel-judge-fable51` (`claude-fable-5-1`) | `general-purpose`, `model: fable`, same |

   On a split day launch all of them in the same message, four judges times the parts
   (eight for two parts), each with its part's packs file and its part's output path.

   **Never give a judge anything beyond the packs file**: not the hunts, not the
   searcher's scores, not another judge's output, not your own view. Their independence
   is the whole point of the panel.

4. **Check coverage.** Every pack `id` must appear in every member's file (on a split day,
   in the member's part files together). Re-launch a
   member once for the ids it missed (a second file `<member>-retry.json`, merged by hand
   into `<member>.json` before scoring). A member that fails twice is missing for the day.

5. **Combine.**
   ```bash
   python3 researcher_us/scripts/panel_score.py --run <RUN>/edge-panel
   ```
   It reads each member's sizes against that member's own live history from earlier runs
   (`researcher_us/analysis/panel-history.json`) as a signed PERCENTILE, averages the four
   into `panel_pct`, and **selects a name when every member is present and |panel_pct| is
   80 or more** (the mean in the top 20%; since 2026-10-14). The 3-of-4 verdict, the z
   and `panel_score` are still written. It writes `<RUN>/edge-panel/edge-scores-panel.json`
   and appends the day's sizes to the history. Fewer than four members is allowed: the
   mean is then over the members that ran, and the note must say which model was missing.

6. **Record the panel** in `<RUN>/edge-panel/provenance.json`: add a `panel` block with,
   per member, the agent that ran (`panel-judge-…` or the fallback), the model pinned in
   its frontmatter, the model it reported if it reported one, and `missing` where it did
   not run. The searcher's block is already there from step 2b.

## 6. The note (stage E's note, with the panel first)

`<RUN>/edge-panel/edge-note.md`, answer first:

1. Three lines at the top: **this is stage E-P: Opus 5.5 searchers, a blind four-model
   panel, no orders**; which members ran; that one day is an anecdote.
2. **The panel table**, every rankable name: rank, ticker, session, `selected`,
   `panel_pct` (0-100) and its side (long/short) in two columns, its spread, each member's
   own percentile 0-100 with L or S (`*` where in its own top 20%), then `consensus_k` and
   the old `panel_score`. Never print a negative percentile: the side is its own column,
   the searcher's own `impact_sum`, the **expected return at that percentile**
   (`expected_return_pct` with its 80% band and `p_right`), `weight_equal` and
   `weight_precision_tilt`. The percentile leads: every name at the same `panel_pct` has the
   same expected return, so present it as the translation of the percentile, never as a
   separate forecast. Say that it is gross, on the scorebord's window, and flat at about
   +0.3% below a percentile of 50. Report the judges in percentiles, never in raw sizes: a
   top-10% Opus 5 and a top-10% Opus 5.5 are the same statement at different sizes.
3. One paragraph per **selected** name: the evidence items the members' notes name, and
   the strongest case against.
4. Where the panel and the searcher disagree most, one line each.
5. If stage E's `edge-scores.json` exists by then: the names both ranked and the Spearman
   ρ between stage E's `impact_sum` and `panel_pct`. If not, say so; do not wait.
6. What the numbers are not: `expected_return_pct` (and `expected_edge_pct`, which on a
   selected name now carries the same expectation as a signed move) is what names at that
   percentile have earned, with a weak out-of-sample record (correlation +0.12 with the
   realised return over 148 names), not a forecast to size on; the weights are research, nothing trades them; certainty (`p_up`) is
   recorded and decides nothing because it did not predict on the development names.

Then stage E's standing note sections apply where they fit.

Run stage E's step 6d on this directory before the closing reply
(`python3 researcher_us/scripts/edge_context.py --run <RUN>/edge-panel || true`), so the
table's retail, search and volatility columns are filled. Context only, as in stage E: the panel
decides nothing on them.

**The closing chat reply** starts with the verbatim output of
`python3 scripts/score_report.py --run <RUN>/edge-panel --label "Stage E-P (searcher)"`
(the searcher's impact_sum, impact_scaled and V2), then the same command on `<RUN>/edge`
with `--label "Stage E"` if stage E's files exist, then the panel table from
`panel_score.py`'s output. Finish with
`python3 scripts/update_index.py` and `scripts/publish.sh "edge hunt panel: <what> for
<YYYY-MM-DD>"`. It pushes to `main`.

## 6b. Buy the panel's book, after the note is published

```bash
python3 researcher_us/scripts/alpaca_trade.py plan --run <RUN>/edge-panel
python3 researcher_us/scripts/alpaca_trade.py open --run <RUN>/edge-panel --submit --no-flatten
python3 researcher_us/scripts/alpaca_trade.py status --run <RUN>/edge-panel
```

The rule is `execution.panel` in `config/pipeline.yaml` (operator's instruction,
2026-10-19), applied by `select_panel()` and `size()` in `alpaca_trade.py`:

- **Entry**: `panel_pct` >= 80 with all four judges present; never at or below 60. Side
  from the panel. The same $200k turnover floor as stage E.
- **Weight**: `panel_pct` - 60, linear (90 / 90 / 75 -> 40 / 40 / 20).
- **Cap**: 50% of equity a name; what a capped name cannot take goes to the others in
  proportion to their weights, so one name is 50% and cash, two names are 50 / 50.
- **Cannot trade or short**: dropped before the split, so its share goes to the rest.
- **Budget**: 100% of equity minus anything still held after step 3s.

Market orders, so the session must still be open: `open` refuses a closed market rather
than queue an order, and a run that finishes after 16:00 ET buys nothing; say so. Never
pass `--top`, `--min-pct` or `--allow-stale` on a scheduled run; they exist for a
deliberate test. Write one run-log line with what was sent and filled, publish, and put
the plan table in the closing reply.

## 7. Resolve

`edge_resolve.py --run <RUN>/edge-panel` ranks the searcher's key and, beside it,
`spearman_panel` from `edge-scores-panel.json`. The question that matters for this stage
is not ρ over the whole day but whether the **selected** names moved and paid: the
judge-lab scripts (`research/analyses/judge-lab/magnitude.py`, `consensus_top.py`) are how
that was measured, and forward days should be added to them, not judged one at a time.

This is research, not investment advice.
