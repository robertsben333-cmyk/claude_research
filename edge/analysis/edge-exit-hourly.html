What the shape says

amc prints are done reacting before the bell. The book is already worth +7.4% an hour after the close and gains nothing more overnight; it peaks at +8.45% on the 09:00 pre-market print, then loses three points across the session it is held through. For these names the opening auction is the exit, and it is the only pre-session hour with real liquidity.

bmo prints have not happened yet at hour 1. The flat line near zero until hour 14 is not a finding about the market, it is the print not existing. From 07:00 the book turns on and then climbs monotonically to its best value at the close. For these names, holding is right.

Read the pre-market hours as prices, not as fills. This source reports no volume on extended-hours bars, so every hour before 09:30 is a price that existed and not size that could have traded. Six of the 22 book positions turn over under $1m a day. The 09:00 peak is a real number about information; it is not a real number about capacity.

And the split was read off these same 38 events. A paired bootstrap over the five days puts the amc-at-the-open, bmo-at-the-close rule ahead of the current uniform close by +1.87 points per trade with a 95% interval of [−1.30, +4.55], and the best of six candidate rules beats the close in 91% of resamples. Run it forward before believing it.
