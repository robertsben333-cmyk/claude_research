# Lessons — stage CA

**Deliberately empty. Nothing has resolved in Canada.**

This file exists so `unpriced-hunter-ca` has something to read at step 5 and so the
`pre_lessons` control is live from the first run: a hunter that reads an empty file
emits `pre_lessons` equal to its final set, which is the correct measurement of a file
that says nothing, not a missing measurement.

Rules go in here only when a resolved Canadian run has paid for them. Do not copy
`researcher_us/LESSONS.md` across. Its rules were bought with US events on a US
calendar against a US consensus, and the two things most likely to matter here — an
absent consensus EPS and an anchor that exists for half the names — have no US analogue
at all. `researcher_canada/scripts/ca_resolve.py` scores a resolved run; what it finds
is what belongs on this page.
