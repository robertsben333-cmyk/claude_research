           Kroger (NYSE: KR)  reported first quarter 2026 results with total sales of  $46.1 billion , up from $45.1 billion. Identical sales without fuel increased  1.0% , while EPS was  $1.46  and adjusted EPS  $1.58 . Operating profit reached  $1,407 million  and adjusted FIFO operating profit  $1,544 million . Adjusted eCommerce sales grew  19%  and Kroger Precision Marketing profit grew over  20% . Gross margin declined to  22.7% . Kroger reaffirmed full-year 2026 guidance, including EPS of  $5.10–$5.30 , free cash flow of  $2.7–$2.9 billion , and capital expenditures of  $3.8–$4.0 billion . The company highlighted a  $2 billion  share repurchase authorization and a net debt to adjusted EBITDA ratio of  1.75 .

           FIFO (first-in, first-out) is an accounting and inventory rule that treats the oldest acquired items or shares as the ones sold first, like taking the oldest milk from the front of a fridge before newer cartons. For investors, FIFO matters because it changes reported profits, inventory values and tax bills — in rising-price environments it usually shows higher profits and higher taxes than alternative methods, affecting how company performance and cash flow are interpreted.

     FAQ   
   
  
  
     
         How did Kroger (KR) perform in its first quarter 2026 earnings? 
         
          Kroger reported higher sales and earnings in first quarter 2026. According to Kroger, sales were $46.1 billion, EPS was $1.46, adjusted EPS was $1.58, and operating profit reached $1,407 million, supported by 1.0% identical sales growth without fuel.
         
     
  
     
         What were Kroger's identical sales results without fuel in Q1 2026? 
         
          Kroger's identical sales without fuel increased 1.0% in Q1 2026. According to Kroger, this figure includes an approximately 130 basis point unfavorable impact from the Inflation Reduction Act, highlighting regulatory effects on comparable sales performance during the quarter.
         
     
  
     
         What full-year 2026 guidance did Kroger (NYSE: KR) reaffirm on June 18, 2026? 
         
          Kroger reaffirmed its previously issued full-year 2026 financial guidance. According to Kroger, it expects identical sales without fuel growth of 1.0–2.0%, FIFO operating profit of $5.0–$5.2 billion, EPS of $5.10–$5.30, free cash flow of $2.7–$2.9 billion, and capital expenditures of $3.8–$4.0 billion.
         
     
  
     
         How fast did Kroger's eCommerce and Kroger Precision Marketing grow in Q1 2026? 
         
          Kroger reported strong growth in digital and media-related businesses in Q1 2026. According to Kroger, adjusted eCommerce sales grew 19%, while Kroger Precision Marketing profit increased by more than 20%, reflecting expanding online engagement and retail media monetization during the quarter.
         
     
  
     
         What is Kroger's share repurchase plan through fiscal 2026 for KR stock? 
         
          Kroger plans to continue returning capital through share repurchases in fiscal 2026. According to Kroger, the board approved an additional $2 billion repurchase authorization in December 2025, and the company expects to complete these buybacks by the end of fiscal 2026.
         
     
  
     
         What is Kroger's current net total debt to adjusted EBITDA ratio? 
         
          Kroger reported a modestly higher leverage ratio year over year. According to Kroger, net total debt to adjusted EBITDA was 1.75 at the end of the quarter, compared with 1.69 a year earlier, versus a stated target range of 2.30 to 2.50.
         
     
  
     
         How did Kroger's gross margin and expenses trend in Q1 2026? 
         
          Kroger experienced margin pressure and higher expense rates in Q1 2026. According to Kroger, gross margin declined to 22.7% from 23.0%, FIFO gross margin rate fell 9 basis points, and the OG&A rate excluding fuel increased 16 basis points versus the prior year.
