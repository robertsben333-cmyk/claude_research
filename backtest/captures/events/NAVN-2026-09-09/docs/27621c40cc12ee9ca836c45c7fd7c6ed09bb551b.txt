 The aftermarket delivered the era’s standard lesson: shares fell 20 percent on day one, closing at $20, and were down nearly 50 percent by mid December amid profitability concerns, before recovering through 2026 on solid earnings reports, with analysts at BTIG and BNP Paribas raising targets to around $30. 
 The deal structure: 30 million primary shares from the company plus roughly 6.9 million from selling stockholders, a 30 day greenshoe, and a dual class structure concentrating founder control. 
 For this series, NAVN is the completed case study: a 2015 vintage company that once carried a $9.2 billion era private mark, listed below it, absorbed the drawdown, and now trades on results, the honest template for every candidate file in this cluster.

 The deal, as it actually happened 
 Navan, founded in 2015 (originally TripActions) and built into an  all in one business travel, payments and expense platform , filed its amended S-1 on  October 10, 2025 , marketed at  $24 to $26 , and  priced at $25 on October 29 . The offering:  36,924,406 Class A shares , of which  30 million were primary  (proceeds to the company) and about  6.9 million came from selling stockholders , plus a 30 day underwriter option on roughly 5.5 million more, raising about  $923 million at a valuation near $6.2 billion . Trading opened  October 30 on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under NAVN , with a dual class structure keeping voting control concentrated with founders, the governance note the S-1 commentary flagged in advance. 
 Context made the pricing itself a lesson: Navan’s private era marks had reached the  $9.2 billion range in 2022 , so the deal cleared meaningfully  below the last cycle’s price , the pattern this series keeps documenting from a distance and NAVN executed in public. 
       
 The aftermarket education 
       
 Day one delivered the era’s signature move:  NAVN fell 20 percent, closing at $20  against a $25 print, as CNBC reported profitability concerns dominating the reception. By  mid December the stock was down nearly 50 percent  from the IPO price, tracking the same post debut arc that hit Klarna, Figma and much of the 2025 class. Then the mechanism this series always points at took over:  earnings . Through 2026, reported results came in solid, enterprise logos kept signing, Cummins and Enbridge among the announced partnerships, and analysts responded,  BTIG raising its target to $30 and BNP Paribas to $30 with an outperform rating . The stock’s authority migrated from roadshow narrative to quarterly prints, exactly on schedule. 
 For candidates and their investors, the sequence carries three durable lessons.  The 2021 to 2022 private mark rarely survives the bookbuild.   Day one and the lockup window are supply events, not verdicts.  And  the recovery, when it comes, runs through disclosed results , nothing else. 
 What NAVN is now, for traders 
       
 Today NAVN is simply a tradable mid cap with a live tape: earnings dates, analyst coverage, borrow availability, index eligibility questions and a post lockup float. It also serves as the  anchor comp for the entire private travel and spend management complex , every private candidate in the category now prices against a public multiple that updates daily.
