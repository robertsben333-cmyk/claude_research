reported record Q2 fiscal 2026 revenue of $22.2 billion, up 48% year on year, with AI semiconductor revenue reaching a record $10.8 billion, up 143% year on year, and Q2 operating margin hitting a record 67%.    For Q3 fiscal 2026, Broadcom guided consolidated revenue of $29.4 billion, up 84% year on year, with AI semiconductor revenue expected to reach $16 billion, up over 200% year on year, and infrastructure software revenue of approximately $8.9 billion, up 31% year on year.    For full year fiscal 2026, Broadcom expects AI semiconductor revenue of $56 billion, up approximately 180% from fiscal 2025, and reaffirmed that fiscal 2027 AI semiconductor revenue is expected to exceed $100 billion, with further growth anticipated in fiscal 2028.    Broadcom announced major multi-year customer agreements, including     a long-term TPU deal with Google,     a five gigawatt next-generation compute agreement with Anthropic beginning 2027,     a 1.3 gigawatt commitment with OpenAI in 2027 as part of a 10 gigawatt deal through 2029, and    a three gigawatt partnership with Meta through end of 2028, with Q2 AI semiconductor bookings exceeding $30 billion.      Broadcom announced the creation of the AI SPV platform with Apollo and Blackstone to deploy over 20 gigawatts of compute capacity through 2028, with the first tranche valued at $35 billion being launched by Apollo, while Q2 free cash flow reached a record $10.3 billion representing 46% of revenue, and CFO Kirsten Spears announced her retirement effective June 12th with Amy Tyner named as incoming CFO.
