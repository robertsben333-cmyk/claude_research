This quarter, the market is expecting NetApp’s revenue to grow 8% year on year, improving from the 3.8% increase it recorded in the same quarter last year.    The majority of analysts covering the company have reconfirmed their estimates over the last 30 days, suggesting they anticipate the business to stay the course heading into earnings. NetApp rarely misses Wall Street’s revenue estimates.   Looking at NetApp’s peers in the hardware &amp; infrastructure segment, some have already reported their Q1 results, giving us a hint as to what we can expect. IonQ delivered year-on-year revenue growth of 755%, beating analysts’ expectations by 30%, and Diebold Nixdorf reported revenues up 5.6%, topping estimates by 3.5%.
