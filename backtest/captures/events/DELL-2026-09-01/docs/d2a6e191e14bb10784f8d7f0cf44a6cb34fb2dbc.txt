  The solvency profile demonstrates a significant evolution in leverage and capital structure between 2021 and 2026, characterized by an initial period of aggressive deleveraging followed by a long-term phase of relative stability. 
 
 Debt to Equity and Financial Leverage 
 A precipitous decline is observed in both metrics during the first three quarters of the analyzed period. Debt to equity decreased from 13.39 in April 2021 to 5.36 by October 2021. Concurrently, financial leverage dropped from 34.92 to 15.15. These trends indicate a rapid reduction in the reliance on debt relative to equity during 2021. 
 Debt to Capital 
 The debt to capital ratio experienced an upward shift in early 2022, rising from 0.84 in October 2021 to 1.07 in January 2022. Following this adjustment, the ratio remained consistently elevated, fluctuating within a tight corridor between 1.05 and 1.15 for the remainder of the period. A marginal decline is noted in the final quarters, ending at 1.05 by May 2026. 
 Debt to Assets 
 A general downward trend is observable in the proportion of assets financed by debt. From a peak of 0.38 in April 2021, the ratio fluctuated but trended lower, reaching 0.27 by May 2026. This suggests a gradual improvement in the overall asset coverage of the company's debt obligations over the five-year span. 
 
 Overall, the data indicates that after a period of volatility and restructuring in 2021 and early 2022, the solvency ratios transitioned into a stable equilibrium. The reduction in the debt to assets ratio suggests a strengthened balance sheet position by the conclusion of the observed period.
