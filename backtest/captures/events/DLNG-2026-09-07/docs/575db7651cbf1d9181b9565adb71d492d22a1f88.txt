 The 2007-built LNG carrier Clean Energy has been employed for a time charter period of about two years, commencing between March-May 2026, following the expiration of the existing time charter to SEFE Marketing &amp; Trading, and the 2013-built LNG carrier Arctic Aurora has been employed for a time charter period of about seven years, commencing between September–November 2026, following the expiration of the existing time charter to Equinor ASA.
