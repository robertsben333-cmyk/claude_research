For its previous quarter, the company reported a revenue increase of 31% to $3 billion, while Next-Generation Security ARR reached $8.1 billion, up 60%. According to Nikesh Arora, chairman and chief executive officer of Palo Alto Networks, Q3 was a standout quarter for PANW marked by accelerating organic bookings growth as customers used PANW to secure their AI deployments at scale.              The company's platformization strategy is also gaining traction, with 110 net new platformizations this quarter. Its AI-adjacent solutions are also witnessing strong adoption, with the customer count for its automated security operations solution, XSIAM, reaching 740 in the previous quarter.               Premium Valuation               PANW's stock appreciation has pushed its valuation multiples well above historical norms. Investors anticipate rapid growth for the cybersecurity company and AI security demand; something more than a routine earnings beat can deliver.               This is also the tension pointed out by Benchmark: PANW must prove that the AI enthusiasm surrounding PANW is converting into measurable ARR growth, platformization deals are expanding, and that management also delivers an upbeat framework for fiscal 2027.
