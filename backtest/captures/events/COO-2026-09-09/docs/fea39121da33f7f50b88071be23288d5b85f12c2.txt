  DATE  Tuesday, Aug. 11, 2026 at 8 a.m. ET  CALL PARTICIPANTS   Vice President and Global Head, Investor Relations - Daniel Cravens  Chief Executive Officer - David Endicott  Chief Financial Officer - Timothy Stonesifer                         TAKEAWAYS    Net Sales  -- Net sales for  Alcon Inc.   ( ALC   -0.20% )  reached $2.8 billion, growing 7% on a constant currency basis.   Surgical Revenue  -- Surgical franchise sales reached $1.6 billion, growing 7% on a constant currency basis due to strong performance in equipment and consumables.   Vision Care Revenue  -- Vision Care sales grew 7% to $1.2 billion, supported by the ocular health and contact lens portfolios.   Core Gross Margin  -- Core gross margin expanded 250 basis points to 64.7%, reflecting price increases and manufacturing efficiencies.   Core Operating Margin  -- Core operating margin rose 160 basis points on a constant currency basis to 20.6% of sales.   Core Diluted EPS  -- Core diluted earnings per share were $0.84, a 9% increase compared to the prior year.   Equipment Sales  -- Equipment and other sales rose 25% to $279 million, led by the UNITY platform launch.   Consumables Revenue  -- Consumables revenue grew 5% to $825 million, reflecting vitreoretinal market trends and healthy international cataract volumes.   Implantables Revenue  -- Implantables revenue increased 1% to $466 million, with intraocular lens growth partially offset by surgical glaucoma declines.   Contact Lens Sales  -- Contact lens sales rose 5% to $726 million, driven by market share gains and momentum in the TOTAL1 and PRECISION1 daily disposable lines.   Ocular Health Revenue  -- Ocular health revenue grew 12% to $486 million, fueled by TRYPTYR and Systane performance.   PowerVision Charge  -- Management recorded a $402 million pretax noncash charge related to the discontinuation of the PowerVision program following clinical study results.   EPS Guidance  -- Full-year core diluted EPS growth guidance was raised to a range of 12% to 15% in constant currency.   Sales Guidance  -- Full-year constant currency sales growth guidance was maintained at 5% to 7%.   Margin Guidance  -- Operating margin expansion guidance was increased to a range of 90 to 190 basis points for the full year.   Free Cash Flow  -- Free cash flow for the first half of 2026 totaled $693 million.   Shareholder Returns  -- The company returned $538 million to shareholders through dividends and $295 million in share repurchases during the first half of the year.   AT-IOL Penetration  -- Advanced technology intraocular lens penetration increased 110 basis points globally and 180 basis points in the U.S.   TRYPTYR Market Share  -- TRYPTYR captured approximately 5% market share within the first year of its launch.   Tariff Refund  -- The company expects a $60 million tariff refund in the third quarter, with two-thirds of the benefit planned for reinvestment into commercial initiatives.   Systane MDPF Growth  -- The Systane multidose preservative-free portfolio grew more than 40% during the quarter.   Cataract Market Volume  -- Global cataract procedure volumes grew low single digits, while U.S. volumes were flat during the second quarter.   Contact Lens Market Growth  -- The global contact lens market grew mid-single digits, led by 8% growth in the United States and 3% in international markets. SUMMARY  Alcon prioritized the commercial scale-up of its new surgical platforms and prescription ocular health products during the quarter. Management reported that the discontinuation of a major intraocular lens development program followed clinical data showing unpredictable patient outcomes, leading to a focus on other pipeline initiatives. The company&#x27;s financial performance was supported by equipment adoption and price increases across the Vision Care segment. Strategic capital use remains focused on shareholder returns through dividends and share repurchases while reinvesting operational savings into marketing for recent product launches.   CEO Endicott stated, &quot;Nearly all PanOptix accounts have been converted to PanOptix Pro,&quot; with the platform now representing approximately 90% of PanOptix implants.  Management highlighted the first sale of the UNITY M microscope, signaling the start of broader commercialization for the digitized operating room ecosystem.  The company announced a collaboration with RxSight to develop next-generation lenses that combine Alcon optics with RxSight&#x27;s adjustability platform.  CEO Endicott noted that market access for the TRYPTYR prescription dry eye treatment now includes &quot;nearly two-thirds of commercial lives and more than 20% of Medicare lives.&quot;  Management indicated that Valeda, a treatment for dry AMD, has the potential to generate annual sales between $100 million to $150 million over time.  CFO Stonesifer confirmed the company is on track for a $100 million run rate in savings from its cost efficiency program, with the majority of impact expected in the second half of the year.  Management highlighted the introduction of the UNITY DX diagnostic platform, featuring hyper parallel OCT technology, with a pilot launch expected later this year.                           INDUSTRY GLOSSARY    Phaco : Phacoemulsification, a surgical procedure for cataract removal using ultrasonic vibration.   IOL : Intraocular Lens, an artificial lens implanted in the eye to replace the natural lens.   AT-IOL : Advanced Technology Intraocular Lens, a premium lens category offering multifocal or astigmatism correction.   MDPF : Multidose preservative-free, a delivery system for ocular medications that avoids preservatives.   SLT : Selective Laser Trabeculoplasty, a laser treatment used to lower eye pressure in glaucoma patients.   OCT : Optical Coherence Tomography, a non-invasive imaging test that uses light waves to take cross-section pictures of the retina.   TRx : Total prescriptions reported within a specific period. This data demonstrated persistent unpredictable shifts in postoperative distance vision in a subset of patients that remain unresolved after multiple developmental efforts. As a result, the programs did not meet our standards for visual performance and patient outcomes.  Although we are disappointed that the programs ultimately did not advance, they generated valuable insights into accommodation, tunability and long-term visual outcomes that will inform future innovation efforts. Looking at our performance in the quarter, implantables grew 1% with IOLs up 2% despite new competitive launches. The PanOptix family grew double digits in the quarter, driven by strong adoption of PanOptix Pro. Building on the foundation of PanOptix, the world&#x27;s most implantable trifocal IOL, PanOptix Pro enhances quality of vision through its advanced optical design and continues to gain traction with surgeons globally. In the U.S., adoption has exceeded expectations. Nearly all PanOptix accounts have been converted to PanOptix Pro with the platform now representing approximately 90% of PanOptix implants.  Feedback on visual performance and reduced light scatter remains very encouraging. We expect this momentum to extend internationally as we roll out PanOptix Pro and continue to build on the strength of Clareon Toric. Early launches in Japan, Canada, Australia and more recently in Europe have been well received, supporting our confidence in share stabilization and long-term growth. And we&#x27;re also excited about the acceleration of our pipeline of new IOLs. We&#x27;ve begun a KOL launch of TruPlus in the U.S. and recently received CE Mark for Europe. This lens is an important addition to our portfolio and provides an entry point into the monofocal plus segment.  We intend to phase these launches deliberately as we continue to prioritize the scale-up of PanOptix Pro in international markets and prepare for the introduction of Vivity Pro. Expected to launch with KOLs late this year, Vivity Pro builds on the success of the Vivity platform through a next-generation lens that is designed to extend the range of vision and enhances near performance. The new lens is designed to deliver up to one additional line of near vision while maintaining Vivity&#x27;s strong distance and intermediate vision performance and its clinically proven low visual disturbance profile. Importantly, TruPlus and Vivity Pro represent only the next wave of innovation from our IOL portfolio.  Our pipeline remains robust, and we expect to continue to deliver a steady cadence of new technologies and product enhancements in the years ahead. Beyond cataract surgery, we continue to see enthusiasm for Valeda, our first-of-its-kind treatment for dry AMD. This technology uses 3 specific wavelengths of light to improve mitochondrial activity in retinal health. Importantly, clinical studies showed that more than 80% of patients maintained or improved their vision at approximately 2 years. Adoption accelerated during the quarter as we expanded the installed base and increased utilization across existing accounts. We were also encouraged by continued progress with the Medicare administrative contractors, which we believe will further support access to this therapy.  Based on current adoption trends, clinical experience and reimbursement progress, we continue to believe the platform has the potential to generate sales of between $100 million and $150 million over time. Against that backdrop, we achieved a record global market share position, supported by strong U.S. share gains and continued momentum across both dailies and reusables. TOTAL30 continues to perform well across the family, supported by the recent launch of TOTAL30 multifocal for astigmatism, which expands our reach into an attractive and underserved segment. In addition, PRECISION7 sales have more than doubled versus the prior year, reflecting strong adoption of the weekly replacement category and providing another meaningful avenue for growth. With multiple platforms across dailies and reusables, we believe we&#x27;re well positioned to continue capturing share and pursuing attractive growth opportunities across the contact lens market. As we look ahead, we see a robust pipeline of growth catalysts across both our Surgical and our Vision Care franchises. We&#x27;re also excited about the potential of our recently announced collaboration with RxSight. Together, we aim to develop a next-generation lens designed specifically for the platform with the potential to further enhance visual performance and refractive precision.
