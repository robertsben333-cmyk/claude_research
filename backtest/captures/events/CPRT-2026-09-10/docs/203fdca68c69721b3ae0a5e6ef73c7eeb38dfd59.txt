Email pr@fool.com    TAKEAWAYS    Consolidated Revenue  -- $1.24 billion, up 2.1% year over year, attributed to both service and purchased vehicle sales.   Average Selling Price (ASP)  -- Rose 4.6% globally, overtaking a 2.4% decline in unit volumes. Noninsurance Unit Volume  -- Fell 1.4% globally; U.S. Copart direct unit volume down 26.3% due to a strategic shift to the direct buy channel. Fleet and Finance Seller Volume  -- Increased by double digits, partially offset by reduced rental customer repair activity. Long-Haul Delivery Product  -- Generated about $15 million of year-over-year cost in facility operations with "nice margin" contribution and rapid buyer adoption.   SUMMARY  Management attributed growth in consolidated revenue and profit primarily to higher average selling prices that more than offset declining volumes, especially within insurance units. The international segment delivered outsized growth in units, revenues, and ASPs, with notable contributions from the U.K., Germany, and Canada. Strategic investments, particularly in technology, logistics, and the buyer network, were emphasized as key to supporting expanded service offerings and higher returns for sellers. The heightened mix of pure sale units reflects a shift in insurance carrier preferences, while purple wave expansion and share buybacks signal ongoing momentum and capital deployment.   Stearns noted, "global assignment volumes grew at a low single-digit pace," despite overall inventory declines.  Liaw emphasized the cyclical nature of reduced claims activity and insurance coverage, reinforced by macro data indicating a 4% YOY decline in earned car years as vehicles in operation rose 1.4%.   Copart  (NASDAQ:CPRT) ’s marketplace resilience is connected to buyer diversity, with over one-third of auction volume and nearly half of proceeds now coming from international buyers.  Investments in long-haul delivery and AI-enabled claims solutions are designed to increase both buyer engagement and insurance carrier efficiency.   INDUSTRY GLOSSARY    Pure Sale : An auction lot sold with no reserve price and no right of refusal or minimum price set by the seller; all bids are final.   Assignment Volumes : The count of vehicles allocated or assigned by sellers, typically insurers or fleet operators, to Copart for remarketing.   BluCar : Copart’s commercial consignment channel focused on dealers and fleets.   Truck In a Box : Proprietary Copart program to support third-party towing contractors through leasing and structured towing arrangements.   GTV (Gross Transaction Value) : Total sales value of all vehicles and equipment sold through a given platform—in this context, the Purple Wave unit.   Crossover Buyers : Buyers who enter Copart’s ecosystem via noninsurance vehicles and subsequently participate in insurance vehicle auctions.
