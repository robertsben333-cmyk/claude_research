  ABI Research’s latest  Competitive Ranking  offers a clear view of today’s most advanced fleet telematics companies. As software platforms evolve beyond traditional vehicle tracking to support broader fleet and operational intelligence, ABI Research evaluates each vendor across two critical dimensions: implementation and innovation. 
 
 The top four companies in our ranking are: 
 
  1. Geotab  
  2. Samsara  
  3. Powerfleet  
  4. Verizon Connect  
 
 Below is a breakdown of what distinguishes each telematics vendor, as evaluated by our analysts.

 &nbsp; 
 &nbsp; 
   1. Geotab   
     
 Geotab, once again, was the overall leader in fleet telematics solutions, securing the top spot in implementation and second in innovation. The company’s patented CurveLogic capability, its open Application Programmable Interfaces (APIs), and the data insights generated through MyGeotab contribute to its leadership position. 
 Additionally, the Geotab Marketplace offers more than 430 partner solutions, giving fleets unmatched flexibility when it comes to customizing their fleet telematics system. 
 Geotab’s platform  is certified in 96 countries , and the company reports more than 4.6 million active vehicle connections. It supports over 37 Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) integrations and provides fast onboarding through streamlined configuration and deployment options. 
 Key highlights include: 
 
 CurveLogic enables harmonized, high-resolution fleet data collection and analysis. 
 MyGeotab and public APIs support deep system integration and customization. 
 Geotab Ace is a Generative Artificial Intelligence (Gen AI) assistant enabling fleet managers to access data through natural language queries. 
 The Marketplace allows seamless plug-in of third-party software tools and services, such as Electric Vehicle (EV) support, fuel management, connected sensors, etc. 
 Procurement is available through both Capital Expenditure (CAPEX) and Operational Expenditure (OPEX) models, supporting different fleet investment strategies. 
 Customers often report Return on Investment (ROI) within just 3 months of deployment. 
 
 Geotab continues to lead by delivering scalable, data-rich solutions with global reach. Its platform flexibility and broad integration support position it well for fleets of all sizes seeking operational intelligence. 
 &nbsp; 
  
 &nbsp; 
   2. Samsara   
 &nbsp; 
     
 Samsara placed second overall in ABI Research’s commercial fleet telematics ranking, up from ninth place in  our last assessment . The San Francisco-based company’s Connected Operations Cloud continues to gain momentum in large-scale fleet deployments. 
 Notable use cases for the system include truck breakdown alerts, vehicle maintenance reminders, driver behavior monitoring,  risk management  and compliance, EV insights, and more. 
 With over  US$1.35 billion in Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)  and more than 2,300 enterprise customers generating at least US$100,000 ARR each, Samsara is a key player in digitally transforming fleet operations. The company offers modular hardware and has cultivated more than 300 ecosystem integrations to streamline data sharing across applications. 
 Samsara’s standout features include: 
 
 AI-based video telematics and real-time vehicle diagnostics (learn more in this  ABI Insight ). 
 An intuitive, low-code interface for building workflows and dashboards. 
 Easy access to data across teams, enabling faster decision-making. 
 Open APIs to support third-party integrations. 
 
 The Connected Operations Cloud is specifically designed to scale across large, complex organizations. That way, logistics managers can optimize costs and improve safety outcomes through a highly integrated fleet telematics system. 
 &nbsp; 
   3. Powerfleet   
 &nbsp; 
     
 Powerfleet was recognized by ABI Research as  one of the most innovative technology vendors in 2025 . The New Jersey-based firm&nbsp;rose in the rankings following its merger with MiX Telematics and the acquisition of Fleet Complete. It now ranks first in innovation and fourth in implementation. 
 The company’s Unity platform, combined with its Gen AI assistant Aura, allows fleet managers to customize Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and real-time dashboards. This positions Powerfleet as a strong choice for organizations with mixed asset environments. 
 Notable strengths helping Powerfleet rank highly include: 
 
 A customer base of over  48,000 organizations  across 120 countries. 
 Support for vehicle,  cold chain  trailer, warehouse, and lone worker solutions. 
 AI-powered tools for performance benchmarking and data harmonization. 
 No-code capabilities allow users to self-guide and customize functionalities. 
 EV transition tool helps support enterprises’  fleet electrification  plans. 
 
 Finally, Powerfleet continues to push into key verticals like logistics, retail, and manufacturing with a strong focus on operational efficiency and usability. 
  
 &nbsp; 
   4. Verizon Connect   
 &nbsp; 
     
 Verizon Connect is the fourth-best fleet telematics provider, according to the competitive assessment. Its strength lies in being part of Verizon Business, leveraging the company’s extensive network infrastructure and ecosystem capabilities. 
 Verizon Connect’s AI-driven dashcams now include a new event classification system that categorizes video footage by severity. These updates are part of its ongoing effort to improve safety and compliance outcomes for enterprise customers. 
 Key platform capabilities include: 
 
 Fleet and asset tracking with high system uptime (99.9%) 
 Integrated fleet safety features with configurable driver workflows 
 Large OEM partnership network with Ford, General Motors, and Navistar 
 EV Suitability Tool to accelerate fleet electrification strategies 
 Robust AI technologies to improve safety insights (e.g., seatbelt usage, speeding, tailgating, etc.), vehicle performance, and sustainability 
 A platform Marketplace with more than 50 integrations, including with major OEMs 
 
 Although Verizon Connect lacks some of the openness seen in other leading telematics platforms, it remains a preferred choice for customers that value end-to-end reliability, extensive support, and telco-grade scalability. Customers of Verizon Connect range from traditional supply chains and&nbsp; &nbsp; construction firms  to small businesses and  local governments . 
 
 &nbsp; 
  Conclusion  
 This year’s Competitive Ranking reflects the shift in telematics from siloed solutions to broad, integrated platforms that prioritize openness, scalability, and intelligence. 
 
  Geotab  stands out for its advanced analytics, open architecture, and ecosystem depth. 
  Samsara  delivers strong innovation with modular, scalable solutions for large enterprise fleets. 
  Powerfleet  offers cross-asset intelligence and user-driven analytics capabilities. 
  Verizon Connect  remains a consistent and trusted provider for organizations needing scale and stability. 
 
 Each of these leading telematics vendors is helping shape the future of fleet management, offering the tools that companies need to improve safety, customize dashboards, enhance efficiency, and achieve End-to-End (E2E) visibility. 
 To learn how ABI Research compiled these rankings, read  Selecting the Best Fleet Telematics Vendor for Your Supply Chain | ABI Research Competitive Assessment .
