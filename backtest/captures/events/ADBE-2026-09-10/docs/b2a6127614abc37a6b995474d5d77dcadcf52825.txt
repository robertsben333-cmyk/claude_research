 Adobe Firefly Content Generation Growth 

 Adobe Firefly added 6 billion generations between March and October 2024 alone, matching its entire output from the preceding twelve months. The platform surpassed 13 billion images by October 2024 during Adobe MAX, reaching 16 billion content pieces by November 2024.
