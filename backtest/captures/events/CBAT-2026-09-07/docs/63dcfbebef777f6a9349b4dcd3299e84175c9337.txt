Key Elements      EVE Energy    CATL    BMW    SNE Research    China&#x27;s Ministry of Finance    China Automotive Power Battery Industry Innovation Alliance    Gaogong Industry Research    EVTank                                                                                           On the evening of August 19, EVE Energy (300014.SZ) released its 2026 semi-annual report. During the reporting period, the company achieved revenue of 45.69 billion yuan (approximately $6.8 billion), up 62.20% year-on-year, and net profit attributable to shareholders of listed companies of 3.3 billion yuan (approximately $491.5 million), up 105.66% year-on-year. This marks the first time EVE Energy's single half-year net profit has exceeded the 3 billion yuan threshold since its listing, with both revenue and net profit landing at the upper end of its previously issued earnings guidance range. 
 However, while book profits swelled substantially, the company's net cash flow from operating activities was negative 388 million yuan (approximately $57.8 million), a decline of 116.35% from 2.37 billion yuan in the same period last year. The coexistence of aggressive scale expansion and tightening cash flow has become the most striking contradiction in this semi-annual report. 
 Three core business segments advance in tandem 
 By segment, EVE Energy has established a "three-pillar" business structure spanning consumer batteries, power batteries, and energy storage batteries. 
 The consumer battery segment posted first-half revenue of approximately 6.41 billion yuan (approximately $954.6 million), up 26.22% year-on-year. The segment's gross margin was 22.64%, down 4.04 percentage points year-on-year but still the highest among the company's four business lines. In the consumer battery space, EVE Energy has achieved full coverage across all technology routes, with its primary lithium battery products ranking first in China in both sales and exports for 10 consecutive years, and first globally for five consecutive years. The company has also rapidly established a presence in emerging fields including robotics, the low-altitude economy, medical batteries, detachable lithium batteries, and BBU (backup battery units). 
 The power battery segment generated revenue of approximately 17.28 billion yuan (approximately $2.6 billion), up 53.31% year-on-year, with power battery shipments of 35.76 GWh during the period, up 66.47% year-on-year. EVE Energy leveraged its first-to-mass-production 46-series large cylindrical cells to enter the premium passenger vehicle market, becoming the first battery supplier for BMW's Neue Klasse models. However, in the passenger vehicle segment, EVE Energy remains outside the third tier. Data from the China Automotive Power Battery Industry Innovation Alliance shows that in the first six months of this year, EVE Energy's new energy passenger vehicle installation volume ranking was not particularly strong. What truly underpins its position among China's top five power battery makers is the commercial vehicle market — since 2024, the company's new energy commercial vehicle installation volume has consistently ranked second nationally. In the first half of this year, its commercial vehicle installation volume was 11.34 GWh, with a market share of 13.16%, second only to CATL (300750.SZ). According to SNE Research, EVE Energy ranked second globally in commercial vehicle power battery installation volume in the first half of 2026, up 65.3% year-on-year. 
 The energy storage battery segment posted revenue exceeding 15.09 billion yuan (approximately $2.2 billion), with a year-on-year growth rate of 69.15% — the fastest-growing among the three core segments. Energy storage battery shipments reached 44.46 GWh, up 54.88% year-on-year. EVE Energy is the world's first company to mass-produce 600Ah+ large-format lithium iron phosphate energy storage batteries. During the reporting period, production capacity for its 628Ah Mr.Big ultra-large-capacity cells was gradually released, while the next-generation 702Ah stacked cells and the accompanying Mr.Giant energy storage system have completed R&#x26;D and entered the customer sampling phase. According to SNE Research, the company ranked second globally in energy storage battery shipments in the first half of 2026. 
 In addition, the battery materials segment recorded revenue of 6.87 billion yuan (approximately $1.0 billion), up 140.73% year-on-year, reflecting the accelerating realization of the company's upstream integration strategy.

 Structural factors behind the profit surge 
 The fact that net profit growth far outpaced revenue growth stems from multiple factors working in concert. The semi-annual report explains that the high net profit growth came partly from operating leverage generated by revenue expansion, and partly from a substantial increase in investment income — investment income during the reporting period was 1.18 billion yuan (approximately $176.1 million), up 263.55% year-on-year, which the company attributed to the completion of equity swaps and improved profitability at associated companies. 
 Meanwhile, financial expenses rose 49.65% year-on-year, primarily due to increased foreign exchange gains and losses; income tax expenses rose 171.16% year-on-year, a result of higher profit accruals. Operating costs rose 68.12% year-on-year, outpacing the 62.20% revenue growth, which the company attributed to the combined effect of business growth and rising material prices. 
 As for the swing from positive to negative cash flow, the semi-annual report cited "strategic inventory buildup and payment for goods" as the explanation. Looking at the balance sheet, inventory at the end of the reporting period reached 15.42 billion yuan (approximately $2.3 billion), with its share of total assets rising from 6.56% at the end of last year to 11.14%; accounts receivable also increased from 14.86 billion yuan at the end of last year to 19.71 billion yuan (approximately $2.9 billion). Against the backdrop of raw material prices entering an upward cycle, stockpiling in advance to lock in costs is a proactive choice, but it also means more capital is tied up in inventory and receivables. 
 Global expansion and frontier technology reserves 
 During the reporting period, EVE Energy's overseas sales revenue was 11.33 billion yuan (approximately $1.7 billion), up 62.61% year-on-year, accounting for roughly one-quarter of total revenue; domestic revenue was 34.36 billion yuan (approximately $5.1 billion), up 62.07% year-on-year. The near-identical growth rates in domestic and overseas markets suggest that overseas expansion has not come at the expense of the domestic market. 
 On the frontier technology front, the company has made substantive progress on both solid-state battery and sodium-ion battery tracks: in March 2026, the "Longquan No. 4" all-solid-state battery, focused on power applications with a capacity of 60Ah, successfully rolled off the production line; the self-developed NF155L sodium-ion battery has achieved mass-production capability and is expected to begin batch delivery for energy storage and light power applications by the end of 2026. R&#x26;D expenses during the reporting period reached 1.7 billion yuan (approximately $253.4 million), up 34.96% year-on-year, with more than 6,000 R&#x26;D personnel and over 17,000 authorized and pending patents both domestically and internationally. 
 Industry boom and policy shifts 
 In the first half of this year, the lithium battery industry as a whole maintained strong momentum. According to Gaogong Industry Research, China's lithium battery shipments in the first half reached approximately 1.2 TWh, up more than 50% year-on-year, breaking the TWh threshold in a half-year period for the first time. Industry leader CATL also disclosed an impressive semi-annual report, with first-half net profit reaching 43.28 billion yuan (approximately $6.4 billion), up 41.98% year-on-year. 
 However, tax tightening is arriving simultaneously. Recently, China's Ministry of Finance and two other departments jointly issued the "Announcement on Adjusting Certain Battery Consumption Tax Policies," which will impose a 2% consumption tax on lithium batteries starting September 1, 2026, and a 4% rate starting September 1, 2027. 
 Regarding the potential impact of this policy, EVE Energy's board secretary stated in response to investor inquiries that overall, the policy's impact on the company is manageable. The company will share the new tax burden through upstream and downstream transmission while continuing to optimize efficiency and improve internal management precision; additionally, the company's forward-looking investments in sodium-ion and solid-state batteries will enjoy tax exemptions over the medium to long term. The board secretary noted that in the long run, this policy may accelerate the elimination of backward production capacity in the industry, which would be favorable for industry consolidation and market share gains by leading players. 
 For EVE Energy, the next phase after its scale leap will test whether it can reliably convert its accumulated inventory, production capacity, and overseas orders into sustainable cash flow and profits.
