Total quarterly revenues increased  21%  to   $19.2 billion  , reflecting broad-based demand for Oracle's industry-leading cloud technology and applications suites. Cloud revenues (IaaS + SaaS) increased  47%  to   $9.9 billion   driven by  93%  growth in Cloud Infrastructure (IaaS), and  10%  growth in Cloud Applications (SaaS). Software revenues were down  2%  to   $6.8 billion  , reflecting our customers' continuing migration from on-premise software to the Cloud. Services revenues were   $1.5 billion  , up  13% , and Hardware revenues were   $0.9 billion  , up  9% .

GAAP net income available to common shareholders reached   $4.2 billion  , up  23% , and non-GAAP net income available to common shareholders grew to   $6.2 billion  , up  26% . Q4 GAAP earnings per share increased to   $1.45  , up  21% , and non-GAAP earnings per share climbed to   $2.11   1 , up  24% .    Financial Results for FY 2026  Fiscal year 2026 total revenues were up  17%  to a record   $67.4 billion  . Cloud revenues increased  39%  to   $34.0 billion  . Software revenues were down  1%  to   $24.5 billion  . Services revenues were   $5.7 billion  , up  10% , and Hardware revenues were   $3.1 billion  , up  5% .  Fiscal year 2026 GAAP operating income was   $20.6 billion  , up  17% , and non-GAAP operating income rose to a record   $28.9 billion  , up  16% . GAAP net income available to common shareholders reached   $17.0 billion  , up  36% , while non-GAAP net income available to common shareholders grew to   $22.2 billion  , up  29% . Oracle's strong operating income translated to record fiscal year operating cash flow of   $32.0 billion  , up  54% . Free cash flow was negative   $23.7 billion   for fiscal year 2026 as Oracle continued to execute on investments to support the growth of its Cloud Infrastructure business.    Remaining Performance Obligations  Remaining Performance Obligations, or RPO, ended the quarter at   $638 billion  , up  363%  USD year-over-year and up   $85 billion   sequentially from the end of Q3.   Most of the RPO increase in both Q3 and Q4 were large scale AI contracts where the customer prepaid Oracle for the purchase of the GPUs, or the customer bought and supplied the GPUs to Oracle. The prepaid and customer supplied hardware portions of our large AI contracts now total   $75 billion  . In fiscal year 2027, Oracle expects to raise approximately   $40 billion   through a combination of debt and equity financing including its previously announced   $20 billion   at-the-market equity issuance. Oracle does not expect to issue additional debt in calendar year 2026. Oracle is building datacenters that are intended to use clean energy from natural gas fuel cells to generate electricity with minimal emissions. Other innovations in the areas of high-performance networking, advanced security and autonomous software have made Oracle the world's fastest growing provider of cloud datacenters.  Our database and applications businesses are both benefiting from Oracle's early adoption of AI. The Oracle Multicloud AI Database grew  404%  in Q4—making it our fastest growing business ever. The Oracle Health application suite will soon include a completely new AI version of the Cerner hospital and clinic patient care management system.      FAQ   
   
  
  
     
         How did Oracle (ORCL) perform in Q4 2026? 
         
          Oracle reported record Q4 2026 revenue of $19.2 billion, up 21% year-over-year. According to Oracle, cloud revenue reached $9.9 billion, up 47%, while GAAP EPS rose 21% to $1.45 and non-GAAP EPS increased 24% to $2.11.
         
     
  
     
         What were Oracle (ORCL) full-year 2026 earnings and revenue results? 
         
          Oracle’s FY 2026 revenue was $67.4 billion, up 17% year-over-year. According to Oracle, GAAP EPS grew 34% to $5.83, non-GAAP EPS 27% to $7.63, and cloud revenue rose 39% to $34.0 billion, reflecting strong demand for infrastructure and applications.
         
     
  
     
         How fast is Oracle (ORCL) cloud business growing in FY 2026? 
         
          Oracle’s total cloud revenue grew 39% in FY 2026 to $34.0 billion. According to Oracle, Cloud Infrastructure (IaaS) revenue increased 77% to $18.1 billion and Cloud Applications (SaaS) rose 11% to $15.9 billion, highlighting accelerating demand for AI-capable cloud services.
         
     
  
     
         What does Oracle (ORCL) Remaining Performance Obligations of $638 billion mean? 
         
          Oracle ended Q4 2026 with Remaining Performance Obligations of $638 billion, up 363% year-over-year. According to Oracle, about $75 billion reflects prepaid or customer-supplied AI hardware, which reduces required capital for building AI datacenters and supports long-term cloud revenue visibility.
         
     
  
     
         What guidance did Oracle (ORCL) give for FY 2027 revenue and EPS? 
         
          Oracle reaffirmed FY 2027 total revenue guidance of $90 billion and raised non-GAAP EPS guidance to $8.05. According to Oracle, this EPS outlook implies 18% growth after adjusting for one-time FY 2026 investment gains from specific transactions.
         
     
  
     
         How much capital is Oracle (ORCL) raising to fund its AI cloud expansion? 
         
          In FY 2026, Oracle raised $43 billion in debt and $5 billion in equity financing. According to Oracle, it expects to raise about $40 billion more in FY 2027 via debt and equity, including a previously announced $20 billion at-the-market equity issuance.
         
     
  
     
         What dividend did Oracle (ORCL) declare with its FY 2026 results? 
         
          Oracle’s board declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.50 per common share. According to Oracle, the dividend will be paid on July 24, 2026, to shareholders of record as of the close of business on July 10, 2026.
