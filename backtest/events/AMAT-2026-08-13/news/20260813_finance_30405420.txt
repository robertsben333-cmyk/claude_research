Applied Materials (NASDAQ:AMAT) shares climbed 4% Wednesday as investors positioned for the semiconductor equipment maker's fiscal third-quarter report, with Wall Street looking for another period of strong growth. The company is scheduled to release results after the market closes.
Applied Materials is expected to post revenue of about $8.99 billion, an increase of roughly 23% from a year earlier. Analysts also project adjusted earnings of $3.36 per share, which would represent growth of about 36%.
Applied Materials previously set a fiscal third-quarter revenue target of $8.95 billion, with a $500 million range on either side, while its adjusted earnings outlook was $3.36 per share, plus or minus 20 cents. Investors may focus on demand from advanced logic and memory customers as well as spending tied to artificial intelligence infrastructure.
Applied Materials has experienced mixed trading reactions following its last four quarterly reports. Shares declined 14.9% after fiscal third-quarter 2025 results and 2.1% after the following quarter, before gaining 4.4% after fiscal first-quarter results and remaining nearly unchanged after fiscal second-quarter earnings. Options markets imply a potential 7.4% move following the latest report.
