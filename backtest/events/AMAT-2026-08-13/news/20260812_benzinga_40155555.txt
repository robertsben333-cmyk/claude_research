A wall of blowout artificial intelligence infrastructure earnings pushed semiconductor and optical networking stocks sharply higher on Wednesday, dragging the Nasdaq 100 higher.
A July inflation print that landed exactly on consensus did the rest, pulling Treasury yields lower across the curve and trimming the odds of a September Federal Reserve rate hike.
The July consumer price index rose 0.1% month-over-month and 3.4% year-over-year, both in line with forecasts and a second straight annual deceleration from June’s 3.5%. Core CPI rose 0.2% on the month and cooled to 2.5% annually, the softest in five months.
Energy inflation slowed to a four-month low of 14.7%, with gasoline prices down 2.9% on the month. Markets remain split on whether the Fed hikes 25 basis points in September, but the print removed the tail risk that forced the question.
Treasury yields eased after the inflation data. The 10-year yield fell to 4.67%, down 2 basis points, while the policy-sensitive 2-year dropped 3 basis points to 4.20% and the 30-year edged down 1 basis point to 5.24%.
The S&P 500 rose 0.2% to 7,744.94, holding just below its record close from last week. The Dow Jones Industrial Average was effectively flat at 53,806.41, up 0.1%.
The Nasdaq 100 outperformed, rising 0.7% to 29,717.90 on the back of chip strength. Within Magnificent Seven stocks, Nvidia Corp. (NASDAQ:NVDA) climbed 2.8%.
