Item 2.02    Results of Operations and Financial Conditions.

On November 6, 2025, AAON, Inc. (the "Company") announced its financial and operating results and backlog for the third quarter ended September 30, 2025. A copy of the Company's press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference. The Company plans to host a teleconference at 9:00 A.M. (Eastern Time) on November 6, 2025 to discuss these results. The accessible dial-in is 1-800-836-8184 for domestic callers.
