The issue now is whether Box's current share price of US$32.83 offers enough upside against the intrinsic value estimate, given that the DCF suggests roughly 40.3% undervaluation while the multiples point to a richer valuation.
The Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) approach used here projects what Box could generate in free cash flow and then discounts those cash flows back to today. Box produced about $334.7 million of free cash flow over the latest twelve months, and the model assumes that these cash flows continue to grow from this base over time rather than shrinking.
On that basis, the DCF model arrives at an estimated intrinsic value of about $55 per share, compared with the current share price of $32.83. That implies the stock is 40.3% below the cash flow based estimate. The recent focus on Box as an AI-ready content platform, highlighted by the Quintas Energy partnership, helps explain why long-term cash flow expectations are central to this valuation, even if the market is not fully reflecting them in the price yet.
Based on the company's cash flow profile and this DCF estimate, Box stock currently appears undervalued.
