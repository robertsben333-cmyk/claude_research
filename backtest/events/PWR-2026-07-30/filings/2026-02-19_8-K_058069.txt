Quanta’s Fourth Quarter and Full-Year 2025 Operational and Financial Commentary, dated February 19, 2026, prepared by the Company and relating to its results for the fiscal quarter and year ended December 31, 2025, as noted in the press release described in Item 2.02 above, will be posted to the Company’s website at www.quantaservices.com under the “Financial Info” area of the Investor Relations section.
