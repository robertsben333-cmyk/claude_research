Item 7.01.    Regulation FD Disclosure.

On September 16, 2025, Black Stone Minerals, L.P. (the "Partnership") posted to the Partnership's investor relations website at https://investor.blackstoneminerals.com an updated investor presentation that it intends to use in conferences and meetings with investors.
